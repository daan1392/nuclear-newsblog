The Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics (IPP) in Germany and Columbia University established a new joint research center called the Max Planck Columbia Center to advance fusion energy science through transatlantic collaboration.

Directed by IPP director Thomas Klinger and Columbia associate professor Carlos Paz-Soldan—who also heads the Columbia Fusion Research Center—the virtual center brings together European and North American research capabilities and expertise. The initiative aims to strengthen collaborative research on magnetic confinement fusion and related physics.

The center's establishment reflects growing recognition that fusion development benefits from sustained international partnerships. By formalizing the relationship between two major research institutions, the collaboration creates structured pathways for knowledge sharing, joint projects, and coordinated research efforts that can accelerate progress toward practical fusion energy.
