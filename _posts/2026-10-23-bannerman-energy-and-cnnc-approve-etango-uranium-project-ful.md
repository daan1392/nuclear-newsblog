The joint venture of Bannerman Energy and CNNC Overseas Limited approved a positive final investment decision for the Etango uranium project in Namibia's Erongo Region, 30 kilometers southeast of Swakopmund. The project can now transition to full-scale construction, with commissioning and production targeted for 2028. The Etango deposit has an estimated mineral resource of 207 million pounds of U₃O₈ (79,622 tonnes of uranium) at a 100 ppm cut-off grade. Bannerman holds a 55 percent interest in the joint venture while CNNC Overseas holds 45 percent. The project's advancement reflects growing uranium demand anticipated from nuclear capacity expansion globally and addresses supply security concerns for reactor fuel. Etango's development timeline positions it to contribute to the fuel supply chain as new reactor construction accelerates worldwide.
