Eight nuclear fuel technology projects were chosen to receive federal support under the Department of Energy's HORNIG (High-performance Optimised Recycled Nuclear Isotopes for Gen IV reactors) programme, which aims to deliver commercially deployable technologies for recycling spent fuel. The projects, which share up to $50 million in funding, focus on recovering transuranic elements from existing spent fuel inventories and converting them into fissile materials suitable for advanced reactors. HORNIG is part of the DOE's Advanced Research Projects Agency-Energy initiative and builds on prior work in fuel reprocessing. Successful demonstration of these recycling technologies could reduce the volume of high-level waste requiring geological disposal while extending the utility of existing fuel resources.
