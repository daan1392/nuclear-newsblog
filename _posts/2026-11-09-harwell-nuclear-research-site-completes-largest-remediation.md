Nuclear Restoration Services completed its largest land remediation project at its Oxford-based Harwell site, removing more than 97,000 tonnes of material from 4.2 hectares of the former Liquid Effluent Treatment Plant. The facility had operated for over 60 years treating liquid nuclear waste before closure.

The project demonstrates progress in decommissioning and site restoration efforts at legacy nuclear research facilities across the UK. Remediating contaminated land is a critical phase in returning former nuclear sites to beneficial use, and the scale of material handling at Harwell illustrates the complexity of restoring sites with lengthy operational histories.
