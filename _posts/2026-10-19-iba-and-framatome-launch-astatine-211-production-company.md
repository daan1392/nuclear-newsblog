Ion Beam Applications (IBA) and Framatome announced the formation of Ekalia, a jointly owned company dedicated to industrial-scale production of astatine-211, a medically important isotope used in targeted cancer radiotherapy. The partnership aims to deploy a network of At-211 production sites close to customers across Europe and the United States.

The first production facility is planned for Nantes, France, with a €30 million investment. Ekalia has submitted the permit application, with construction expected to begin in early 2027 and operations anticipated to commence in 2028. A second pilot facility is planned for the United States, though the location and timeline remain to be confirmed.

Astatine-211 enables alpha-particle radiotherapy, offering potential treatment advantages for patients with certain cancers by delivering targeted radiation directly to tumor cells. The development of dedicated production infrastructure responds to growing clinical demand for this isotope and represents industry confidence in the therapeutic potential of alpha-emitter radiopharmaceuticals. The initiative positions Europe and North America to reduce dependence on imports for this specialized nuclear medicine reagent.
