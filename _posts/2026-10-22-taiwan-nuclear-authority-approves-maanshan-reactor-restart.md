Taiwan's nuclear regulatory authority completed its technical review of the Maanshan Nuclear Power Station restart plan, paving the way for the facility's return to service in 2028 at the earliest, according to the government. The two-unit 900 MW pressurized-water reactor station has been offline since 2025. The approval follows Taiwan's 2025 legislative changes that extended reactor operating licenses from 40 to 60 years and enabled previously shutdown units to be relicensed. State operator Taipower submitted the Maanshan restart application in March 2026. The move reflects Taiwan's shifting energy strategy in the face of rising electricity demand and energy security concerns. The Maanshan restart would represent a significant reversal of Taiwan's recent nuclear phase-out direction and signal renewed confidence in nuclear power's role in the island's energy mix.
