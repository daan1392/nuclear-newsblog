The UK Nuclear Decommissioning Authority appointed Aureos, a UK supply-chain engineering firm, to deliver major decommissioning work at Dungeness A nuclear power station in Kent under a contract valued at up to £190 million. The project will facilitate removal of some of the site's largest remaining structures, advancing the safe decommissioning and site clean-up program. Dungeness A housed two advanced gas-cooled reactors and has been undergoing phased decommissioning following reactor shutdown. The contract award accelerates scheduled structural demolition and radioactive waste management activities. Leveraging UK supply-chain capabilities, the project reflects domestic expertise and industrial capacity in decommissioning operations across British nuclear facilities managed by the authority's remediation program.
