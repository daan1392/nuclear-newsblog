American Electric Power and X-energy signed a joint development agreement with tech giant Amazon to evaluate a 960 MW small modular reactor project at a former coal plant site in Sullivan County, Indiana. The proposed facility would deploy 12 of X-energy's Xe-100 reactors—each producing 80 MW—arranged in three groups of four.

Amazon's involvement goes beyond investor interest: the company committed to a long-term power purchase agreement designed to cover facility costs and operations for the duration of the contract, providing the financial certainty utilities need to advance such projects. The development partnership could create more than 1,000 construction jobs and over 100 permanent positions.

The agreement marks a significant convergence of trends reshaping nuclear deployment in the US. Tech giants increasingly seek reliable, low-carbon power to meet surging artificial intelligence workload demands, while utilities gain investment confidence through long-term offtake commitments. Sullivan County, Indiana recently designated itself an advanced nuclear zone, positioning itself to attract further nuclear development.
