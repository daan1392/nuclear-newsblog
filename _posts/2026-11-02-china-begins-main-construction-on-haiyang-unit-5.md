China National Nuclear Corporation subsidiary China Nuclear Industry 24 Construction Co Ltd poured first safety-related concrete on 31 October for the reactor building of unit 5 at Haiyang nuclear power plant in Shandong province. This milestone marks the official commencement of main construction for the fifth unit at the site and the entry into the third phase of the plant's expansion. Unit 5 is one of six large pressurized water reactors planned for Haiyang, part of State Power Investment Corporation's broader nuclear fleet expansion. The progression from earlier site preparation and infrastructure work to the main reactor building construction demonstrates the acceleration of China's nuclear build programme amid efforts to decarbonize electricity generation and meet rising energy demand.
