Urenco USA has produced its first cylinder of uranium hexafluoride enriched to levels between 5 and 10 percent uranium-235, known as LEU+, according to the company. The milestone marks what Urenco describes as the first instance of commercial LEU+ production in "the Western world."

Higher-assay uranium (HAU) fuels—enriched above the standard 5 percent U-235 used in most commercial reactors—can reduce the number of fuel assemblies needed in a reactor core, potentially lowering costs and enabling longer operating cycles. Russia and China have previously explored commercial use of such fuels.

Urenco's Capenhurst facility in the UK produced LEU+ samples enriched to 7 percent U-235 in a trial run in May 2026. The shift to commercial-scale production suggests growing interest from reactor operators and indicates that enrichment providers are preparing for potential demand as advanced reactor designs and uprating projects proceed.
