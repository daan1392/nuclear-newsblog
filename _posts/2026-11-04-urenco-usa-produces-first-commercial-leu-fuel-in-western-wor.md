Urenco USA's National Enrichment Facility in New Mexico has produced its first commercial cylinder of LEU+ (low-enriched uranium enriched to 5–10% uranium-235), marking the first Western production of this fuel type and expanding available options for power reactor operators. The facility received authorization from the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission in September 2025 to enrich uranium up to 10% U-235, and all gas centrifuge cascades are now licensed for LEU+ production.

LEU+ represents an intermediate enrichment level between conventional low-enriched uranium (5%) and highly enriched fuel, offering potential benefits for certain reactor designs and reducing the need for very high enrichment levels. Russia and China have previously explored LEU+ development, but this marks the first commercial production in the Western fuel supply chain.

"A tremendous amount of work has gone into preparing our plant and our staff to reach this achievement," said Urenco USA Managing Director Jody Blackshear, highlighting the operational readiness required to begin commercial production. The milestone expands the fuel options available to utilities and demonstrates Western capacity to produce intermediate-enrichment fuel domestically.
