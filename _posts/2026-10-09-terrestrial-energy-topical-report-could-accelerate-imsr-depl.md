Terrestrial Energy submitted a Thermal and Electric Facility Topical Report to U.S. regulators for its Integrated Molten Salt Reactor (IMSR) design. If approved, the report would allow portions of the IMSR power plant to be classified as conventional industrial facilities rather than nuclear projects, potentially enabling those components to be built and operated under accelerated schedules ahead of full nuclear licensing.

The topical report approach—where specific design features or systems are pre-approved by regulators—streamlines the licensing pathway by addressing safety and technical issues earlier in the process. Classification of balance-of-plant systems as industrial rather than nuclear could reduce regulatory oversight and timelines for non-nuclear components, while the reactor core and primary systems remain under full NRC authority.

This licensing innovation supports faster deployment of advanced reactor designs by separating conventional engineering from nuclear-specific safety systems, a strategy gaining traction in the industry.
