The Canadian Space Agency issued a request for information (RFI) to assess Canadian industry and academic capabilities in lunar microreactor technologies. The agency identifies fission power as complementary to solar and battery systems for supporting lunar exploration, mobility, and future crewed operations.

The RFI targets technologies at technology readiness level 2 or higher, encompassing either complete reactor systems or specific subsystems. Interest in space reactors reflects the growing recognition that sustained lunar and deep-space missions require reliable, compact power sources independent of solar conditions. Canada's participation in space nuclear assessment aligns with broader international efforts to enable extended extraterrestrial operations.
