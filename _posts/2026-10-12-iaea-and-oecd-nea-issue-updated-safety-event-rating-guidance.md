The new guide provides standards for assessing incident severity and implementing consistent, transparent communication across the international nuclear community.

The updated guidance reflects lessons learned over decades of operational experience and incorporates best practices in event classification and disclosure. It enables regulatory bodies, operators, and communication specialists to convey technical information in a manner that is both accurate and accessible to non-specialist audiences.
