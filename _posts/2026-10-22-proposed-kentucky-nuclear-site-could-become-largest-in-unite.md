Project developer The Nuclear Company announced Prosperity One, a proposed nuclear power facility along the Ohio River in eastern Kentucky, designed to accommodate up to six Westinghouse AP1000 reactors. If fully constructed, the site would generate approximately 6.6 GW of electricity and become both Kentucky's first nuclear power plant and the largest nuclear generation facility in the United States. The AP1000 is a 1,100 MW pressurized-water reactor featuring passive safety systems. The announcement reflects intensifying interest in large-scale reactor deployment among independent project developers and follows the NRC's recent streamlining of licensing pathways for advanced reactor designs. The proposal would require substantial infrastructure, supply chain, and regulatory approvals over the coming years.
