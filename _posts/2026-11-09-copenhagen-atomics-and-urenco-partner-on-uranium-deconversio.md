Copenhagen Atomics and Urenco signed a memorandum of understanding to develop a deconversion plant producing uranium tetrafluoride (UF4) at a Copenhagen Atomics facility. The MOU establishes a framework leveraging Urenco's expertise in uranium hexafluoride (UF6) production to create a UF6-to-UF4 conversion pathway.

Copenhagen Atomics is developing molten salt reactors, which require UF4 as feedstock. The collaboration addresses a supply chain gap for MSR developers by building on Urenco's existing enrichment infrastructure. Copenhagen Atomics has also purchased low-enriched uranium from Urenco as part of the broader partnership, combining fuel supply and conversion services within a single commercial relationship.
