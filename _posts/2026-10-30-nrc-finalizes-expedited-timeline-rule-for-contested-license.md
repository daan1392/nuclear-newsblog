The Nuclear Regulatory Commission finalized a rule establishing time limits for contested hearings on nuclear license applications, with most proceedings now targeted for completion within six months. The rulemaking responds to directives in the ADVANCE Act of 2024 and President Trump's Executive Order 14300, which called for streamlining NRC procedures to reduce regulatory burden.

Contested hearings have historically extended for years, creating lengthy delays in the licensing process. The final rule creates three hearing categories—standard, focused, and expedited—allowing adjudication timelines to be tailored to case complexity. The restructuring aims to maintain procedural safeguards and public participation while eliminating unnecessary delays in license reviews and construction permits.

For reactor developers, particularly those advancing SMR designs and license applications, expedited hearing procedures reduce uncertainty in project timelines and lower the cost of regulatory approval processes. The rule represents a substantive shift in NRC administrative practice intended to accelerate nuclear new build.
