South Korea's Nuclear Safety and Security Commission has approved 10-year operating extensions for Kori Nuclear Power Plant's units 3 and 4, both 1,025 MWe Westinghouse pressurized water reactors near Busan.

The reactors received their initial operating licenses in 1984 and 1985, with their 40-year design lives expiring in September 2024 and August 2025, respectively. The approved extensions will permit unit 3 to operate until September 2034 and unit 4 until August 2035. The units are currently out of service, and Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power plans to restart both during the first half of 2027.

The decision reflects South Korea's strategy of extending the operating lives of existing reactors to support stable electricity supply. Obtaining regulatory approval for life extensions requires demonstrations that safety and reliability standards can be maintained beyond the original design period, including assessments of aging components and systems.
