Holtec International completed the recovery of a fuel assembly that had tilted from its upright position at the Palisades nuclear station in Michigan. The company deployed a purpose-built Fuel Movement Device to safely lift and extract the assembly, which was engineered, manufactured and delivered in just two weeks.

Palisades, a single-unit pressurised water reactor, was removed from service in 2022. Holtec began fuel loading in late August as part of efforts to return the plant to operation, but announced a halt to loading in September when the fuel assembly tilted after being placed into the core. The specially designed device enabled controlled lifting and recovery without damaging the fuel or reactor systems.

This incident and its resolution underscore both the complexities of returning long-dormant reactors to service and the adaptability of operators in developing solutions to unforeseen technical challenges. The successful recovery clears the way for Holtec to resume its commissioning schedule.
