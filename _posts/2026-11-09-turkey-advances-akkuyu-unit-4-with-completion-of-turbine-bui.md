Construction crews completed the turbine building foundation for Turkey's Akkuyu Nuclear Power Plant's fourth unit using 2,240 cubic metres of concrete in a continuous pour supported by nine concrete pumps. The operation followed installation of over 308 tonnes of rebar and is expected to reach design strength within 60 days.

According to Akkuyu Nuclear CEO Sergei Butskikh, the team has now completed four turbine foundations at the site, with the process emphasizing reliability established during the construction phase. Regulatory approval from Turkey's Nuclear Regulatory Authority (NDK) must follow before turbine component installation can proceed. The project continues advancing toward bringing additional capacity online at the nation's first nuclear power station.
