Holtec International successfully retrieved a fuel assembly that tilted from its upright position during loading operations at the Palisades nuclear station in Michigan. The company deployed a purpose-built Fuel Movement Device, engineered and manufactured in just two weeks, to safely extract and reposition the assembly.

Palisades, a single-unit pressurized water reactor shut down in 2022, began fuel loading in August as part of Holtec's restart effort. On 21 September, the company halted operations after the fuel assembly tilted once the fuel-handling machine's grapple was disengaged from the assembly. The incident required careful analysis and equipment design to prevent damage to the fuel or reactor internals.

The rapid response demonstrates specialized competency in fuel handling recovery and custom equipment fabrication under time pressure. Safe resolution of the tilted assembly supports the restart project timeline.
