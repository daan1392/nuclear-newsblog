Swedish nuclear technical services provider Studsvik is providing technical information and design guidance to Japanese nuclear engineering start-up ReNu Industries for a domestic version of inDRUM, Studsvik's proprietary technology for treating problematic radioactive wastes. The collaboration supports Japan's decommissioning and waste management objectives at Fukushima.

The inDRUM process addresses waste streams that are difficult to handle using conventional methods, such as ion exchange resins, sludges, and other secondary waste forms generated during site remediation. Treating these materials through inDRUM enables volume reduction, immobilization, and preparation for final disposal or recycling.

The partnership demonstrates growing international collaboration on waste solutions for major decommissioning sites. ReNu's adoption of proven Swedish technology accelerates Japan's ability to process accumulated waste streams while protecting worker safety and the environment. Such technology transfer between established waste specialists and emerging Japanese firms reflects the scale of Japan's long-term decommissioning commitments.
