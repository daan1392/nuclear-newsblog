The Etango uranium project in Namibia has received a positive final investment decision from the joint venture of Bannerman Energy (55%) and CNNC Overseas Limited (45%), clearing the way for full-scale construction. The project is expected to reach commissioning and production by 2028.

Located in the Erongo Region 30 kilometers southeast of Swakopmund, Etango has an estimated mineral resource base of 207 million pounds of U₃O₈, equivalent to approximately 79,622 tonnes of uranium. The site holds significant potential to contribute to global uranium supply as demand rises with nuclear capacity expansion plans worldwide.

The final investment decision represents a major milestone for one of the world's largest undeveloped uranium deposits. Namibia has established itself as a stable jurisdiction for uranium mining and processing, with the infrastructure and regulatory framework to support large-scale operations.
