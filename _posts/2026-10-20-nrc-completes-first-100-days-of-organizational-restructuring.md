The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission assessed progress from its major structural reorganization initiated roughly 100 days prior, which included establishing two new offices: the Office of the Chief Nuclear Reactor Inspector and the Office of Advanced Reactors, along with reorganization around core business lines.

NRC officials and staff met with the five commissioners at the Region IV offices in Arlington, Texas, to evaluate successes and challenges. A key structural change consolidated all four regional offices under the Chief Nuclear Reactor Inspector, centralizing oversight of operating reactors.

The reorganization reflects recognition that regulatory frameworks must evolve as the industry diversifies beyond large conventional light-water reactors toward small modular reactors, accident-tolerant fuels and advanced designs. Establishing dedicated advanced reactor oversight infrastructure acknowledges the need for regulatory expertise development and specialized review processes distinct from legacy reactor licensing.
