California-based Deep Fission has completed a demonstration in which a full-sized prototype canister for its Gravity reactor was installed in a borehole and then retrieved.

The company is developing a small modular reactor designed to sit underground in a borehole about one mile (1.6 km) deep. For the test, it lowered the 20-foot (6-metre) canister to a depth of 100 feet inside a 34-inch (86 cm) wide borehole, aligned it and brought it back to the surface. Deep Fission said this emplacement and retrieval sequence is one its deployment model depends on.

The company said the work used standard equipment already in commercial service in the drilling industry. That supports its argument that the concept can rely on existing oilfield-type technology. The test was at a much shallower depth than the planned operating depth, and it did not involve a nuclear reactor.
