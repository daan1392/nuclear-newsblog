Oklo revealed details of Groves-2, the next phase of its Groves project, building on earlier progress through the Department of Energy's Reactor Pilot Program and enabled by the DOE's follow-on Nuclear Energy Launch Pad Program. The project will focus initially on medical isotope production, aligning with growing demand for nuclear medicine applications.

Oklo's multi-unit Groves project represents one of several private ventures developing advanced reactor technology with federal support. By targeting medical isotope production as an initial revenue stream, the company aims to demonstrate the commercial viability of its reactor design while contributing to domestic supply of critical medical radionuclides.

The shift toward isotope production reflects broader industry interest in deploying advanced reactors for non-electricity applications, including medical, industrial, and research markets where demand continues to outpace supply.
