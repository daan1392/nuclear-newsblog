Fluor Corporation has been contracted to provide design and engineering services for X-energy's second commercial deployment of its Xe-100 small modular reactor, at Energy Northwest's proposed Cascade Advanced Energy Facility near Richland, Washington. This represents the second commercial engagement between Fluor and X-energy, building on work initiated in April supporting the first Xe-100 project at Dow's UCC Seadrift facility in Texas under the Department of Energy's Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program. The Xe-100 is a 76-MW high-temperature gas-cooled design aimed at industrial heat and electricity applications. Fluor's continued involvement demonstrates confidence in both the X-energy design and the commercial viability of the ARDP demonstration pathway. Multiple projects reaching design and engineering phases indicate progress toward operational deployment of advanced reactor technologies.
