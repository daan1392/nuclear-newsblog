Copenhagen Atomics and Urenco have established a partnership to develop a deconversion plant that converts uranium hexafluoride (UF6) to uranium tetrafluoride (UF4), the feedstock required by Copenhagen Atomics' molten salt reactors (MSRs). The memorandum of understanding leverages Urenco's expertise in uranium enrichment services to support the MSR developer's fuel requirements.

The arrangement represents a novel collaboration between an advanced reactor developer and a traditional fuel cycle company, creating a pathway to supply specialized fuel forms for next-generation reactor designs. Copenhagen Atomics has also purchased low-enriched uranium from Urenco as part of their growing commercial relationship.

This partnership demonstrates how emerging reactor technologies are driving evolution across the fuel cycle supply chain to meet non-traditional fuel specifications.
