Copenhagen Atomics and Urenco signed a memorandum of understanding to develop a deconversion facility producing uranium tetrafluoride (UF4) feedstock at a Copenhagen Atomics location. The partnership leverages Urenco's expertise in uranium hexafluoride (UF6) production to create a UF6-to-UF4 conversion pathway suited to molten salt reactor fuel requirements.

Molten salt reactors require UF4 fuel form, which differs from the UF6 used in conventional light-water reactor fuel cycles. The collaboration addresses a supply chain gap for emerging reactor technologies by enabling conversion of existing enriched uranium stocks into formats compatible with alternative reactor designs.

Copenhagen Atomics has also purchased low-enriched uranium from Urenco as part of the collaboration, establishing a commercial relationship alongside the technical partnership. This arrangement exemplifies how fuel cycle suppliers are adapting to serve diverse reactor technologies beyond the traditional pressurised and boiling water reactor markets.
