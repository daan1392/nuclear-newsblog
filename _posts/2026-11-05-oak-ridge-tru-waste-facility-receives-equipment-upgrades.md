The Department of Energy's Office of Environmental Management has announced upgrades to the Transuranic Waste Processing Center at the Oak Ridge Reservation cleanup site. The facility's cask processing enclosure has been remodeled with new equipment to expand its capability to process forms of transuranic waste different from those handled in the past.

The TWPC comprises four functional areas: a contact-handled glovebox system, a hot cell facility, a box breakdown area, and the recently updated cask processing enclosure. The CPE upgrades enable the facility to address a broader range of TRU waste types encountered during site remediation, supporting more efficient cleanup operations.

Transuranic waste—containing human-made elements heavier than uranium—requires specialized handling and eventual disposal in deep geological repositories. The expanded processing capability at Oak Ridge accelerates the pathway toward permanent waste disposition and reduces the burden on interim storage.
