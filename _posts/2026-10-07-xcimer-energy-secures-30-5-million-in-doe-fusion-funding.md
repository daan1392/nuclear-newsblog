Xcimer Energy announced a finalised agreement for up to $30.5 million in additional federal funding through the U.S. Department of Energy's Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program, awarded for the second budget period of the company's development roadmap.

In June, the DOE formally approved Xcimer's preconceptual design and technology roadmap for Athena, the company's architecture for industrial-scale fusion power plants. Athena integrates Xcimer's proprietary excimer laser platform with target delivery, fusion chamber, tritium breeding, and power conversion systems designed for continuous operation and industrial deployment.

The milestone-based approach ties funding to technical achievements, aligning investment with demonstrated progress. Xcimer's laser-based inertial confinement fusion approach complements other private fusion ventures pursuing magnetic confinement and alternative concepts, diversifying the technological portfolio U.S. fusion development.
