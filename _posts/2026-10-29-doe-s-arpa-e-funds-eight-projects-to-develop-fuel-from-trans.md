The Department of Energy's ARPA-E announced eight research projects selected for funding under its High-Performance Optimized Recycled Nuclear Isotopes for Gen IV Reactors (HORNIG) program. The initiative supports technology development to repurpose transuranic waste elements—including plutonium derived from nuclear weapons production—into fuel for commercial advanced reactors. By converting legacy spent fuel stockpiles and weapons-derived materials into reactor fuel, the program advances circular fuel cycle strategies while reducing long-term waste storage burdens. The projects align with Executive Order 14302 on reinvigorating the nuclear industrial base and demonstrate federal commitment to closing the fuel cycle for next-generation reactor designs.
