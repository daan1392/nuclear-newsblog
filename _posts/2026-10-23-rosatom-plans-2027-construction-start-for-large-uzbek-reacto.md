Rosatom CEO Alexey Likhachev announced during a site visit that construction of large-scale nuclear units will commence in 2027 at the planned power station in Jizzakh region, Uzbekistan. Two small modular reactors are already under construction at the location, with foundation work begun in May and scheduled for completion by year's end. The facility will eventually include two 1,000 MW conventional units alongside the SMRs. Likhachev described the SMR component as "a unique, state-of-the-art small nuclear power plant project" and "the only one in the world currently being implemented using an export model." The phased approach—combining SMR deployment with subsequent large reactor construction—reflects Uzbekistan's energy expansion strategy and positions Russia's nuclear export sector for sustained international projects.
