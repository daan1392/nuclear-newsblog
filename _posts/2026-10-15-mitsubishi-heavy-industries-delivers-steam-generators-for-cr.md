Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (MHI) delivered three replacement steam generators to Électricité de France (EDF) for installation at Cruas unit 2, a pressurised water reactor (PWR) in southeastern France. Each steam generator is approximately 21 metres tall, weighs roughly 330 tonnes, and is fabricated from low-alloy steel (containing not more than 5% alloying elements) to withstand high pressure and temperature.

Steam generators are crucial secondary-side components in PWRs, transferring thermal energy from the reactor's primary cooling circuit to the secondary circuit, where steam drives the electricity-generation turbine. Replacement of aging steam generators is a routine life-management activity at operating reactors, typically conducted during outages planned years in advance.

MHI's delivery demonstrates ongoing international supply chain activity for reactor components and highlights France's strategy of maintaining and upgrading existing PWR capacity. The logistics of manufacturing, transporting and installing massive pressure vessels underscore the industrial complexity of reactor operations and maintenance.
