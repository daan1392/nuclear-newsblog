Germany's Federal Company for Radioactive Waste Disposal announced that it has narrowed the area under consideration for a high-level radioactive waste repository from 25 percent of the country's territory to less than 5 percent. The site selection procedure, mandated by German nuclear policy, evaluates geological suitability for long-term isolation of long-lived radioactive waste. Reduction of candidate regions reflects geotechnical and hydrogeological assessment phases that screened out areas not meeting stringent disposal criteria. Germany's disposal program targets identification of an optimal site by the early 2030s, with repository development extending into subsequent decades. The narrowed search area accelerates the systematic evaluation process while maintaining scientific rigor in identifying formations capable of providing multi-generational containment.
