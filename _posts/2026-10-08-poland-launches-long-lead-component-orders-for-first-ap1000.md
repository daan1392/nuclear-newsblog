Polskie Elektrownie Jądrowe (PEJ), Poland's dedicated nuclear operator, commissioned Westinghouse to start ordering long-lead components for the first unit of Poland's inaugural nuclear power plant, a project planned to comprise three AP1000 reactors. Long-lead items typically include heavy forgings and castings for pressure vessels, steam turbines, generators, and engineered components subject to lengthy manufacturing and delivery schedules.

With limited global capacity for such components, early ordering is essential to meet project timelines. The initial order covers long-lead items for unit 1, with procurement proceeding in parallel with detailed design and permitting activities. Poland's first nuclear plant represents a major energy security investment, supplementing the country's coal-dependent grid with large-scale low-carbon generation.

The start of long-lead procurement signals advancing readiness for construction, though full financing, regulatory approvals, and final investment decisions remain to be formalised.
