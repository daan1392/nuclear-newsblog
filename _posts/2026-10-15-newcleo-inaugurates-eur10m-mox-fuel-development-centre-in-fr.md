Newcleo inaugurated its FASTER facility near the Marcoule nuclear site in Chusclan, France, investing EUR10 million in advanced nuclear fuel engineering, testing and training capabilities. The centre supports the development of mixed-oxide (MOX) fuel manufacturing technology, a key pathway for utilizing plutonium and reducing long-term radioactive waste storage requirements.

The facility serves Newcleo's team of approximately 100 fuel engineers and specialists, providing prototype testing and immersive training environments. FASTER—standing for Fuel process Assembly Storage Training and Enhanced Reality—was announced in March 2025 and reflects growing industry focus on advanced fuel technologies that can improve reactor safety and efficiency.

MOX fuel, which blends plutonium with uranium oxides, enables the recycling of spent nuclear fuel and reduces the volume of material requiring long-term geological disposal. The technology is particularly important for countries pursuing closed fuel cycle strategies. Newcleo's investment underscores renewed commercial interest in advanced fuel development as nuclear capacity expands globally.
