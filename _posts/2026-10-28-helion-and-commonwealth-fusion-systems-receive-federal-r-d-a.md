The US Department of Commerce announced the signing of letters of intent for federal research and development awards to fusion companies Helion and Commonwealth Fusion Systems under the CHIPS and Science Act, focused on semiconductors and power electronics required for fusion reactor development.

Under the terms of the awards, the Department of Commerce will receive minority, non-controlling and non-voting equity stakes in each company as a condition of the funding. According to the Commerce Department announcement, the investments are intended to "expand domestic research, secure critical supply chains, and create high-paying American jobs."

The awards represent continued federal support for private fusion development, recognizing the technology's potential for clean baseload electricity generation. The equity stake structure aligns government and private sector incentives while preserving company operational independence. Both Helion and Commonwealth Fusion Systems are among the more advanced private fusion ventures pursuing demonstration and commercial deployment pathways.
