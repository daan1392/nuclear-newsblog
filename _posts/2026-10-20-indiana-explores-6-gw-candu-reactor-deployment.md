Indiana, Hoosier Energy Rural Electric Cooperative and Montreal-based AtkinsRéalis signed a memorandum of understanding to explore the feasibility of deploying up to eight CANDU nuclear power reactors across the state, potentially adding 6 GW of new generating capacity. The evaluation will also consider converting retired or retiring fossil fuel power plants to accelerate deployment.

The MOU signals growing state-level interest in nuclear capacity as electricity demand pressures continue. The partnership will examine potential deployment scenarios and construction models, with project timeline contingent on site characterization, environmental assessments and transmission infrastructure readiness.

The announcement comes as Indiana has emerged as a focal point for nuclear development, with multiple competing proposals announced within days. The diversity of technologies under consideration—from X-energy's Xe-100 SMRs to large CANDU units—reflects the varied pathways different regions are exploring to meet rising power demand.
