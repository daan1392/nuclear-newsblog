TÜPRAŞ, Turkey's largest crude oil refiner, signed an agreement with the US Trade and Development Agency to fund technical assistance evaluating small modular reactors for supplying electricity, steam, and heat to its refining processes. The study will assess the technical and economic feasibility of SMR deployment for industrial applications.

Refining operations are energy-intensive, requiring sustained heat and steam supply for crude oil processing. SMRs designed for industrial process heat and cogeneration offer potential advantages including smaller physical footprint, passive safety features, and flexibility in siting compared to large reactors. Industrial heat applications represent a significant market opportunity for advanced reactors beyond traditional electricity generation.

The feasibility study reflects growing interest among energy-intensive industries in evaluating nuclear options for decarbonization and cost reduction. Such assessments provide technical and commercial foundations necessary for future deployment decisions and help establish the market case for industrial SMR designs.
