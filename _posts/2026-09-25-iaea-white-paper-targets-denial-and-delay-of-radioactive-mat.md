An IAEA working group has issued recommendations to reduce the denial and delay of shipments of radioactive material that nuclear plants, hospitals, industries and researchers depend on.

The White Paper on the Facilitation of the Safe and Secure Transport of Radioactive Material was prepared by the agency's Denial of Shipment Working Group. It was presented at a side event during the IAEA's 70th General Conference.

More than 20 million packages requiring transport are shipped each year. IAEA Director General Rafael Mariano Grossi told the packed meeting that, although there had been progress, the issue still presents challenges.

Denials and delays can disrupt supplies of materials such as medical isotopes, where short half-lives make timing critical, and affect plant operations and research. The paper's recommendations aim to help states and carriers ensure that safe and secure transport is not obstructed.
