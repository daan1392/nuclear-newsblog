The provision aims to reduce bureaucratic delays in adapting transport regulations to new technologies and operational needs. This change is part of a broader legislative package that addresses multiple sectors of the UK energy system, including support for offshore wind, grid modernisation, residential efficiency, and an end to new oil and gas licences. Streamlining transport regulations could support the expansion of the UK's nuclear infrastructure, particularly for new reactor projects and waste management initiatives, by removing procedural bottlenecks.
