Hyundai Engineering of South Korea signed memorandums of understanding with two US small modular reactor developers—NANO Nuclear Energy and ARC Clean Technology—to establish potential collaboration frameworks. The MoU with NANO Nuclear aims to advance the KRONOS micro modular reactor (MMR) energy system project at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and evaluate broader manufacturing and deployment cooperation. NANO Nuclear's KRONOS design is based on high-temperature gas reactor technology. The partnerships position Hyundai to develop capabilities in the emerging global SMR market, combining South Korean engineering and construction expertise with American advanced reactor designs. The agreements remain non-binding assessments of technical and commercial feasibility. Hyundai's international partnerships reflect competitive positioning in the anticipated global expansion of SMR deployments across the 2030s.
