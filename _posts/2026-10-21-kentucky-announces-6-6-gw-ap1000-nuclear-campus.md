The Nuclear Company unveiled plans for an energy campus in Lewis County, Kentucky, featuring up to six Westinghouse AP1000 reactors with combined capacity of 6.6 GW—larger than any single nuclear plant currently operating in the US. The development partnership with landowner Trace Creek Construction aims to begin construction in 2028.

The "Prosperity One" project will initially focus on detailed site characterization, geotechnical investigations, environmental assessments and transmission infrastructure planning. Situated along the Ohio River, the site offers access to cooling water and existing infrastructure advantages.

The ambitious proposal reflects a trend of large-scale nuclear development initiatives attracting venture-backed developers willing to pursue multi-reactor deployments at previously unexplored sites. Kentucky's emergence as a nuclear development focus follows state government interest in leveraging available federal funding for new nuclear projects.
