The International Atomic Energy Agency and the Inter-American Development Bank expanded their cooperative framework for nuclear energy projects in September 2026. The initiative responds to significant untapped potential across Latin America, where only Argentina, Brazil, and Mexico currently operate nuclear power plants.

Nuclear energy accounts for less than 2 percent of regional electricity generation, substantially below the global average of roughly 8 percent. However, substantial nuclear deployments are projected to increase in coming decades across the continent, driven by decarbonization priorities and energy demand growth.

The IAEA-IDB partnership aims to facilitate knowledge transfer, build regulatory capacity, and mobilize financing mechanisms necessary to support these expansions. By coordinating institutional resources and expertise, the agencies are positioning themselves to help Latin American nations develop robust nuclear frameworks and realize planned projects.
