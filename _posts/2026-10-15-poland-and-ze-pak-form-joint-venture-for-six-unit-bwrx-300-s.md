Poland's SGE (Sythos Green Energy) and ZE Pak agreed to form a joint venture to develop a nuclear power plant at Pątnów featuring up to six BWRX-300 small modular reactors. The proposed facility would have a total capacity of 1,800 MWe and represents part of Poland's broader strategy to deploy 26 BWRX-300 units across multiple sites.

SGE is a co-investor in GE Vernova Hitachi's BWRX-300 standard design and is pursuing SMR projects across Europe, including proposals for the UK Advanced Nuclear Framework programme. The Pątnów project builds on Poland's decision in principle to support SMR deployment, reflecting the country's shift toward reducing reliance on coal-fired generation.

The BWRX-300 is a 300 MWe boiling water reactor design that operates at atmospheric pressure and uses passive safety cooling, reducing construction complexity and cost compared to large conventional reactors. Poland's investment in distributed SMR capacity addresses both decarbonization goals and the technical challenge of integrating large new reactors into existing grids with aging coal infrastructure.
