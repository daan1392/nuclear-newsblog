The US Department of Energy's Office of Energy Dominance Financing announced a conditional loan commitment of up to USD 5 billion to support Constellation Energy's uprate and modernisation projects across eight nuclear plants in Illinois, Maryland and Pennsylvania. The investments are expected to add approximately 1.1 GW of generating capacity to the existing US nuclear fleet.

Uprates involve increasing the electrical output of reactors above their original licensed power level through equipment upgrades and operational optimisations. Modernisation projects improve plant efficiency and extend operational lifespans. The DOE loan programme aims to accelerate deployment of such projects, which contribute to grid decarbonisation and energy security.

This financing exemplifies the US government's renewed commitment to supporting existing nuclear capacity as part of its clean energy strategy. By making capital available for uprate projects—which typically represent lower development risk and faster deployment timescales than new builds—the programme can deliver emissions reductions and generation capacity relatively quickly.
