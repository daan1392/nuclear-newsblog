Polskie Elektrownie Jądrowe (PEJ), Poland's nuclear power operator, has commissioned Westinghouse Electric to begin procuring long-lead items (LLIs) for the first AP1000 reactor unit of Poland's inaugural nuclear power plant at Lubiatowo-Kopalino. Long-lead procurement is one of the most critical stages in new plant development, ensuring that key components are available on schedule to support construction timelines.

Long-lead items for large reactors typically include forgings, pressure vessels, steam generators, and other components with extended manufacturing lead times of two to three years or more. Early procurement prevents delays that could otherwise jeopardise overall project schedules.

Poland has committed to building multiple AP1000 units as part of its decarbonisation strategy and to reduce reliance on imported energy. Westinghouse's long-lead procurement marks a tangible step toward realising the Lubiatowo-Kopalino project and establishing Poland as a new nuclear-powered nation in Central Europe.
