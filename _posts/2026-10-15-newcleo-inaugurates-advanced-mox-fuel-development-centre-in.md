Innovative nuclear energy company Newcleo has inaugurated its FASTER (Fuel process Assembly Storage Training and Enhanced Reality) facility in Chusclan in the Gard department of southern France. The EUR10 million centre provides advanced engineering, prototype testing, and immersive training capabilities for Newcleo's fuel team of approximately 100 engineers and specialists.

Located near the Marcoule nuclear site, the facility supports development of Newcleo's mixed-oxide (MOX) fuel manufacturing technology, a key component of the company's advanced reactor designs. The centre combines hands-on engineering capabilities with digital training environments to support fuel development and workforce training.
