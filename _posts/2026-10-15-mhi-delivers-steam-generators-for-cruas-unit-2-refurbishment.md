Japan's Mitsubishi Heavy Industries has delivered three replacement steam generators to EDF for installation at unit 2 of the Cruas nuclear power plant in southeastern France. The generators, each standing approximately 21 metres tall and weighing roughly 330 tonnes, are pressure-resistant vessels made of strong low-alloy steel.

Steam generators are critical components in pressurised water reactors (PWRs), transferring thermal energy from the primary reactor cooling system to the secondary system that drives the electricity-generating turbine. Component replacements like this support long-term reactor operations.
