Seven packages of processed low- and intermediate-level radioactive waste were shipped from Chernobyl's new Industrial Complex for Solid Radioactive Waste Management (ICSRM), the first such transfer from the facility.

The packages went to the Specially Equipped Near-surface Disposal Facility for Solid Radioactive Waste. The ICSRM began operating in May after receiving its licence from Ukraine's State Nuclear Regulatory Inspectorate.

The complex processes solid waste accumulated while the Chernobyl plant was operating, which had been held in a storage facility. Conditioning such waste into stable packages is a necessary step before it can be placed in permanent disposal, so the first transfer marks the facility moving from start-up to routine output.

The work is part of the long-running effort to manage the legacy of the Chernobyl site.
