Fuel loading at the Palisades nuclear plant in Covert, Michigan, has been paused after an incident involving a fuel assembly, operator Holtec has acknowledged.

Loading began on August 30, when the plant entered Mode 6, the refuelling stage of the restart. The core comprises 204 fuel assemblies, a mix of new fuel and partially used fuel from the plant's most recent operating cycles. The loading was reported as the first time a commercial US reactor that had entered decommissioning had begun reloading fuel; the vessel had been empty since the plant shut down in 2022.

The supplied reports give no details of the nature or consequences of the incident or how long the pause will last. The plant has also been connected to the switchyard, the site's interface with the electrical grid.

The pause is a setback, at least in the short term, for a closely watched restart project. Further information from Holtec and the regulator will clarify its significance.
