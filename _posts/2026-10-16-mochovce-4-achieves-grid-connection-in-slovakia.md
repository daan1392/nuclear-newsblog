Slovakia's newest nuclear power unit, Mochovce 4, has been connected to the electricity grid and started supplying its first electricity on October 15 at 20% power output. The VVER-440 unit has a total capacity of 471 MWe and will generate electricity equal to about 13% of Slovakia's current total consumption.

The grid connection marks the culmination of decades of construction and commissioning work at the site. Mochovce 4 joins Slovakia's existing nuclear fleet in supporting the country's low-carbon electricity supply and energy security.
