Ion Beam Applications and Framatome announced formation of Ekalia, a jointly owned company to deploy a network of astatine-211 production sites near customers across Europe and the US. Astatine-211 is an alpha-emitting isotope used in targeted cancer therapies and theranostics (combined diagnostic and therapeutic applications).

The first production site, planned for Nantes, France, will receive €30 million investment. Permit applications have been submitted, with construction anticipated to begin in early 2027 and operational status targeted for 2028. A second pilot site is planned in the US, though location and timing remain to be confirmed.

The expansion addresses growing clinical demand for alpha-emitting radiopharmaceuticals as oncologists recognize advantages in targeting treatment to cancer cells while minimizing damage to healthy tissue. Establishing dedicated At-211 production infrastructure close to hospitals and nuclear medicine centers eliminates decay losses inherent in long-distance isotope transport.
