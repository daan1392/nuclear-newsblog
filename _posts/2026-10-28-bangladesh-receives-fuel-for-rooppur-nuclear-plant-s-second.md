Rosatom, Russia's state nuclear corporation, delivered the initial fuel load for Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant's second unit to Bangladesh. The fuel assemblies were produced at Rosatom's Novosibirsk Chemical Concentrates Plant and transported by cargo aircraft and road to a specialized storage facility at the site.

Both Bangladeshi experts and representatives from the general contractor and fuel manufacturer conducted mandatory incoming inspections, including visual and instrumental testing to confirm compliance with technical specifications. The Rooppur plant, a joint Bangladesh-Russia project, features two VVER-1200 reactors and represents Bangladesh's first nuclear power generation capacity.

Fuel delivery marks a significant step in plant commissioning, preceding the sequence of physical and operational start-up tests required before commercial operation. The two-unit project is expected to contribute substantially to Bangladesh's electricity supply, supporting the country's growing demand while reducing reliance on fossil fuels.
