Oklo announced plans for the Groves-2 Forge isotope production and processing pilot facility as part of the Department of Energy's Nuclear Energy Launch Pad initiative. The facility will expand on the company's Groves Isotope Test Reactor, which achieved criticality earlier in 2026.

The Launch Pad initiative, managed by Idaho National Laboratory's National Reactor Innovation Center, provides a streamlined regulatory pathway for reactor and fuel cycle developments. Oklo was one of twelve companies selected for the program in August.

The pilot facility represents a next step in commercializing medical isotope production at smaller scale using the Groves reactor platform. Oklo joins a growing cohort of companies seeking to increase domestic production of isotopes for nuclear medicine applications.
