Urenco USA achieved the first commercial production of LEU+ at its National Enrichment Facility in New Mexico, making it the first production of this higher-assay low-enriched uranium outside of state-controlled facilities. The company received NRC authorisation in September 2025 to enrich uranium up to 10% U-235, and has now licensed all gas centrifuge cascades at its site for LEU+ production. The fuel option provides reactor operators with an alternative to traditional low-enriched uranium and enables designs that benefit from higher assay without requiring highly enriched fuel. Managing Director Jody Blackshear highlighted the coordination across site and company teams required to reach this production milestone.
