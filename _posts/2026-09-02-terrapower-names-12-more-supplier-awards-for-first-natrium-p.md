TerraPower announced 12 supplier awards as it builds out the supply chain for its first Natrium plant in Kemmerer, Wyoming.

The awards went to Merrick & Company (two contracts), Mirion Technologies, BWXT Canada, Teledyne Brown Engineering, Marmen, Thermal Engineering International (USA), Jensen Hughes, Boston Government Services, James Fisher Technologies, KROHNE Nuclear and Valserve.

Natrium pairs a 345 MWe sodium-cooled fast reactor with a molten salt energy storage system. The storage can temporarily lift output to 500 MWe. That lets the plant follow daily changes in electricity demand and work alongside variable renewable generation.

The selections show the project moving from design towards procurement, with a mix of Canadian, US and other suppliers.
