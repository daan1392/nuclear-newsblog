The second round of selections under the US Nuclear Energy Launch Pad included fuel cycle projects as well as reactor developers.

The Launch Pad is an initiative of the Department of Energy's National Reactor Innovation Center. It aims to speed up the development and deployment of advanced nuclear technologies by private industry. Among the projects in this round are plans for a new conversion facility and a fuel fabrication plant at Idaho National Laboratory.

Conversion turns uranium concentrate into a form suitable for enrichment. Fabrication makes finished fuel for reactors. Both are steps that many advanced reactor designs need before they can operate commercially.

The inclusion of fuel-side projects suggests that supply chain bottlenecks are being treated as seriously as reactor designs themselves.
