Holtec Nuclear Corporation postponed the initial public offering (IPO) it announced in July, citing market conditions.

The company's statement was brief and said only that it would keep assessing the timing of an offering. In an interview published by the Financial Times, however, CEO Kris Singh said a recent market turn against the AI data centre economy was responsible. "It's like a perfect storm," he told the newspaper.

The delay is notable because investor appetite for nuclear companies has been closely tied to expectations of rising electricity demand from data centres. Holtec is involved in several areas of the sector, including plans for small modular reactors at the Palisades site in Michigan. The decision shows how exposed nuclear fundraising can be to swings in sentiment around AI infrastructure. No new date for the offering was given.
