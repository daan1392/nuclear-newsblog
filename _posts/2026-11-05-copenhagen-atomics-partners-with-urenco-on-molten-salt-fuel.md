Danish molten salt reactor developer Copenhagen Atomics and uranium enrichment services provider Urenco have signed a memorandum of understanding to construct a uranium deconversion facility at Copenhagen Atomics' fuel production site in the Danish capital. The facility will convert uranium hexafluoride (UF6) into uranium tetrafluoride (UF4), which is then synthesized into high-purity molten salt fuel for commercial deployment. Copenhagen Atomics will produce fuel salts for its own reactor designs while also supplying salts to other molten salt reactor developers and research institutions, supporting the emerging MSR commercial sector. The partnership leverages Urenco's expertise in nuclear fuel services with Copenhagen Atomics' advanced reactor technology platform. Molten salt reactors, which operate at high temperatures with liquid fuel, are seen as a promising technology for both power generation and industrial heat applications.
