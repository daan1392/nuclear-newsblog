Canada's 2026 budget, released in early November, sets out government funding for nuclear energy investments and a strategy to double national grid capacity by 2050. The budget frames these initiatives as part of broader "nation-building" policy, with 28 major projects referred to the federal Major Projects Office for expedited review and approval.

Ontario Power Generation's Darlington New Nuclear project, which proposes new reactor construction at an existing station, was among the first projects referred for accelerated consideration. The budget signals sustained government commitment to nuclear power as a key component of Canada's energy security and industrial competitiveness, particularly as electricity demand from emerging technologies and manufacturing rises.
