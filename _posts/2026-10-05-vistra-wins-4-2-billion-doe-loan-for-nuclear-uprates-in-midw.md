The Department of Energy's Office of Energy Dominance Financing announced a conditional loan commitment of up to $4.2 billion to support uprates and modernization at three nuclear plants operated by Vistra Energy.

The projects target Beaver Valley in Pennsylvania and Davis-Besse and Perry in Ohio, with the potential to add 433 megawatts of net new nuclear capacity. Uprates—operating changes that increase electrical output—offer cost-effective approaches to expanding clean electricity supply without building new facilities.

The loan backing reflects federal policy prioritizing nuclear generation as a decarbonization solution. By financing modernization and output increases at operating plants, the programme aims to extend nuclear contributions to grid reliability and emissions reduction, particularly in regions facing growing electricity demand.

These projects demonstrate the economic viability of extracting additional capacity from the existing fleet, a strategy complementing new reactor development efforts across the U.S. nuclear sector.
