An International Chamber of Commerce arbitration tribunal has ruled on Fennovoima's termination of its contract with Russia's Rosatom for the Hanhikivi-1 nuclear power plant project. According to Rosatom's in-house publication, the tribunal found that while Fennovoima had not been technically entitled to terminate the contract, the company was entitled to receive reimbursement of advance payments and interest.

Fennovoima terminated the Hanhikivi-1 project, which was to deploy a 1,200 MWe AES-2006 VVER unit in Finland, only weeks after Russia's invasion of Ukraine began in February 2022. The decision reflected Finland's strategic pivot away from Russian energy dependencies following the invasion.

The arbitration ruling, which has not yet been published by the ICC, establishes the legal basis for Fennovoima's actions while clarifying financial obligations. The outcome allows Finland to proceed with its revised nuclear strategy—pivoting toward Western reactor suppliers—while resolving outstanding contractual disputes with Rosatom. The ruling underscores the complex legal and geopolitical dimensions of large international nuclear projects.
