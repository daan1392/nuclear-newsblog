Deep Isolation completed a cost assessment for Slovenia's radioactive waste agency examining options for disposing of spent fuel from the TRIGA II research reactor and the Krško Nuclear Power Plant. The analysis concluded that co-locating both waste streams in a single deep borehole disposal facility would be more economical than separate disposal pathways. Deep borehole disposal is an alternative to traditional deep geological repositories, using boreholes up to several kilometers deep to isolate waste in stable rock formations. The study suggests this approach could offer cost advantages while maintaining safety and environmental protection. Slovenia's consideration of deep borehole disposal reflects broader interest in the international nuclear waste management community in diversified technical and economic approaches to spent fuel disposition.
