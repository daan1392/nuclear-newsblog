The Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics in Germany and Columbia University established the Max Planck Columbia Center, a joint transatlantic virtual fusion research center directed by IPP director Thomas Klinger and Columbia associate professor Carlos Paz-Soldan.

The partnership pools expertise across two leading fusion research programs to accelerate progress toward commercially viable fusion energy. Paz-Soldan also directs the Columbia Fusion Research Center, positioning the collaboration to leverage institutional capabilities in plasma physics, materials science and systems engineering.

The center reflects growing international coordination in fusion development as multiple public and private programs advance toward demonstration and commercial deployment. Virtual collaboration models enable sustained research cooperation independent of geographic constraints, particularly valuable for fusion science requiring specialized facilities and long-term research commitments.
