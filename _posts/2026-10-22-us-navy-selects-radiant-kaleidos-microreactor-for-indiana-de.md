The US Navy selected Radiant's Kaleidos microreactor for deployment at Naval Weapons Station Crane in Indiana, making Radiant the only company to receive selection from all three US military departments—Army, Air Force, and Navy.

The Kaleidos is a factory-built, 1 MW high-temperature gas-cooled transportable microreactor. The company describes it as "plug-in ready" for direct on-site deployment without requiring cooling water, major excavation, or extensive construction. Each reactor provides approximately five years of power before refueling across a 20-year operational life.

This deployment represents progress in integrating microreactors into US military infrastructure for remote operations and base power. The selection by all three service branches underscores military interest in distributed nuclear generation as a solution for energy security and resilience at facilities where grid power may be unreliable or vulnerable.
