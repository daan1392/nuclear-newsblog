Officials in Wisconsin and Vermont announced plans to assess small modular reactor deployment at sites formerly occupied by traditional large reactors. In Wisconsin, Dairyland Power Cooperative and reactor developer Applied Atomics signed a letter of intent to explore SMR feasibility at Dairyland's La Crosse nuclear power plant site in Genoa. Vermont's comparable initiative targets a former reactor location. Site reuse represents a pragmatic pathway for SMR deployment, leveraging existing infrastructure including power lines, cooling water access, regulatory familiarity, and trained workforces. Former nuclear sites offer reduced licensing complexity compared to greenfield development and community history with nuclear operations. Both projects remain in early assessment phases, with technical, economic, and regulatory feasibility studies forthcoming. The announcements reflect broader industry momentum toward SMR commercialization in the United States, supported by federal policy prioritizing distributed nuclear generation.
