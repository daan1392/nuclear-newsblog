BWX Technologies announced that its BANR (BWXT Advanced Nuclear Reactor) microreactor has been selected by Prodigy Clean Energy and First Nations partners Pabineau and Eel River Bar for a planned project near Belledune in New Brunswick. This marks both the first commercial deployment and first international deployment of the BANR design.

The BANR is a 22 MWe/75 MWt high-temperature gas-cooled reactor using TRISO (TRIstructural-ISOtropic) fuel. Prodigy's Transportable Nuclear Power Plant (TNPP) design will feature two BANR units, intended to validate logistics and operational requirements for wider deployment across Canada's northern regions and global export markets.

The project represents a milestone for advanced reactor development in North America, combining Indigenous-led energy development with commercial microreactor deployment. The partnership demonstrates growing interest in smaller modular reactors as a path to decarbonization in remote and industrial applications.
