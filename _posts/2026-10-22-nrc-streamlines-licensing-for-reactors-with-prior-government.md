The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission finalized a new rule that permits commercial reactor applicants to leverage prior Department of Energy or Department of Defense authorization to support their licensing applications. The measure establishes a more direct pathway to incorporate analyses, test data, and technical evidence from government-sponsored reactor demonstrations into NRC commercial licensing reviews. The rule aims to reduce redundant technical evaluations and accelerate the review process for advanced reactor designs, particularly beneficial for small modular reactors and other innovative concepts that have undergone government-sponsored demonstration programs. By eliminating duplicate reviews of already-validated technical data, the NRC expects to expedite licensing while maintaining rigorous safety standards. This regulatory adjustment reflects growing cooperation between federal agencies and the emerging private reactor sector.
