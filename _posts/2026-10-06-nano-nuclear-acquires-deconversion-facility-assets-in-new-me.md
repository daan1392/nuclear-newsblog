Nano Nuclear announced on October 1 that it had agreed to acquire intellectual property assets and related holdings from Radnostix Inc. associated with a previously planned deconversion facility in Lea County, New Mexico.

Deconversion facilities process depleted uranium—a byproduct of uranium enrichment—into more manageable forms for long-term storage or beneficial reuse. Radnostix had planned the Lea County facility but did not proceed to completion. Nano Nuclear's acquisition of these assets enables the company to pursue development in the nuclear fuel cycle segment alongside its primary focus on high-temperature gas-cooled microreactor deployment.

The transaction reflects increasing vertical integration in the nuclear industry, with reactor vendors seeking to establish supply chain capabilities. Controlling deconversion and fuel production assets can support competitive positioning and operational independence as the nuclear sector expands.

Nano Nuclear's expansion into multiple sectors—reactors, fuel cycle, and related infrastructure—mirrors broader industry strategies to establish comprehensive capabilities across the nuclear value chain.
