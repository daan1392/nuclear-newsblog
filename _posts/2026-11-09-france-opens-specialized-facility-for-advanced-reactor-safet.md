France's nuclear safety regulator ASNR (Autorité de Sûreté Nucléaire et de Radioprotection) opened a new testing facility at its Cadarache site dedicated to evaluating the reliability of passive safety systems. These systems, which operate without active intervention or power supply, are a key feature of next-generation reactor designs including small modular reactors and other innovative concepts.

The facility aims to generate the scientific knowledge necessary for independent safety assessment of these advanced technologies, supporting France's regulatory review process. Passive safety systems are particularly important for SMRs and other designs intended for remote or industrial applications where active safety infrastructure may be limited.

The investment reflects growing regulatory focus on establishing rigorous testing frameworks for emerging reactor technologies as they move toward commercialization. By establishing independent evaluation capacity, regulators can better assess technical claims and ensure alignment with international safety standards.
