Centrus Energy's manufacturing plant in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, has dispatched the first AC-100M centrifuge for the company's multibillion-dollar expansion of uranium enrichment capacity at its Piketon, Ohio facility. Under a Department of Energy contract initiated in 2019, the company has been working to establish commercial production of high-assay, low-enriched uranium (HALEU), an advanced nuclear fuel required for next-generation reactor designs including high-temperature gas reactors and fast neutron reactors.

The shipment marks a transition from the Oak Ridge production phase to the final assembly stage at Piketon, with completed centrifuges now being transferred to Ohio for integration into operational cascades. Centrus indicated that manufacturing rates will increase over time as the facility scales up production capacity.

HALEU production addresses a critical supply gap for advanced reactor developers and supports the U.S. domestic fuel cycle infrastructure, a strategic priority underscored by growing commercial interest in SMRs and specialized reactor technologies.
