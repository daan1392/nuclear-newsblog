The Philippines is considering refurbishing and putting into service the Bataan Nuclear Power Plant, a facility completed in 1986 but never operated in the four decades since. The assessment follows growing recognition of nuclear energy's role in meeting electricity demand and decarbonisation goals. The plant's long dormancy presents both an opportunity and a challenge: while the infrastructure exists, extensive upgrades to safety systems, controls, and regulatory compliance will be necessary to meet modern standards. Successful revival would add meaningful capacity to the Philippines' grid and demonstrate renewed political commitment to nuclear power in Southeast Asia, a region where energy demand continues to rise amid climate pressures.
