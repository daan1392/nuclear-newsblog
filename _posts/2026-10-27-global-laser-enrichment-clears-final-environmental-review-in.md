The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission released a final environmental impact statement for Global Laser Enrichment's proposed Paducah Laser Enrichment Facility (PLEF) in Kentucky. The document supports issuance of a construction and operation licence for the plant, which would use laser-based isotope separation technology developed by Australian firm Silex Systems.

GLE submitted its Environmental Report to the NRC in December 2024 and completed its licence application in July 2025. The Final Safety Evaluation Report—the last major regulatory milestone before a licensing decision—is expected by the end of 2026. The environmental review represents significant regulatory progress for a technology that could expand US domestic enrichment capacity beyond existing gaseous diffusion methods.

Silex Systems holds a 51% ownership stake in GLE. The facility represents part of broader US efforts to strengthen nuclear fuel supply chains and reduce reliance on foreign enrichment services.
