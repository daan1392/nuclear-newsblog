China National Nuclear Corporation reported significant construction progress on two Hualong One reactor projects. The inner containment dome was completed at Zhangzhou unit 3 in Fujian province on 30 September, marking completion of the unit's civil engineering construction. This milestone prepares the way for prestressed containment construction and installation of core nuclear equipment.

Concurrently, concrete pours were finished at units 1 and 2 of the Zhaoyuan plant in Shandong province, advancing another pair of Hualong One units. The Hualong One is CNNC's domestically developed large reactor design, with variants operating and under construction across China and internationally.

These milestones illustrate the pace of nuclear construction in China and underscore the technology's role in meeting the country's energy and climate targets. Multiple Hualong One units are in various stages of construction and operation globally.
