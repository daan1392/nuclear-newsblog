Bruce unit 5 was taken offline on 1 November to begin its Major Component Replacement (MCR) outage, the fourth reactor at the Ontario site to undergo this renewal programme. Bruce Power is executing MCR work on units 3 through 8 under a privately funded plan designed to extend operational life and maintain reliable generation for decades. Units 6 and 3 are already operating post-refurbishment with strong performance, and unit 4 has set production records during its MCR outage. The company noted that these refurbished units provide reliable clean energy supporting Ontario's hospitals, businesses and grid, while maintaining a stable workforce for nuclear operations.
