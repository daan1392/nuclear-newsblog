Akkuyu Nuclear Power Plant in Turkey has completed the turbine building foundation pour for its fourth unit, a major construction milestone involving a continuous pour of more than 2,240 cubic metres of concrete supported by nine concrete pumps and more than 308 tonnes of reinforcing steel. The foundation is expected to reach design strength within approximately 60 days, after which Turkish Nuclear Regulatory Authority inspection and approval will precede installation of turbine equipment. This represents the fourth turbine foundation completed at the site and reflects steady progress on the multi-unit project. Akkuyu is Russia's first nuclear power plant built abroad and an important addition to Turkey's energy infrastructure, demonstrating continued construction momentum despite regional and global economic challenges.
