The Canadian Space Agency has issued a request for information (RFI) seeking input from Canadian industry and academia on technologies and capabilities related to lunar microreactors. The agency views fission power as complementary to solar and battery systems for sustained lunar operations, including exploration activities, mobility, and crewed missions. The RFI targets technologies at technology readiness level 2 or higher, which may encompass complete reactor systems or specific critical subsystems. This assessment aims to identify areas where Canada possesses differentiated or strategically valuable capabilities in space nuclear power. Such assessments support long-term planning for space exploration architectures and help position participating nations within the emerging sector of space-based fission power systems.
