Russia's state nuclear corporation Rosatom is designing a hydrometallurgical processing plant at Krasnokamensk to increase uranium ore processing capacity to 11,000 tonnes of uranium oxide per year. The facility will process ores from the Argunskoye and Zherlovoe deposits as well as concentrates from other Russian and international Rosatom deposits. Supporting infrastructure and site surveys have already been completed, and design work is progressing toward construction. The expansion reflects Rosatom's strategy to secure fuel cycle capability to support both domestic and international reactor programmes, particularly as global nuclear capacity growth accelerates.
