Molten salt reactor developer Thorizon and Dutch research and technology organisation TNO have established a collaborative materials and corrosion programme aimed at generating the experimental data and predictive models required to qualify materials for use in molten salt reactors (MSRs).

Materials performance is a critical technical challenge for MSR development. Molten salt environments present unique corrosion conditions that demand materials with proven long-term stability and well-characterised mechanical properties at operating temperatures. The Thorizon-TNO programme addresses this gap by systematically investigating material behaviour and developing qualification pathways.

This collaboration exemplifies the research infrastructure being built to support advanced reactor commercialisation, with academic and technical institutes partnering with industry developers to solve engineering challenges that currently limit deployment. Successful qualification of MSR materials is essential for regulatory licensing and investor confidence in the technology.
