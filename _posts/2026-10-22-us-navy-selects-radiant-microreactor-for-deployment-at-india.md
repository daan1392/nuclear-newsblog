The US Navy selected Radiant's Kaleidos microreactor for deployment at Naval Weapons Station Crane in Indiana. The factory-built, 1 MW high-temperature gas-cooled transportable microreactor requires no cooling water, major excavation or extensive on-site construction, delivering up to five years of power before refueling across a 20-year design life.

Radiant is now the sole company selected by all three US military departments—Army, Air Force and Navy—for microreactor deployment programs. The military's sustained interest in distributed power generation reflects operational advantages of decentralized, transportable nuclear systems for remote bases and installations.
