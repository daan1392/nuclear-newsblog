Saltstone Disposal Unit (SDU) 6 at the Savannah River Site near Aiken, South Carolina, has been filled with grouted legacy tank waste, marking completion of the first of seven mega-volume disposal units at the site.

SDU 6 began receiving waste material in 2018 and represents a major milestone in the decades-long effort to remediate radioactive waste stored underground in 51 carbon-steel tanks. The mega-volume units, approved in 2012, were engineered to accommodate large volumes of grouted waste—a cement-based waste form used to immobilize and stabilize legacy tank contents.

The disposal approach converts liquid radioactive waste into solid, chemically stable waste forms suitable for near-surface disposal. Completion of SDU 6 demonstrates progress in reducing the site's environmental liability and accelerates the overall tank farm closure timeline.
