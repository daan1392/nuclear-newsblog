The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) and the Department of Energy's Office of Energy Dominance Financing have issued an environmental assessment and a finding of no significant impact for the proposed federal actions tied to reauthorising power operations at the Christopher Crane Clean Energy Center in Pennsylvania.

A finding of no significant impact (FONSI) means the agencies concluded the proposed actions would not significantly affect the environment. That removes the need for a more detailed environmental impact statement. The assessment covers the federal actions related to restarting the plant, which is a reactor being brought back into service.

The decision is a regulatory step for the restart. The supplied reports do not say what approvals remain or when the plant might return to operation.

The American Nuclear Society's regulatory roundup also noted that the NRC has kept up a fast pace of decisions. Other developments it listed included Indiana becoming an NRC Agreement State, meaning it takes on regulatory authority over certain nuclear materials. It also said a new rulemaking tied to Executive Order 14300 had been proposed.
