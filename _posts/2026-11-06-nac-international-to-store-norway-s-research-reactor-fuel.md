NAC International of the United States has been contracted to deliver casks for dry storage of used nuclear fuel from Norway's former research reactors at Halden and Kjeller. Norway holds approximately 16.5 tonnes of spent fuel accumulated over six decades of research reactor operations. The inventory comprises diverse fuel types including metallic uranium, aluminium-clad assemblies, and experimental fuels, many of which cannot be safely stored long-term or disposed of in their current form without reprocessing or conditioning. Following an international competitive tender that yielded four qualified proposals, NAC's solution was selected. Implementation of improved storage and transport systems represents a concrete advance toward managing Norway's legacy fuel inventory, enabling facilities to transition from wet storage to more stable dry-cask configurations that extend storage intervals and reduce operational complexity.
