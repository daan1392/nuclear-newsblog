The Province of Ontario has announced contracts worth more than CAD3 billion (about USD2.1 billion) for the refurbishment of four units at Ontario Power Generation's Pickering plant, as site construction gets under way.

Pickering units 5 to 8, known as Pickering B, are to be taken out of service by the end of September. The refurbishment is due to begin officially in January 2027, subject to final regulatory approval. The work includes replacing 1,520 fuel channels and 48 boilers and building a 1.5-kilometre deep-water intake.

OPG describes it as Canada's largest clean energy infrastructure project. The refurbishment is expected to extend the operating lives of the four CANDU reactors by as much as 38 years and to support Ontario's nuclear supply chain. Ontario characterised the project as strengthening the province's economic sovereignty and energy security.

Refurbishing existing CANDU units is a major route to keeping large volumes of low-carbon generation available, alongside new build plans in the province.
