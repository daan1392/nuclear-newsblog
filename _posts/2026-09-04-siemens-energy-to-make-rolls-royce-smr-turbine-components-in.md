Siemens Energy will manufacture key steam turbine components for Rolls-Royce SMR's first three small modular reactors at its Newcastle facility in north-east England.

The parts include high-pressure turbines and valve casings and are intended for the Gwyndod project on Anglesey in North Wales. The announcement was made on Thursday at the CA Parsons Works site, with UK Energy Secretary Miatta Fahnbulleh and Business Secretary Jonathan Reynolds present.

The site has supplied UK energy infrastructure since 1889. It built the steam turbine for Calder Hall in West Cumbria, the world's first commercial nuclear power plant, which began generating in 1956. Rolls-Royce SMR selected Siemens Energy as its global turbine supplier in February 2025.

The decision ties part of the SMR supply chain to UK manufacturing, which is a priority for the government.
