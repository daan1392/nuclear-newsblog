Finland's Helen Ydinvoima Oy has launched an environmental impact assessment for the construction of a small modular reactor at one of three potential sites within Helsinki. The utility, which currently operates district heating and electricity generation plants across the city, is targeting carbon-neutral energy production by 2040.

The SMR program will evaluate proven designs capable of producing heat, electricity, or both. Helen began the first phase of planning in September 2024 and is now conducting the formal EIA process required under Finnish law before permitting and construction can proceed.

The project reflects growing Nordic interest in SMRs for urban heating and decarbonization. A small reactor sited near a population center could displace fossil fuels currently used for district heating while generating electricity. Finland's regulatory framework and existing nuclear expertise position the country as a test case for SMR deployment in northern Europe.
