The Government of Saskatchewan has published a plan that sets a goal of at least 2,600 MWe of nuclear generating capacity online in the province by 2050.

The plan is titled *Delivering Energy Security Through Nuclear Power*. It supplements the Saskatchewan First Energy Security Strategy and Supply Plan released in October 2025, and it sets out next steps toward a nuclear power programme. It has four priorities: building electricity capacity for long-term growth, bringing large reactors into supply planning, and continuing nuclear development in Estevan in the southeast of the province. The fourth priority was not included in the supplied material.

Saskatchewan has some of the world's largest and highest-grade uranium mines but no nuclear power reactors. It is working with Ontario, New Brunswick and Alberta on deploying small modular reactors (SMRs) under a joint strategic plan released in 2022. Utility SaskPower has also started a formal process to evaluate large reactor technologies.
