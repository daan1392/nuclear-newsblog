General Matter has submitted a licence application to the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) for a proposed commercial uranium enrichment facility in Paducah, Kentucky.

Enrichment is the step in the fuel cycle that raises the share of fissile uranium-235 in natural uranium so it can be used as reactor fuel. Building new enrichment capacity in the US is a stated priority for the nuclear industry, as advanced reactor developers and operators look for more domestic fuel supply options.

The filing starts the NRC's formal review of the project. Before the review can conclude, the regulator must decide whether the application is complete enough to examine and then assess it against its requirements.

The supplied report does not give technical details of the plant, its planned capacity or a schedule for review or construction.
