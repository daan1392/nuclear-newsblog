China General Nuclear announced the completion of fuel loading at San'ao Unit 2 in Zhejiang province on November 2, marking a major milestone in the preparation for reactor startup. Unit 2 is the second of six HPR1000 (Hualong One) reactors planned at the San'ao site as part of the facility's first phase development.

Fuel loading represents a critical transition point from construction and pre-operational testing toward active nuclear operation. The fuel assembly loading operation employed semi-automatic equipment, reflecting modern reactor construction practices that enhance precision and safety. CGN described the milestone as "official entry into a new stage of nuclear-powered operation and commissioning."

The HPR1000 is China's domestically developed pressurized water reactor design and has been offered internationally as a competitive new-build option. The San'ao project demonstrates sustained Chinese investment in nuclear baseload capacity expansion and represents another step toward full completion of the facility's initial 6-unit configuration.
