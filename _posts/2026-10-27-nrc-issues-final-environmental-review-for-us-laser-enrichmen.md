The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission has issued its Final Environmental Impact Statement for Global Laser Enrichment's proposed Paducah Laser Enrichment Facility in Kentucky, recommending that a construction and operation licence be issued. The facility would use laser enrichment technology developed by Australia's Silex Systems, which holds 51 percent ownership of GLE.

GLE submitted its Environmental Report to the NRC in December 2024. The Final Safety Evaluation Report—the last major regulatory milestone before a licensing decision—is expected by year-end 2026. The recommendation marks significant progress toward commercial deployment of laser enrichment in the United States, offering an alternative to centrifuge-based enrichment technology.

Laser enrichment uses selective photon ionization to separate uranium isotopes, potentially offering improved efficiency and reduced energy consumption compared to established methods. The Paducah facility would support domestic uranium enrichment capacity, contributing to fuel supply security for US reactors.
