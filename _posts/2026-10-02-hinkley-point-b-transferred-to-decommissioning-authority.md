Hinkley Point B nuclear power station in Somerset, England, has been transferred from EDF Energy to the Nuclear Decommissioning Authority for formal decommissioning. The two Advanced Gas-cooled Reactors (AGRs) permanently shut down in August 2022 after 46 years of operation, having generated over 311 terawatt-hours of electricity since first connecting to the grid in February 1976.

The transfer marks Hinkley Point B as the second AGR station to move into formal decommissioning ownership. Before the transfer, all nuclear fuel was systematically removed from the site, with the final shipment departing on 28 November 2025. More than 4,900 fuel elements were transported from the facility as part of this defueling campaign.

The shift of ownership and responsibility to the dedicated decommissioning authority enables systematic planning and execution of decommissioning activities according to the UK's long-term waste and site remediation strategy. The site will now enter the formal decommissioning phase, including facility decontamination, structural demolition, and preparation for eventual site release.
