The European Investment Bank announced support of up to €40 million for Steady Energy, a small modular reactor developer, in September 2026. The commitment, with an initial tranche of €30 million, represents the EIB's first dedicated financing for an SMR project.

The move signals the bank's strategic interest in next-generation nuclear technologies. Officials indicated the EIB is actively reviewing other SMR proposals alongside fourth-generation reactor concepts and fusion ventures, all subject to standard evaluation criteria.

This inaugural investment marks a broader pattern of institutional capital flowing toward innovative nuclear platforms. As SMR designs advance toward commercialization, access to development financing from established financial institutions becomes increasingly important for reducing technology risk and accelerating deployment timelines.
