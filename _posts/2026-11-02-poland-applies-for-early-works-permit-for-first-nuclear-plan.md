Polskie Elektrownie Jądrowe applied for an early works permit from the Pomeranian regional authority to begin preparatory construction at the Lubiatowo-Kopalino site in Choczewo, marking a procedural step toward Poland's first nuclear power plant. Early works encompass site preparation, infrastructure development, and other activities that establish conditions for the full construction phase to follow. The planned facility will comprise three Westinghouse AP1000 reactors. The early works permit is a distinct regulatory stage undertaken before the main construction licence is sought, allowing the project to prepare the site while formal licensing reviews continue.
