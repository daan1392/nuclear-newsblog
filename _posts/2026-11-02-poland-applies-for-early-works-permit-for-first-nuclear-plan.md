Polskie Elektrownie Jądrowe submitted an application to regional authorities for a permit to conduct early works at the Lubiatowo-Kopalino site in the Choczewo municipality, a crucial step toward Poland's inaugural nuclear power plant. Early works encompass site preparation and construction of necessary infrastructure to enable the main construction phase. The planned facility will comprise three Westinghouse AP1000 pressurized water reactors. These preparatory works, carried out under applicable regulations without requiring a separate nuclear operating license, establish the groundwork for subsequent construction authorization. The application represents Poland's advancement along its nuclear development pathway, aligned with the country's strategy to diversify electricity generation and reduce carbon emissions from the national grid.
