The U.S. Department of Energy announced a conditional loan commitment of up to $4.2 billion to support Vistra's planned uprates and modernisation at Beaver Valley in Pennsylvania and Davis-Besse and Perry in Ohio. The projects will add 433 MW of new generating capacity while preserving approximately 4 GW of reliable baseload power and extending the plants' operations for an additional 20 years beyond their existing licences.

The DOE emphasized that these upgrades will avoid the need for new transmission corridors or equivalent replacement generation resources. Vistra's investments are expected to support roughly 3,000 engineering, construction, and outage jobs, plus thousands of additional permanent positions across the three sites.

This financing underscores the Biden administration's commitment to sustaining existing nuclear capacity as a critical decarbonization strategy while simultaneously adding new generation from existing facilities—a lower-cost alternative to new reactor construction.
