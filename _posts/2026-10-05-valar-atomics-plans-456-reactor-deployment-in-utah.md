Reactor start-up Valar Atomics announced plans for Project Beehive, a large-scale deployment of 456 of its microreactors on more than 9,000 acres of Bureau of Land Management land in Utah.

The facility would integrate co-located data centres and a fuel production facility, creating a multi-purpose nuclear energy and industrial heat complex. The scale of the planned deployment—hundreds of small modular reactors in a single campus—represents an ambitious vision for distributed microreactor technology and its potential for industrial applications.

Microreactors, typically rated under 20 MW per unit, are a developing technology category targeting remote locations, industrial heat demand, and specialized applications where conventional utility-scale reactors are impractical. Valar's proposal to concentrate hundreds of units suggests confidence in factory-production and economies of scale for these designs.

The project remains in early stages and will require permitting, regulatory approval, and financing. It represents the growing private sector effort to commercialize non-traditional reactor concepts.
