China General Nuclear (CGN) announced on November 2 that fuel loading into the core of unit 2 at the San'ao nuclear power plant in Zhejiang province has been completed. The milestone marks the second HPR1000 (Hualong One) reactor entering active commissioning at the site, where six units are planned as part of the first phase.

Fuel loading represents a critical transition point in reactor startup, moving the unit from the construction and equipment installation phase into the operational commissioning phase. CGN noted that the operation employed semi-automatic equipment, reflecting modern practices for precision and efficiency.

The Hualong One is CGN's domestically-developed large light-water reactor design with significant technological advancement features. The San'ao site development demonstrates China's capacity to execute multi-unit projects on schedule and represents an important data point for the global fleet expansion.
