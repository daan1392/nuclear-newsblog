Wyoming has joined the federal Fixing America's Surface Transportation (FAST) Act Title 41 permitting program, aimed at accelerating approval timelines for major infrastructure projects. On October 26, Governor Mark Gordon and Emily Domenich, executive director of the Federal Permitting Improvement Steering Council, signed a memorandum of understanding to align Wyoming's permitting processes with FAST-41 requirements.

The agreement covers multiple infrastructure categories including uranium mining projects, which are critical to the domestic fuel cycle. FAST-41 establishes coordinated federal and state review procedures designed to reduce permitting delays without compromising environmental or safety review quality. Wyoming's participation strengthens procedural certainty for uranium companies operating or planning operations in the state.

As global nuclear capacity additions accelerate, supply chain constraints—including uranium production capacity—have become a strategic concern. Streamlining permitting can help domestic producers respond faster to increasing demand.
