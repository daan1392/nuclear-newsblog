The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission approved a construction permit for a proposed small modular reactor plant at Dow's Seadrift site in Texas, featuring four Xe-100 units. This marks the first construction permit for a high-temperature gas-cooled reactor issued in the United States in nearly 60 years, representing a significant regulatory milestone for advanced reactor technology.

The Xe-100 is a pebble-bed HTGR design that operates at higher temperatures than conventional light-water reactors, offering potential benefits for industrial process heat and electricity generation. The approval reflects the NRC's progress in evaluating novel reactor designs and the growing commercial interest in small modular reactors for diverse applications beyond traditional utility-scale power generation.
