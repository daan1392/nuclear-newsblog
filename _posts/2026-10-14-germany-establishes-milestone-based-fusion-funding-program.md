Germany's Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation (SPRIND) has established a new milestone-based fusion program on behalf of the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology, and Space as part of the government's High-Tech Agenda Germany (HTAD). The initiative aims to accelerate fusion technology development through competitive financial support for private companies, particularly startups.

The program represents a strategic shift toward using technology-specific roadmaps and flagship initiatives to strengthen Germany's innovation capabilities and translate research into practical applications. By tying funding to measurable milestones, the government seeks to reduce risk and encourage rapid progress in a field where numerous ventures globally are pursuing commercial fusion demonstration within the next decade.

The move reflects growing international competition in fusion, with private companies in the United States, Europe and elsewhere seeking to demonstrate fusion viability ahead of public programs like ITER. Germany's approach emphasizes performance-based funding rather than baseline support, creating incentives for disciplined technical progress.
