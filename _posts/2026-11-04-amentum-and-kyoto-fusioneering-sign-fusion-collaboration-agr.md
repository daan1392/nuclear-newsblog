Kyoto Fusioneering and Amentum signed a memorandum of understanding covering international fusion energy cooperation spanning the UK, Japan, and USA. The agreement seeks to advance fusion power plant commercialisation by combining complementary capabilities: Amentum's fusion engineering, systems integration, nuclear safety, and programme delivery expertise with Kyoto Fusioneering's work on fuel cycle systems, breeding blankets, and plasma heating technologies.

The two companies intend to pursue joint opportunities within major public and private fusion programmes, integrating their respective technical strengths to address engineering challenges in fusion plant design and commercialization. The partnership reflects the international nature of modern fusion development, with Japanese, UK, and US collaboration addressing the complex systems engineering required for commercial fusion power.

This type of strategic partnership between established engineering firms and specialized fusion technology developers is becoming increasingly common as the fusion sector matures toward demonstration plants.
