Rosatom, Russia's state nuclear corporation, signed an agreement during Russian Energy Week to supply electricity via floating nuclear power plants to the northern part of Halmahera Island in East Indonesia. Two FPU-100 floating power units with a combined capacity of 200 MW will serve the region under the agreement with PT Pembangkit Listrik Nuklir, an Indonesian power generation company.

Floating nuclear power units are designed to serve remote or island locations lacking developed grid infrastructure and onshore construction facilities. Russia has operated pilot floating nuclear plants and is commercializing the technology for export markets. Rosatom anticipates the Halmahera units will begin supplying electricity to consumers within five years of deployment.

The agreement represents expansion of nuclear technology into Southeast Asian markets and reflects Indonesia's openness to diversified energy solutions. Floating units offer advantages for archipelagic regions with limited land availability for conventional nuclear facilities and distributed electricity demand.
