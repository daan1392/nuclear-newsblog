Installation of the 480 calandria tubes at Bruce unit 4 has been completed faster than at Bruce unit 3, as the Major Component Replacement (MCR) project continues to set records for CANDU reactor refurbishment. Bruce unit 4 is the third of six units at the Ontario site to undergo MCR.

The calandria tube is a key CANDU component made of Zircaloy (a zirconium alloy) that forms the outer part of fuel channels. The MCR process involves removing and replacing major reactor components—a multi-year, multi-billion dollar undertaking that extends a reactor's operating life by 30–35 years.
