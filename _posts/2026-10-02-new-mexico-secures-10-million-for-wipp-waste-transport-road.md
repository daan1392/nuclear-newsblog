New Mexico's congressional delegation announced $10 million in federal funding for repairing roads used to transport radioactive waste to the Waste Isolation Pilot Plant near Carlsbad. The appropriation, secured in the fiscal year 2026 budget cycle, marks the first dedicated WIPP road maintenance funding awarded since 2014.

The Waste Isolation Pilot Plant is the U.S. Department of Energy's deep geological repository for transuranic radioactive waste generated by defense and research programmes. Maintaining transport infrastructure in good condition is essential for safe, reliable waste shipments to the facility, which operates in bedded salt formations approximately 2,150 feet below the surface. Road degradation from heavy waste transport vehicles poses safety and operational risks.

The funding, announced by Senators Ben Ray Luján and Martin Heinrich and Representative Gabe Vasquez, underscores the ongoing infrastructure needs associated with long-term waste management operations. Sustained road maintenance is a practical requirement for reliable waste disposal operations at the national scale.
