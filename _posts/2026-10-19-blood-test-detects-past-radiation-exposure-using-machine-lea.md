Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory developed a machine learning tool capable of detecting and estimating past radiation exposure using data from a complete blood count (CBC), one of the most routinely performed clinical blood tests.

The breakthrough addresses a critical gap in radiation emergency response. Rapid assessment of exposure levels enables appropriate medical triage and treatment decisions. Traditional biodosimetry methods require specialized equipment and expertise; leveraging existing laboratory infrastructure makes exposure detection more accessible.

The approach has applications ranging from occupational monitoring of radiation workers to emergency response following accidental or deliberate radiological incidents. By converting standard clinical hematology data into exposure estimates, the tool could integrate radiation dosimetry into routine medical practice where timely detection matters most.
