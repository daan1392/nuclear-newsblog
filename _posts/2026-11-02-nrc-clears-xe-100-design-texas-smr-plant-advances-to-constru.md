The Nuclear Regulatory Commission issued its Final Safety Evaluation Report for the Xe-100 reactor proposed for deployment at Long Mott Generating Station in Texas, concluding that the design meets NRC safety standards and is suitable for licensing. The comprehensive review examined the plant's design criteria, preliminary design, safety analyses, probabilistic risk assessment, and supporting research and development programs.

The Xe-100 is an advanced high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR) design, a technology favored for applications requiring process heat or high-temperature steam output alongside electricity generation. The Texas deployment represents one of the near-term commercial SMR projects in the United States. Completion of the NRC safety review opens the path to issuance of a construction permit, marking a significant milestone in the commercialization of HTGR technology.

The project reflects growing investor and utility interest in siting SMRs at existing fossil fuel plants to accelerate decarbonization while leveraging existing grid connections and workforce infrastructure.
