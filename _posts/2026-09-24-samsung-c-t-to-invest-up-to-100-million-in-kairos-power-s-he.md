Kairos Power and Samsung C&T have signed a binding term sheet under which the South Korean engineering and construction firm will invest in Kairos and collaborate on deploying its reactor technology.

The terms provide for Samsung C&T to contribute up to $100 million through a mix of direct investment and in-kind engineering services for the Hermes 2 demonstration plant. Samsung C&T has also joined Kairos's engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) team, supporting delivery of Hermes 2 and future deployments.

Kairos is developing a fluoride salt-cooled, high-temperature reactor, which uses molten fluoride salt rather than water as coolant and operates at low pressure.

The agreement adds an established construction partner to the company's demonstration effort, with an eye to commercial rollout. Samsung C&T was also among the signatories of a separate BWRX-300 memorandum of understanding announced the same week.
