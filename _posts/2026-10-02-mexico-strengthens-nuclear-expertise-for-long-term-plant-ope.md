The International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) conducted a specialized training course at Mexico's Laguna Verde nuclear power plant, bringing together 30 national nuclear professionals for instruction tailored to the facility's specific operational needs. The training is part of international efforts to build and sustain technical expertise in nuclear operations.

Laguna Verde, Mexico's sole commercial nuclear power plant, has been operating since the 1980s and continues to generate significant baseload electricity for the country. Maintaining a skilled workforce across technical, operational, and managerial roles is essential for safe, efficient long-term plant operation. The IAEA assistance programme strengthens Mexico's capacity to manage the facility reliably and adhere to international safety standards.

Such capacity-building activities support the sustainability of nuclear programmes globally, particularly in countries where domestic technical expertise continues to develop. The IAEA regularly assists member states through training, peer reviews, and advisory missions to ensure nuclear facilities operate to the highest safety and security standards.
