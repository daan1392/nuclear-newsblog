The Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection (MassDEP) dismissed Holtec International's appeal against its refusal to allow treated water from the Pilgrim nuclear plant to be released into Cape Cod Bay.

MassDEP first denied Holtec's application in 2024. Holtec appealed, and the department announced the dismissal on Tuesday. The decision leaves the company, for now, with fewer ways to deal with as much as 1.1 million gallons of water that it wants to discharge as decommissioning of the shuttered plant continues.

The disposal of water from decommissioned plants has been contentious in several places, and the Pilgrim case shows that state-level environmental permitting can constrain options even when the federal nuclear regulator is not the deciding body. The summary did not say what alternatives Holtec would pursue.
