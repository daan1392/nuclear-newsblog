A consortium of Polish entrepreneurs has invested PLN500 million (USD128 million) in SGE's strategy to deploy fleets of small modular reactors in Poland, the UK and other European countries. The funding underscores growing private sector confidence in commercial SMR deployment on the continent.

SGE, formerly Synthos Green Energy, recently signed a cooperation agreement with GE Vernova, Hitachi and Samsung C&T to advance the BWRX-300, a boiling water reactor design rated at 300 megawatts thermal. The company is a co-investor in the reactor's development and holds multiple project prospects across Europe, with particular focus on Poland's energy transition goals.

The timing reflects momentum in the global SMR market. GE Vernova's BWRX-300 received its first US construction permit earlier this week for a deployment in Canada, marking a significant regulatory milestone for the design. This Polish investment signals that European developers and investors view SMRs as increasingly viable technology for near-term deployment, particularly in markets seeking to decarbonize their grids while maintaining energy independence.
