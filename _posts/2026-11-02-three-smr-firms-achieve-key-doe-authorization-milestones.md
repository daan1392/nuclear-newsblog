Three developers from the Department of Energy's Reactor Pilot Program reached significant authorization milestones in October as they advance toward operational licensing. Last Energy and Radiant each earned documented safety analyses (DSAs) for their first reactor designs, while Antares secured a nuclear safety design agreement (NSDA) for its second reactor.

These authorizations represent formal DOE recognition that the companies have adequately addressed fundamental safety questions and design feasibility. A documented safety analysis provides detailed analysis of how a reactor design protects public health and safety, while a safety design agreement confirms alignment between the developer and DOE on key safety principles before a detailed design is finalized.

The milestones underscore progress within the U.S. advanced reactor demonstration pipeline, where multiple private companies are working toward operational licenses under both existing and new regulatory pathways.
