Nuclear Restoration Services completed remediation works at historic liquid effluent treatment facilities at the Harwell nuclear research site in Oxfordshire, restoring approximately 4.2 hectares of land. The project represents a major milestone in Harwell's ongoing decommissioning programme.

Established in 1946 as Britain's first Atomic Energy Research Establishment, Harwell housed five research reactors of various designs alongside plutonium handling facilities, radioactive laboratories, and waste treatment and storage infrastructure. The site is now progressing through systematic decommissioning to return land to productive use.

Completion of the liquid effluent treatment facility remediation demonstrates progress in site cleanup efforts and reflects advances in decontamination and remediation techniques. The recovered land becomes available for future redevelopment as Harwell transforms from an active research complex into a decommissioned industrial site.
