Belgium's government, energy company Engie, and its subsidiary Electrabel have agreed to extend negotiations on the potential state acquisition of Belgium's seven nuclear power reactors to December 12. The delay pushes back the original target date for concluding heads of terms on the proposed transaction.

Engie and the Belgian government began negotiations in late April 2026 following Brussels' reversal of a previous decision to shut down the nuclear fleet by 2025. The Letter of Intent covering the transaction encompasses the full scope of nuclear activities currently operated by Engie and Electrabel, including the complete reactor fleet, personnel, and nuclear subsidiaries.

State acquisition of Belgium's reactors would represent a major ownership restructuring and reflects the government's commitment to maintaining nuclear power as part of its energy mix. The extended timeline suggests the complexity of financial and operational arrangements required to transfer ownership and management of an existing operating fleet.
