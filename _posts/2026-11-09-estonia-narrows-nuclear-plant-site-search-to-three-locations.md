Estonia progressed its plan to develop a nuclear power plant by narrowing the search to three candidate sites, all located in Viru-Nigula municipality roughly 120 kilometres east of Tallinn. Three previously considered locations in Lüganuse municipality were eliminated on security grounds.

The selection process reflects Estonia's strategy to diversify and decarbonise its energy supply while enhancing energy independence. The focus on sites within a single municipality may streamline regulatory review and community engagement for the next phases of development.

Estonia, like other Baltic states, has historically relied on hydroelectric imports and fossil fuels. A nuclear power plant would represent a significant shift in the country's energy infrastructure and support climate objectives while providing stable baseload generation.
