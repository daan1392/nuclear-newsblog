Estonia has progressed its search for a location for its first nuclear power plant by narrowing candidates to three sites within the Viru-Nigula municipality, approximately 120 kilometres east of Tallinn. Three previously considered locations in Lüganuse municipality were excluded from further consideration due to security concerns. The site selection process represents a significant policy step for Estonia, which has renewed interest in nuclear energy as a stable, low-carbon baseload source to support its energy independence and climate goals. The identification of viable candidate locations allows Estonia to move toward more detailed assessment and potential early-stage development planning.
