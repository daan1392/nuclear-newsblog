Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL) and Gujarat State Electricity Corporation Limited formalized collaboration on nuclear expansion by signing a memorandum of understanding to explore suitable locations for new nuclear power plants in Gujarat state.

The agreement facilitates systematic site identification and technical assessment, with emphasis on information sharing between the two organisations. NPCIL characterised the MoU as "a significant step towards exploring new horizons for nuclear power development in Gujarat," signalling the state's role in India's longer-term decarbonisation strategy.

Gujarat is home to existing nuclear capacity and has experience hosting fuel cycle facilities, making it a logical choice for new plant development. The move reflects India's continued commitment to nuclear power as a foundation of its clean energy transition and carbon reduction goals. NPCIL has been progressively expanding India's nuclear footprint, and site identification in promising regions is typically the first formal step toward new-build projects.
