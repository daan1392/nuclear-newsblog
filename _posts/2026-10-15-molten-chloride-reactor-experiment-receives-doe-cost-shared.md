The U.S. Department of Energy has approved cost-shared funding through its Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program (ARDP) for the Molten Chloride Reactor Experiment (MCRE), enabling an accelerated final stage toward first criticality. Southern Company and Core Power, in collaboration with Idaho National Laboratory, are developing the experiment, which could achieve criticality as early as 2027—potentially marking the world's first fast-spectrum molten chloride salt reactor to reach that milestone.

The accelerated funding is intended to advance scientific understanding of molten salt reactor technology and generate data on fuel behaviour, reactor operation, and performance. Such experimental results would provide the scientific and engineering foundation needed to support future commercial reactor designs based on the molten chloride concept.

Molten salt reactors represent an alternative approach to conventional light-water designs, offering potential benefits including higher thermal efficiency and reduced proliferation concerns. The MCRE's progress reflects growing U.S. commitment to demonstrating diverse advanced reactor technologies.
