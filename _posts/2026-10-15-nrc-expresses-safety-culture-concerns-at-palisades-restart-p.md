The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission identified potential concerns with the safety-conscious work environment (SCWE) at the Palisades nuclear power plant in Covert, Michigan, during a July inspection. Employees in supply chain and quality assurance departments may be hesitant to raise legitimate safety concerns through available channels due to fear of retaliation, according to the NRC's published findings.

Palisades has been shut down and is seeking to become the first U.S. nuclear power plant to restart after permanent closure. The facility's restart preparation is contingent on demonstrating full compliance with NRC safety and security standards, including maintenance of a healthy safety culture where workers feel empowered to identify and report problems without fear of adverse consequences.

The NRC sent its inspection report and follow-up letter to Holtec International, which acquired and is preparing Palisades for restart, on October 13. Addressing safety culture deficiencies is a prerequisite for restart authorisation and reflects the regulator's emphasis on human factors and organisational effectiveness in nuclear operations.
