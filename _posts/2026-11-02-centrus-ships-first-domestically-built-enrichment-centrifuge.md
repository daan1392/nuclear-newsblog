Centrus Energy Corp. celebrated on 29 October the completion and shipment of the first centrifuge manufactured at its Technology and Manufacturing Center in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, destined for its enrichment facility in Piketon, Ohio. The milestone demonstrates the company's ability to build advanced centrifuge technology domestically at scale, addressing long-standing US reliance on foreign enrichment capacity. Centrus president and CEO Amir Vexler noted that the achievement connects American manufacturing and uranium enrichment infrastructure, establishing a domestic supply chain critical to national fuel security. The shipment represents tangible progress in the company's expansion of the Piketon facility and aligns with US policy goals to strengthen domestic nuclear fuel production capabilities.
