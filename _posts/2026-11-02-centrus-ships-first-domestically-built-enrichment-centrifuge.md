Centrus Energy marked a milestone in October by completing and shipping the first centrifuge built at its Technology and Manufacturing Center in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, to its uranium enrichment facility in Piketon, Ohio. The shipment symbolizes the reestablishment of domestic production capacity for this critical fuel-cycle technology.

Centrus President and CEO Amir Vexler highlighted the significance at an Oak Ridge press event, noting that the achievement "demonstrates that Centrus has assembled the specialized people capability and domestic supply chain to build this technology at scale." The company is expanding its Piketon plant's enrichment capacity as part of efforts to strengthen U.S. fuel supply security.

This development supports government objectives to reduce dependence on foreign enrichment services while meeting growing demand from both commercial reactors and new advanced reactor deployments.
