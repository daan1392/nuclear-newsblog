Swedish nuclear technical services firm Studsvik is providing technical guidance and design specifications to Japanese nuclear engineering start-up ReNu Industries for a Japanese version of inDRUM, Studsvik's patented technology for treating problematic radioactive waste.

InDRUM is designed to handle waste streams that are difficult to process through conventional routes, including certain forms of contaminated materials and residues. The technology's deployment in Japan reflects ongoing efforts to manage and reduce the inventory of waste from both operational reactors and the Fukushima Daiichi accident site.

This collaboration demonstrates the international nature of waste management solutions and the role of specialised vendors in providing technical expertise for waste treatment challenges. As Japan continues decommissioning and decontamination efforts at Fukushima and other sites, access to proven waste treatment methods becomes increasingly important for managing environmental liabilities and site remediation.
