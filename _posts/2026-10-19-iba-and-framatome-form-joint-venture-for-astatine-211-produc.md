Framatome and Ion Beam Applications announced formation of Ekalia, a jointly owned company to establish an industrial-scale production network for astatine-211, an alpha-emitting isotope used in targeted cancer radiotherapy.

The first production site will be built in Nantes, France, with Ekalia investing €30 million (approximately $33.6 million). The permit application has been submitted, construction is expected to begin in early 2027, and operations are targeted for 2028. A second pilot production site is planned in the United States, though location and timeline remain to be confirmed.

Astatine-211 offers potential advantages for radioimmunotherapy applications due to its alpha-particle emission characteristics. Establishing European and North American production capacity aims to serve growing clinical demand for novel radiopharmaceuticals and reduce dependence on centralized production facilities.
