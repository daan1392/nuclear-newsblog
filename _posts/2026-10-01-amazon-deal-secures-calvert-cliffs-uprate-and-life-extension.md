Constellation Energy and Amazon have agreed a 20-year power purchase agreement that will support a 190 MW capacity uprate at Calvert Cliffs nuclear station in Maryland and secure the plant's relicensing for an additional two decades. The deal, backed by USD 3 billion in investment, will boost the facility's output from 1,790 MW to 1,980 MW between 2030 and 2032. Long-term revenue certainty from Amazon's commitment provides Constellation with the financial foundation to pursue regulatory relicensing and advance development of new clean energy generation capacity. The agreement demonstrates renewed economic viability for existing nuclear plants through direct corporate offtake agreements with large industrial and technology consumers seeking reliable decarbonized power.
