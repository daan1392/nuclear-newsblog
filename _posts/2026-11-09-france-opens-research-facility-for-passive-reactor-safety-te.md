France's nuclear safety regulator ASNR (Autorité de Sûreté Nucléaire et de Radioprotection) opened new research infrastructure at its Cadarache site dedicated to testing and validating the reliability of passive safety systems. The facility supports independent safety assessment of innovative reactor designs, including small modular reactors and other advanced concepts.

Passive safety systems operate without active intervention or external power, using natural physical principles such as convection and gravity to protect reactor cores during accidents. Demonstrating their effectiveness is essential for regulatory approval of novel reactor technologies. The facility represents France's investment in regulatory science supporting the national fleet modernization program and emerging reactor technologies.
