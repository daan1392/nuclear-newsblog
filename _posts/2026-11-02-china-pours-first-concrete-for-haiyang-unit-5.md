Construction began on unit 5 at the Haiyang nuclear power plant in Shandong province when the first safety-related concrete was poured on 31 October. The unit is the fifth of six planned pressurised water reactors at the site, forming the third phase of expansion at Haiyang. The work is being conducted by China Nuclear Industry 24 Construction Co Ltd under contract to State Power Investment Corporation, which is managing the project. This milestone marks the official entry into the main construction phase for unit 5, as the site continues a significant expansion programme to add three gigawatts of new capacity alongside the three units already in operation.
