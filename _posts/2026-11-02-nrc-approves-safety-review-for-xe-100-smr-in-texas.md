The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission released its Final Safety Evaluation Report on 30 October for the Xe-100 small modular reactor proposed for the Long Mott Generating Station in Texas, concluding the agency's comprehensive review of the reactor's design and safety. The NRC examined principal design criteria, preliminary design specifications, safety analyses, probabilistic risk assessment, and research and development programmes before reaching its affirmative conclusion. The completed safety review opens the path to a construction permit announcement, marking significant regulatory progress for the Xe-100 design and the broader SMR deployment effort in the United States.
