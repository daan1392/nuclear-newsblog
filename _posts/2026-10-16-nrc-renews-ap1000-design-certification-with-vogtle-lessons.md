The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission approved Westinghouse's renewal and update of the standard design certification for the AP1000 reactor. The approval incorporates operational lessons learned from the construction of the Vogtle-3 and Vogtle-4 units in Georgia, establishing Vogtle-4 as the reference plant for future AP1000 deployments in the United States.

Design certification renewals provide updated technical specifications and licensing frameworks that allow manufacturers to streamline future reactor construction and deployment. The inclusion of Vogtle's construction experience is expected to improve project scheduling and efficiency for subsequent AP1000 builds.

The AP1000 is a 1,100 MW passive safety light-water reactor and a central component of US strategy to expand nuclear capacity. The updated certification should facilitate more rapid and cost-effective deployment of the design across multiple sites domestically and internationally.
