The ninth and final vacuum vessel sector (sector #2) for the ITER fusion machine was delivered to the project's site at Cadarache in southern France, marking a major milestone in the international collaboration. With this delivery, all principal components required for core machine assembly are now on-site, including the complete vacuum vessel, superconducting magnets, and cryostat.

The vacuum vessel is among ITER's largest and most complex components, and its segments were manufactured and delivered across a multi-year campaign by an international supply chain. Completing all major component deliveries represents significant progress toward the next assembly phase, though substantial integration and testing remain ahead.

ITER's construction has faced repeated delays and cost overruns, but component delivery milestones underscore continued progress toward demonstrating sustained fusion energy production on an unprecedented scale.
