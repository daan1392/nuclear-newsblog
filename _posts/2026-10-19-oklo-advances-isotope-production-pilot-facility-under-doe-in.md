Oklo announced plans to advance development of the Groves-2 Forge isotope production and processing pilot facility, building on the earlier achievement of its Groves Isotope Test Reactor reaching criticality. The company is participating in the Nuclear Energy Launch Pad initiative operated by the Idaho National Laboratory-based National Reactor Innovation Center.

The Launch Pad initiative, which expands upon the Department of Energy's Nuclear Reactor Pilot Program, offers a streamlined approval pathway for novel reactor designs and fuel cycle developments. Oklo was one of twelve companies selected in August to participate in the program, which aims to accelerate deployment of advanced nuclear technologies.

Oklo's participation reflects its strategy to integrate reactor operation with downstream fuel cycle capabilities. The Groves-2 Forge facility represents a step toward commercial-scale isotope production using heat and neutrons from research and production reactors. This integrated approach addresses growing demand for medical and industrial isotopes while demonstrating the practical value of small reactor deployments beyond electricity generation.
