China National Nuclear Corporation has announced the pouring of the first batch of concrete for the nuclear island of the Jiangsu Xuwei Nuclear Heating and Power Plant, a high-temperature gas-cooled reactor project. The milestone marks the transition from preparatory work to active construction of the reactor structure.

The HTGR design offers features including inherent safety, higher thermal efficiency, and potential for process heat applications beyond electricity generation. The Xuwei facility represents the initial phase of deployment, with plans for both electricity and district heating output.

China's acceleration of diverse reactor types—including HPR1000 pressurized water reactors, HTGRs, and experimental fast reactors—reflects a comprehensive approach to nuclear capacity expansion. Deployment of multiple technology pathways allows China to pursue different use cases while developing supply chains and regulatory experience across reactor types.
