Japanese engineering firm IHI Corporation has been contracted by Westinghouse to provide engineering and procurement support for long-lead components destined for Poland's first commercial nuclear power plant. The order covers fabrication and delivery of a reactor containment vessel and an associated large steel module for the first unit at the facility. IHI's Yokkaichi plant specializes in manufacturing heavy components for nuclear applications. The award demonstrates continued international supplier involvement in Poland's planned new-build program, which aims to diversify the country's energy generation and reduce reliance on fossil fuels. Component manufacturing often represents the longest lead time in new reactor construction, making early procurement critical for meeting project schedules.
