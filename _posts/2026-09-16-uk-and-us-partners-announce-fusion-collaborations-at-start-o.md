Three US–UK partnerships aimed at speeding the commercialisation of fusion energy were announced at the start of UK Fusion Week.

The United Kingdom Atomic Energy Authority (UKAEA) and the US Department of Energy's Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL) signed a joint declaration of intent at the Global Fusion Policy Summit in London on Monday. It would link UKAEA's SUNRISE AI supercomputer with PPPL's STELLAR-AI platform in a partnership called the SUNRISE–STELLAR-AI Federation. The aim is to use artificial intelligence to accelerate fusion development. SUNRISE is described as the UK's first AI supercomputer dedicated to fusion.

In another announcement, Tennessee and the UK said they were strengthening their partnership to explore cooperation on research, supply chains, regulation and other activities supporting fusion.

The agreements are at an early stage. The supercomputer link, for example, is a declaration of intent to explore collaboration rather than a finished integration.
