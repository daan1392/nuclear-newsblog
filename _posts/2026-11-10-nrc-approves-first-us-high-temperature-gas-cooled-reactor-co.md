The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission approved a construction permit for Dow's Long Mott Generating Station in Seadrift, Texas, which will feature four X-energy Xe-100 small modular reactors totalling 320 megawatts electric. This marks the first construction permit issued in the United States for a high-temperature gas-cooled reactor in nearly six decades, representing a major milestone for advanced reactor development.

The approval came earlier than expected and reflects recent Congressional reforms aimed at streamlining the licensing process. X-energy CEO noted that the achievement represents "important" progress for the technology, which benefits from the thorough work of both regulators and the project teams. The Seadrift site, which is part of Dow's UCC manufacturing operations, offers industrial process heat applications in addition to electricity generation.

The Xe-100 is a modular design with passive safety features, intended to support industrial decarbonization and reliable power generation. The permit approval signals growing regulatory confidence in advanced reactor designs and their potential role in meeting future energy demands.
