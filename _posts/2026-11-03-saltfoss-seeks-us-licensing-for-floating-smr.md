Saltfoss (formerly Seaborg), developer of a molten salt power barge small modular reactor, notified the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission of its intent to begin pre-application engagement and plans to submit a Regulatory Engagement Plan in the first quarter of 2027. The Danish company believes it will be the first advanced reactor developer licensed under Part 53 of the NRC's new risk-informed, performance-based regulatory framework for advanced reactors, and the first to license an advanced maritime SMR anywhere. Part 53, issued in March, establishes an alternative pathway for licensing and regulating commercial advanced reactors. Saltfoss is expanding US operations while pursuing this first-of-a-kind licensing engagement.
