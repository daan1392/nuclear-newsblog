Tianwan Nuclear Power Plant's unit 7 achieved grid connection on 8 October after successfully achieving a self-sustaining controlled nuclear chain reaction and reaching minimum controllable power level. The VVER-1200 reactor, developed jointly by Russia and China for the Sino-Russian Tianwan project, represents continuation of collaboration on large water-cooled reactor technology.

Prior to grid connection, specialists completed critical verification steps including reactor protection logic testing, reactor criticality confirmation, turbine start-up, and generator short-circuit tests. Unit 7 will now enter a series of additional tests and power ascension activities to reach full operational capacity.

The Tianwan project demonstrates sustained international partnership in nuclear technology and construction. Successful operation of VVER-1200 units adds to the global fleet of large water-cooled reactors and contributes to China's expanding low-carbon generation capacity.
