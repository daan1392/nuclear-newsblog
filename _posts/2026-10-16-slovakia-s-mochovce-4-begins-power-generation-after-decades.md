Slovakia's Mochovce Unit 4, a 471 MWe VVER-440 reactor, was connected to the national electricity grid on October 15, starting to supply power at 20% output. The connection marks a milestone for a project that has spanned multiple generations, with construction beginning decades earlier.

Mochovce 4 represents approximately 13% of Slovakia's current total electricity consumption and expands the country's low-carbon generation capacity. The phased connection procedure, which began with the reactor at reduced power, is standard practice for newly commissioned units entering commercial operation.

The completion of Mochovce 4 comes amid renewed focus on nuclear power expansion in Central Europe as electricity demand grows and decarbonization targets accelerate. Slovakia now operates four nuclear units across two sites, reinforcing its reliance on nuclear energy for baseload power generation.
