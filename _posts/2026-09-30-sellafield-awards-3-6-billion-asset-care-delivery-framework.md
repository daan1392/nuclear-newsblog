Sellafield Ltd, owned by the UK Nuclear Decommissioning Authority, announced the successful bidders for its Project and Asset Care Execution (PACE) delivery framework in September 2026. The framework, valued at up to £3.6 billion (approximately USD 4.8 billion), consolidates asset care and asset creation activities across the sprawling Cumbrian site in northwest England.

PACE succeeds Sellafield's Integrated Asset Care framework and Design Service Alliance, streamlining support for maintenance, modification, and development work on operational nuclear facilities. The new structure is designed to improve cost efficiency and value-for-money outcomes across the site's complex portfolio.

Sellafield, home to legacy fuel storage, reprocessing, and decommissioning operations, requires sustained technical support and infrastructure investment. Consolidating procurement under a single, long-term delivery framework aims to enhance planning certainty and operational continuity.
