Fusion for Energy, collaborating with Ansaldo Nucleare, Westinghouse, and Walter Tosto, completed manufacturing of Europe's final vacuum vessel sector for the International Thermonuclear Experimental Reactor (ITER) at Cadarache in southeastern France.

The ITER vacuum vessel comprises nine wedge-shaped steel sectors, each exceeding 14 metres in height and weighing 440 tonnes. The completed vessel will have an interior volume of 1,400 cubic metres and serves as the plasma chamber housing fusion reactions and a primary safety containment barrier.

Completion of Europe's manufacturing contributions is a significant step toward ITER assembly. The project represents a landmark international collaboration in fusion research, pooling engineering expertise and industrial capacity across multiple nations to construct the world's largest experimental fusion reactor.

While manufacturing progress is advancing, assembly and commissioning timelines continue to extend. The delivery of all nine sectors brings the project closer to integrated testing of this unprecedented fusion device.
