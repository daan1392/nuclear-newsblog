The Nuclear Company unveiled plans for Prosperity One, a proposed nuclear campus in Lewis County, Kentucky that would feature up to six Westinghouse AP1000 reactors with a combined capacity of 6.6 GW. If fully constructed, the facility would exceed the capacity of any existing nuclear power plant in the United States and mark the first nuclear generation site in Kentucky.

The project, to be sited along the Ohio River, has secured land control and is pursuing federal approvals with a target construction start in 2028. Initial work will include site characterization, geotechnical investigations, and environmental assessments. The venture-backed developer has partnered with local landowner Sam Howard and community leaders to advance the proposal.

The announcement reflects growing developer confidence in AP1000 deployment for new large-scale capacity, following the technology's deployment at Vogtle in Georgia. Kentucky's selection highlights how states without existing nuclear fleets are reconsidering the technology as a means to meet electricity demand and industrial decarbonization goals. Regulatory pathways for multi-unit sites remain complex, but successful licensing of similar proposals would significantly expand the pipeline of new U.S. nuclear generation.
