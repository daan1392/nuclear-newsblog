Urenco USA's National Enrichment Facility in New Mexico has begun production at its sixth new uranium enrichment cascade, achieving 75% completion of a significant capacity expansion programme. The company is installing 700,000 separative work units (SWU) of new enrichment capacity across eight centrifuge cascades between 2025 and 2027, all housed in an existing building at the site.

The milestone brings the company closer to its goal of expanding domestic enrichment production capacity to over 7 million SWU across the next decade. Urenco USA expects to bring the seventh cascade online later in 2026 and complete installation of the final cascade in spring 2027. The company also plans to begin additional construction work in 2027 to further boost capacity at the facility.

This expansion supports growing demand for enriched uranium as new reactor construction accelerates globally. The phased installation approach using existing infrastructure allows Urenco to expand production efficiently while maintaining continuous operations.
