Representatives Zoe Lofgren (D-California) and Jay Obernolte (R-California) introduced the American Leadership in Fusion Act in September 2026. The bipartisan bill proposes $10 billion in direct federal investment earmarked for fusion facilities, research programs, and commercialization activities.

If enacted, the legislation would establish an Office of Fusion within the Department of Energy and mandate a workforce development program led by the National Science Foundation. These structures aim to translate research advances into practical deployment and build the skilled labor base required for a growing fusion sector.

The proposal follows recommendations from the Special Competitive Studies Project's Commission on the Scaling of Fusion Energy. By coupling substantial capital investment with institutional coordination and human capital development, the bill addresses multiple barriers to moving fusion technologies from laboratory demonstration to commercial viability.
