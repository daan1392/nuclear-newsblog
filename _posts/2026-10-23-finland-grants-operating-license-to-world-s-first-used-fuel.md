Finnish radioactive waste management company Posiva has been granted an operating license for the encapsulation and final disposal facility for used nuclear fuel located at Olkiluoto. This marks the world's first repository for used fuel to receive such an operating license, representing a major milestone in the global effort to demonstrate safe long-term disposal solutions.

The facility represents the culmination of decades of research and development into deep geological disposal methods. Olkiluoto provides a well-characterized host rock formation in stable geological conditions, offering long-term isolation of radioactive materials from the biosphere.

The regulatory approval provides significant validation of the technical, safety, and operational frameworks for permanent used fuel disposal. As other nations develop geological repository programs, Finland's experience and successful licensing will serve as a reference case for international best practices in spent fuel management.
