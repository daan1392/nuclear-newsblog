The Department of Energy's Office of Science selected 28 projects for awards through the Innovation Network for Fusion Energy (INFUSE) programme, distributing $8.5 million to support private fusion companies' collaboration with US universities and national laboratories.

INFUSE provides fusion developers with access to specialized facilities, expertise, and capabilities to address critical scientific and technological challenges. By connecting commercial companies with publicly funded research infrastructure, the programme accelerates innovation in fusion energy technology and helps private ventures overcome technical barriers without duplicating expensive experimental facilities.

The awards span research institutions and industry partners, reflecting the growing ecosystem of private fusion ventures pursuing magnetic and inertial confinement approaches. This support mechanism has become central to US strategy for advancing fusion commercialization alongside government-funded research programmes like ITER.
