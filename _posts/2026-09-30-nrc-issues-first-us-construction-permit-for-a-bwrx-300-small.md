The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) has approved a construction permit for the Tennessee Valley Authority's (TVA) planned GE Vernova Hitachi BWRX-300 small modular reactor at the Clinch River site in Oak Ridge, Tennessee. It is the first construction permit issued in the United States for this reactor design.

A construction permit is the first of the two main NRC licences needed for a new power reactor in the US. It lets the applicant build the plant. Operation needs separate later authorisation. The BWRX-300 is a boiling water reactor, a design in which water boils inside the reactor vessel to produce steam. It is sized as a small modular reactor (SMR), meaning a unit smaller than conventional large reactors and intended to be built in a more standardised way.

The approval is notable for the SMR sector. TVA's project is one of the most closely watched attempts to move a Western SMR design from paper to construction, and the permit gives that effort a regulatory basis. Both World Nuclear News and the American Nuclear Society's Nuclear Newswire reported the decision.

The supplied reports give no further detail on construction schedules or permit conditions.
