Constellation Energy announced a long-term power purchase agreement with Amazon that will support a 190-megawatt electrical uprate at the Calvert Cliffs nuclear power plant in Lusby, Maryland. The agreement reflects growing corporate demand for reliable zero-carbon electricity to support data centre operations and other energy-intensive business activities.

Uprates of existing reactors increase power output from currently licensed facilities, extending their economic value while avoiding the permitting and construction timescales of new reactors. Calvert Cliffs' two pressurized water reactors currently operate on the U.S. Atlantic coast and have been reliable generators of baseload power for decades.

The partnership exemplifies an emerging trend in the nuclear sector, with major technology companies and other corporations signing long-term contracts to secure nuclear electricity. These agreements provide utilities with investment certainty for plant upgrades and operations while helping corporates meet climate and sustainability commitments.
