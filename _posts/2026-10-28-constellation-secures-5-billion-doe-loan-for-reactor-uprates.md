Constellation Energy has received a conditional loan commitment of up to $5 billion from the Department of Energy's Office of Energy Dominance Financing to support uprates and modernization across its nuclear fleet in Illinois, Maryland, and Pennsylvania. The proposed projects could add nearly 1.1 GW of generating capacity to existing reactors.

The eight-plant program represents one of the largest recent government commitments to sustain and expand US nuclear generation. Uprating—increasing a reactor's power output within safe operating limits—typically requires upgrades to cooling systems, instrumentation, and other components, alongside regulatory review and approval.

The financing mechanism uses conditional loan commitments, allowing companies to secure funding commitments subject to meeting specified milestones and regulatory approvals before drawdown. This approach has become increasingly important for nuclear projects given substantial capital requirements and the strategic importance of maintaining and growing the nation's existing fleet to support decarbonization and electricity supply goals.
