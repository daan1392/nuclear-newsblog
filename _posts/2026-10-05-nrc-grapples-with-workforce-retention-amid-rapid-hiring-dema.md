The Government Accountability Office released findings that the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission faced significant workforce challenges, with a net loss of approximately 500 employees between July 2024 and June 2026.

The GAO attributed the departures partly to competition from the private nuclear industry, which has expanded hiring as new projects advance. Simultaneously, the NRC has had to implement the ADVANCE Act of 2024 and related executive orders while maintaining oversight of the existing reactor fleet, straining human resources.

The report recommended that the NRC establish clear metrics to assess whether recruitment and retention efforts meet current and future staffing needs, and to complete development of a strategic workforce planning tool by early 2027. These measures aim to help the regulator sustain adequate technical capacity during a period of nuclear sector expansion.

The findings highlight a systemic challenge: regulatory agencies struggle to compete with industry compensation while facing expanded mandates. Without deliberate workforce development strategies, the NRC could face capability gaps in licensing and oversight functions.
