Poland's SGE (formerly Sythos Green Energy) and ZE Pak have established a joint venture to develop a new nuclear power plant at Pątnów featuring up to six BWRX-300 small modular reactors, with a combined capacity of 1,800 MWe. This agreement represents a significant step toward deploying advanced reactor technology in Central Europe.

SGE is a co-investor in GE Vernova Hitachi's BWRX-300 design and holds multiple project opportunities across Europe. In Poland, the company has already secured a decision in principle for 26 BWRX-300 units; in the United Kingdom, it is pursuing a proposal for 14 reactors across three sites under the UK's Advanced Nuclear Framework programme.

The BWRX-300 is a natural circulation boiling water reactor designed for simplified operation and reduced capital costs compared to conventional light-water reactors. The Pątnów plant would leverage Poland's growing commitment to nuclear energy as the nation seeks to diversify its generation portfolio and meet decarbonisation targets.
