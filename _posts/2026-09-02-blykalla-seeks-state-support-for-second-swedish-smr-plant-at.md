Sweden's Blykalla has applied for state support for a proposed small modular reactor (SMR) plant at Untra in Tierp municipality.

On 25 August the company submitted a proposal to the Swedish government for the plant, which would have up to eight lead-cooled SMRs and a total capacity of up to 440 MWe. It follows an earlier application, made in May, for a six-unit project at Norrsundet in Gävle municipality, in the same region of east-central Sweden.

Blykalla has set up Untra Nuclear Power AB as licence holder and financing vehicle. Strategic and financial investors are expected to join its ownership over time.

The filings show the company pursuing two sites in parallel. Whether either is approved will depend on government decisions on both the approval applications and the state support requests.
