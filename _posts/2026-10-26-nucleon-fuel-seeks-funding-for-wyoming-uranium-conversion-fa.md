Nucleon Fuel, a subsidiary of Canadian SMR developer Nucleon Energy, has applied for funding through Wyoming's Energy Dominance Fund to construct a uranium conversion facility near Gillette, Wyoming. The application seeks $20 million in grants with an additional $10 million potential loan to support the project.

The Energy Dominance Fund, recently established by Wyoming, provides capital to advance projects in oil, natural gas, coal, and nuclear energy. Uranium conversion represents a critical intermediate step in the fuel cycle, transforming natural uranium ore concentrates into uranium hexafluoride suitable for enrichment.

The project reflects growing interest in building resilient, domestically-controlled fuel supply chains in North America. Converting uranium close to mining sites reduces transportation costs and enhances security of supply. Wyoming's long history in uranium mining and processing, combined with available infrastructure and workforce, makes it a logical location for fuel cycle development.
