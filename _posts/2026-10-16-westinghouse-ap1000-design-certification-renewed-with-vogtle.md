The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission has approved Westinghouse Electric's request to renew and update the standard design certification (DC) for its AP1000 reactor. The renewal allows Westinghouse to incorporate construction lessons from the Vogtle-3 and Vogtle-4 units in Georgia into the AP1000 design, with Vogtle-4 now designated as the standard reference plant for future U.S. deployments.

Design certification renewal is significant for reactor standardisation and licensing efficiency. By codifying improvements derived from actual construction experience, the updated design reduces technical and schedule risks for subsequent projects, supporting the goal of more rapid and cost-effective deployment of the AP1000 for utilities pursuing fleet expansions.

The AP1000 is a key pillar of U.S. government plans to quadruple domestic nuclear capacity. The renewed certification establishes a proven, refined design baseline that should facilitate permitting and construction timelines for additional units planned by utilities across the country.
