NASA and the US Department of Energy signed a comprehensive Memorandum of Understanding on 8 October to establish a unified framework for space nuclear development, replacing and consolidating two earlier agreements. Titled "Accelerating American Leadership in Space Nuclear Power and Propulsion," the MOU entered into force on 1 November and covers the full spectrum of activities from research and fuel production through testing, launch integration, and operations.

The agreement was signed by NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman and US Secretary of Energy Chris Wright at the White House's "Science: A New Golden Age" summit. The new framework aims to streamline coordination between the two agencies and accelerate progress on advanced propulsion systems and power sources for deep-space exploration.

The integrated approach reflects growing recognition that space nuclear technologies are critical to future US space ambitions. By aligning funding, technical expertise, and programme timelines, the agencies intend to overcome development bottlenecks and position the United States for leadership in what is expected to be an expanding domain for nuclear applications.
