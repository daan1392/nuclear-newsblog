Bechtel will no longer take part in TerraPower's Natrium plant in Kemmerer, Wyoming, after the companies failed to agree on the next phase of the project.

TerraPower said it intends to rebid the engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) contract for the first-of-a-kind plant. A company spokesperson said the split would not affect the timeline of the Kemmerer-1 project.

Bechtel was named as TerraPower's design, licensing, procurement and construction partner in a federal grant application for a demonstration Natrium plant as long ago as 2020. A ground-breaking ceremony for the 345 MWe sodium-cooled fast reactor and its molten salt-based energy storage system took place at the site in June 2024.

The change of construction partner is notable because Natrium is one of the most closely watched advanced reactor projects in the United States. How quickly a replacement EPC contractor is chosen will be a point to watch.
