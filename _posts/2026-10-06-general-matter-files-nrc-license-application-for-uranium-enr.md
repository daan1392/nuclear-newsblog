General Matter announced its submission of a Nuclear Regulatory Commission license application to begin construction at the former Gaseous Diffusion Plant site in Paducah, Kentucky, signalling the next phase of efforts to revive domestic uranium enrichment capacity. The company had previously received NRC approval in early 2026 for preconstruction site preparation work.

Public documents related to the application, including an environmental report, became accessible in the NRC's ADAMS database on 5 October. If approved, the license would allow General Matter to conduct full facility construction and operation—moving beyond the limited site preparation activities previously authorised.

The application represents a broader push to expand U.S. enrichment capacity domestically, reducing reliance on foreign suppliers. The Paducah site, which hosted enrichment operations for decades, offers existing infrastructure and experienced workforce, making it a logical location for renewed enrichment development.
