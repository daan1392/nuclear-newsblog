Holtec International has submitted the Preliminary Safety Analysis Report (PSAR) to the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) for two small modular reactors planned at the Palisades Energy Center in Michigan, completing the second phase of the Construction Permit Application process.

The PSAR represents a critical regulatory milestone, detailing the reactor design basis, safety systems and operational procedures needed to demonstrate compliance with NRC safety standards. The document forms the technical foundation for NRC staff review and public comment before advancing toward a final safety evaluation and construction permit decision.

The Palisades site offers several advantages for SMR deployment: existing grid interconnections, trained workforce and regulatory familiarity with nuclear operations. Holtec's HI-SMR design is designed for flexible deployment across diverse sites. The submission advances the US licensing pathway for commercial SMRs and reinforces the NRC's role in evaluating new reactor technologies under existing regulatory frameworks adapted for smaller, modular designs.
