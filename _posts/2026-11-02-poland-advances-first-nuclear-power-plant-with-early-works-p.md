Poland's state nuclear company Polskie Elektrownie Jądrowe has applied to regional authorities in the Pomeranian Voivodeship for a permit to begin early works at the Lubiatowo-Kopalino site in Choczewo municipality, marking a key procedural step toward the country's first nuclear power plant.

Early works encompass site preparation and the construction of necessary infrastructure to enable subsequent phases of actual reactor construction. The planned facility will comprise three Westinghouse AP1000 reactors, each a proven large light-water design with significant global deployment.

Poland's nuclear program has gained political momentum as energy demand grows and the country seeks to reduce carbon emissions while improving energy independence. The permitting process for early works does not require a separate nuclear license, allowing preliminary activities to proceed in parallel with the broader licensing pathway.
