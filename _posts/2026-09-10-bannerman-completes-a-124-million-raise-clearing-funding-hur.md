Bannerman Energy has completed an underwritten placement of A$124 million (USD89 million), which it expects to fully fund the Etango uranium project in Namibia through construction and ramp-up.

The ASX-listed company launched the placement on 9 September after CNNC Overseas Limited confirmed that all conditions for its strategic investment and joint venture had been satisfied or waived. The placement closed a day later. The joint venture covers funding, development and operation of Etango.

Bannerman said the raise resolves the last funding obstacle as it moves towards a final investment decision (FID), the formal commitment to build. The company aims to become a major new greenfield uranium producer.

The announcement shows Chinese capital taking part in uranium supply development, and comes as executives at the World Nuclear Symposium described strong uranium demand from buyers in the East.
