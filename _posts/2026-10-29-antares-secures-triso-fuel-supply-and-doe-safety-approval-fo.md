Antares Nuclear announced a multi-year fuel procurement agreement with BWX Technologies (BWXT) for TRISO fuel to power its Mark-1 microreactor, alongside approval from the US Department of Energy for the reactor's Nuclear Safety Design Agreement. The commercial agreement specifies an initial tonne of TRISO fuel fabricated from high-assay low-enriched uranium (HALEU) at BWXT's Virginia facility, with potential for additional quantities as deployment scales. The DOE safety approval represents a critical milestone for Antares's transition from prototype testing to commercial electricity production and potential military deployments. TRISO particles—tristructural isotropic fuel consisting of a fuel kernel surrounded by carbon and silicon carbide layers—are designed for high-temperature gas reactor applications. Antares's Mark-0 demonstration reactor previously achieved criticality using BWXT fuel, establishing manufacturing readiness.
