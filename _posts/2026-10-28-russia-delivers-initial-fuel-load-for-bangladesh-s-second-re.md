Russia's state nuclear corporation Rosatom delivered the initial fuel load for the second unit of Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant in Bangladesh via cargo aircraft and road transport. The VVER-1200 fuel assemblies originated from Rosatom's Novosibirsk Chemical Concentrates Plant and are now stored in a specialized facility pending reactor commissioning. Bangladeshi technical experts and representatives from the general contractor and manufacturer conducted mandatory incoming inspections, including visual and instrumental testing to verify compliance with technical specifications. The fuel delivery marks another milestone in Rooppur's progression toward dual-unit operation. Earlier deliveries brought fresh nuclear fuel for the first unit. The facility represents Bangladesh's entry into nuclear power generation and reflects Russia's expanded role in constructing and supplying reactor technology across Asia.
