The Philippines is working to establish the policy, regulatory and commercial foundations for its first nuclear power project, with a target of deploying 1.2 GW of capacity by 2032, delegates heard at the World Nuclear Supply Chain conference in Manila.

The country has a longer-term ambition to reach 4.8 GW of nuclear capacity by 2050 as it seeks to meet rapidly growing electricity demand, strengthen energy security and diversify its power generation mix. The Philippines faces substantial electricity demand growth driven by population expansion, industrialization and increasing use of digital technologies.

Nuclear power offers a low-carbon baseload alternative to coal and gas generation, complementing intermittent renewables. The Philippine government is working with international partners and vendors to evaluate potential sites, reactor technologies and financing mechanisms as it advances from policy development toward concrete project development.
