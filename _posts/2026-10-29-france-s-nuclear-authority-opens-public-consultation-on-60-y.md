France's nuclear safety authority, the ASNR, announced a public consultation running until 2 December 2026 on whether the nation's existing reactor fleet could continue operating beyond 60 years. In its draft opinion, the ASNR stated it had "not identified, on the basis of current knowledge, any obstacle to continued operation of nuclear reactors beyond their 60 years" for which electricity operator EDF lacks remedial options. The consultation represents a significant step in evaluating the long-term future of France's aging reactor fleet, most of which were commissioned in the 1980s and 1990s. Extending operational lifetimes could enhance the economics of decarbonized electricity supply while requiring continued focus on safety and maintenance protocols for ageing systems. The ASNR's preliminary position suggests technical feasibility remains compatible with extended operation, contingent on thorough review of component degradation, materials performance, and updated safety analyses.
