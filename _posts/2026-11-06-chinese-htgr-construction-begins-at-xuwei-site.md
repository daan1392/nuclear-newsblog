China National Nuclear Corporation announced the beginning of nuclear island construction at the high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR) that forms part of the initial phase of the Jiangsu Xuwei Nuclear Heating and Power Plant. The milestone marks the transition from engineering and planning phases to active civil construction.

HTGRs are graphite-moderated, helium-cooled reactors designed to produce heat for district heating and industrial processes, with some configurations also generating electricity. China has pursued HTGR technology as a pathway toward high-temperature process heat applications including hydrogen production and industrial steam generation, complementing conventional electrical power generation.

This construction milestone indicates progress in China's diversified reactor portfolio strategy, which includes conventional large reactors (HPR1000), small modular reactors, and specialized designs like the HTGR. The Xuwei facility is positioned to demonstrate commercial viability of HTGR technology for combined heat and power applications.
