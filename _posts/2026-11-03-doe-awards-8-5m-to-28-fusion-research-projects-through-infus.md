INFUSE provides private fusion companies access to capabilities, expertise, and facilities at U.S. universities and national laboratories, helping companies overcome critical technical and scientific obstacles in fusion development.

The program model represents a public-private partnership approach, leveraging government research infrastructure to accelerate technology maturation by private sector ventures. Access to national laboratory equipment, experimental beamtime, and expertise can significantly compress development timelines for emerging technologies.
