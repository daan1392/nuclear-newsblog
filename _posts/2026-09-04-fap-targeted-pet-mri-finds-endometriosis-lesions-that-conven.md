A PET/MRI technique aimed at fibroblast activation protein (FAP) identified endometriosis lesions more effectively than conventional imaging, according to research published in The Journal of Nuclear Medicine.

FAP is a protein that is overexpressed in endometriosis. A radiotracer that binds to it lets PET, which detects radioactive signals, be combined with the anatomical detail of MRI. The researchers found the approach worked particularly well in regions of the pelvis and abdomen that are typically difficult to assess.

The authors suggested that mapping the full extent of disease more accurately could support more personalised management and better outcomes for patients. The summary did not give details on study size or sensitivity figures, so the clinical impact will depend on the full data and on further validation.
