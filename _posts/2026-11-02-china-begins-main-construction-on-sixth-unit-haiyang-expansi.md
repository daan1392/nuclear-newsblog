China National Nuclear Corporation contractor China Nuclear Industry 24 Construction Co Ltd poured the first safety-related concrete for the reactor building of unit 5 at Haiyang nuclear power plant on 31 October, officially commencing main construction for the facility's third expansion phase. Unit 5 is the fifth of six planned large pressurized water reactors at the site in Shandong province, contracted by State Power Investment Corporation.

The Haiyang complex expansion represents part of China's strategy to increase nuclear generating capacity and meet rising electricity demand while addressing carbon emission reduction targets. PWR technology remains the dominant design choice for large-scale reactor deployment in China, with multiple projects in various stages of construction or planning across the country.

Completion of main construction milestones—such as first concrete—represents a critical step toward eventual fuel loading and grid connection. The project advances China's position as a leader in high-volume reactor construction and deployment of proven light-water reactor technology.
