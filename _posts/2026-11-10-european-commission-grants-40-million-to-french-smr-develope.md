Calogena, a small modular reactor developer and subsidiary of the Gorgé group, was awarded a €40 million grant from the European Commission's Innovation Fund. This represents the first time the Commission has funded commercial SMR unit construction, signalling growing European policy support for advanced reactor technology.

The grant is conditional on Calogena obtaining operating rights for the Pierrelatte district heating system, indicating integration of the facility into local energy infrastructure. The announcement follows €50 million in support from the French government earlier in 2026 and reflects momentum in European SMR development.

The award also comes after the European Investment Bank provided support to Finnish developer Steady Energy in September 2026, indicating a broader shift in institutional finance toward advanced reactor technologies. European SMR developers are accessing multiple funding sources as the sector matures and moves from demonstration toward commercial deployment.
