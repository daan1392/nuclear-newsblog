Newcleo, a Paris-based advanced nuclear technology company, has inaugurated its FASTER (Fuel process Assembly Storage Training and Enhanced Reality) facility in Chusclan, Gard, southern France, near the Marcoule nuclear complex. The EUR10 million (USD11 million) centre provides advanced engineering, prototype testing, and immersive training capabilities for Newcleo's fuel engineering team of approximately 100 specialists.

The facility supports Newcleo's development of mixed-oxide (MOX) fuel manufacturing technology. MOX fuel, produced from recycled plutonium and depleted uranium, is a key component of closed fuel cycle strategies and advanced reactor designs. The FASTER facility's capabilities enable rapid iteration on fuel assembly designs and operator training, accelerating the path toward commercial deployment.

The investment reflects Newcleo's strategy to vertically integrate fuel supply for its advanced liquid-metal cooled reactors, positioning the company to offer both reactor technology and associated fuel cycle services to customers.
