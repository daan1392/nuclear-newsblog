Copenhagen Atomics and uranium enrichment services provider Urenco signed a memorandum of understanding to design and construct a uranium deconversion facility in Copenhagen. Under the agreement, Urenco will provide engineering support for a dedicated facility to convert uranium hexafluoride (UF₆) into uranium tetrafluoride (UF₄).

Copenhagen Atomics will source enriched UF₆, convert it to UF₄, and synthesize high-purity fuel salts for its own molten salt reactors while supplying molten salt fuel to other MSR developers and research institutions. Deconversion—the chemical conversion of enrichment tails and finished fuel products—represents a specialized fuel cycle service historically limited in geographic availability outside major nuclear fuel suppliers.

This partnership demonstrates the emerging fuel cycle infrastructure requirements for advanced reactor designs like molten salt reactors, which operate on different fuel chemistries than conventional light-water reactors. The facility would strengthen European fuel cycle independence for emerging reactor technologies.
