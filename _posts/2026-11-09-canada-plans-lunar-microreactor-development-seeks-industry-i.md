The Canadian Space Agency announced a request for information seeking input from Canadian industry and academia on existing technologies and capabilities for lunar microreactors. The RFI targets technologies at technology readiness level 2 or higher, covering both complete reactor designs and individual subsystems.

The agency identified fission power systems as complementary to solar power and batteries for lunar missions, with applications in exploration, mobility, and future crewed operations. Microreactors would provide long-duration power independent of solar illumination cycles, addressing a key constraint for sustained lunar presence.

The assessment aims to identify areas where Canada has differentiated or strategically valuable capabilities in this emerging field. Small reactor technology development has gained momentum globally as agencies and companies explore advanced power systems for space and remote terrestrial applications.
