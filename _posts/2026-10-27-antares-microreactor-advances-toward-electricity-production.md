Antares Nuclear announced a multi-year advanced nuclear fuel fabrication agreement with BWX Technologies and received initial Department of Energy safety approval for its Mark-1 microreactor. These milestones advance the company's transition from reactor testing toward commercial electricity production and military deployments.

The Mark-1 is designed as a transportable microreactor for remote or military applications. Securing a fuel supply agreement with BWXT, a major supplier of naval and research reactor fuel, is essential for scaling production. DOE safety approval—though not a full licensing decision—indicates the agency's confidence in the design's safety fundamentals.

Antares is among several US firms developing microreactors for niche markets where conventional reactors are uneconomical. The company's progress reflects growing private-sector investment in advanced reactor technologies and the role of government agencies in de-risking development pathways.
