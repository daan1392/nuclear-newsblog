More than 300 radiologists, imaging technologists, pediatricians, surgeons and other specialists from every province of Mongolia participated in a national training course conducted by the International Atomic Energy Agency to strengthen their skills in pediatric medical imaging. The initiative aimed to make medical imaging for children safer and more effective across the country. By equipping practitioners across all provincial health systems with updated techniques and best practices, the training contributes to improved diagnostic accuracy and reduced radiation doses in vulnerable pediatric populations. Such coordinated professional development reflects the IAEA's role in building technical capacity for medical applications of nuclear science in member states with developing healthcare infrastructure.
