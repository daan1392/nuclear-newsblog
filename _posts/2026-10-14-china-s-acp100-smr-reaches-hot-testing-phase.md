China's ACP100 small modular reactor demonstration project at Changjiang on Hainan island has entered hot testing, a key milestone that simulates the thermal and pressure conditions the reactor systems will experience during normal operation. The tests represent significant progress toward commercialization of the domestically developed SMR design.

The ACP100 is being developed by China General Nuclear Power Group as part of broader efforts to expand the nation's SMR portfolio alongside larger conventional reactors. SMRs have attracted global interest for their potential in smaller grids, remote locations, and industrial heat applications, though deployment remains limited outside demonstration projects.

Successful completion of hot testing typically precedes licensing and power generation, though timelines for Chinese nuclear projects have historically been faster than those in Western markets. The progress at Changjiang underscores China's continued investment in diverse reactor technologies as it works toward peak carbon emissions before 2030.
