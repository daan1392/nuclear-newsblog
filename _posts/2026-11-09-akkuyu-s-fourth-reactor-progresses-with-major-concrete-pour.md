Construction of the turbine building foundation for unit 4 at Turkey's Akkuyu Nuclear Power Plant reached a major milestone with completion of a large continuous concrete pour. The operation used more than 2,240 cubic metres of concrete and required nine concrete pumps working non-stop for over a day, following installation of 308 tonnes of reinforcing steel.

The foundation will require approximately 60 days to reach its design strength before Turkey's nuclear regulator, the NDK (Nükleer Düzenleme Kurumu), can approve commencement of turbine set component installation. Akkuyu Nuclear CEO Sergei Butskikh emphasised that this marks the fourth turbine foundation successfully completed at the site, reflecting accumulated project experience.

Unit 4 will be a Hualong One pressurised water reactor, the same design used for units 1–3. The project continues to progress on schedule as Turkey advances construction of what will be its first operational nuclear power plant.
