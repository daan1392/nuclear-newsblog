Germany's Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation (SPRIND) has established a milestone-based fusion funding program on behalf of the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology, and Space. The initiative supports the government's High-Tech Agenda Germany, which aims to translate research into practical applications through technology-specific roadmaps.

Funds will be awarded competitively to fusion companies, particularly start-ups, to advance development. The program represents a strategic push by Germany to maintain technology leadership in fusion energy as the sector attracts growing international investment.
