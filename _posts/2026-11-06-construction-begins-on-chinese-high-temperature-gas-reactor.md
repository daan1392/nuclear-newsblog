China National Nuclear Corporation has begun construction of the Jiangsu Xuwei Nuclear Heating and Power Plant with the first concrete pour for the nuclear island of a high-temperature gas-cooled reactor. The HTGR design, developed and demonstrated in China over the past two decades, offers inherent safety characteristics and the ability to deliver both electricity and high-temperature process heat for industrial applications including hydrogen production and district heating. The facility represents the first commercial-scale HTGR plant and a significant step in scaling up the technology for industrial deployment. High-temperature gas reactors operate at significantly higher outlet temperatures than conventional light-water reactors, enabling efficient utilization of thermal energy beyond power generation and making them attractive for decarbonizing industrial sectors difficult to electrify.
