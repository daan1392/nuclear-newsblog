France's nuclear safety regulator Autorité de Sûreté Nucléaire (ASN) has inaugurated new research infrastructure and testing facilities at its Cadarache site dedicated to evaluating the reliability and performance of passive safety systems. These systems, which operate without active components or operator intervention, are central to the safety case for advanced reactor designs including small modular reactors (SMRs). The facility enables ASN to independently generate the scientific knowledge necessary for rigorous safety assessment of innovative reactor technologies before regulatory approval. As the nuclear sector moves toward deployment of diverse reactor types, including SMRs, this dedicated research capacity strengthens the regulator's ability to evaluate novel designs and contribute to harmonised international safety standards.
