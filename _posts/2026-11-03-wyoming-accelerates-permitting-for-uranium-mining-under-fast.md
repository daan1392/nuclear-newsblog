Wyoming Governor Mark Gordon and the Federal Permitting Improvement Steering Council signed a memorandum of understanding on October 26 to accelerate permitting processes for the state's infrastructure projects under Title 41 of the Fixing America's Surface Transportation Act, commonly known as FAST-41. The agreement covers uranium mining projects alongside other critical infrastructure initiatives.

FAST-41 establishes expedited federal and state permitting procedures intended to reduce project timelines and regulatory uncertainty. For uranium mining, which requires approval from multiple agencies, streamlined coordination could help facilitate development of domestic uranium resources at a time when supply chain security is increasingly prioritized.

The agreement represents state-level action to support uranium production capacity, complementing broader US efforts to strengthen domestic fuel cycle independence and reduce reliance on foreign sources.
