Estonia's search for a location to build its first nuclear power plant has been narrowed to three candidate sites, all within Viru-Nigula municipality approximately 120 kilometres east of Tallinn. Three alternative locations in Lüganuse municipality were rejected based on security considerations.

The decision reflects the country's intent to pursue nuclear energy while balancing competing infrastructure and safety concerns. Site selection for new reactor projects involves assessment of geography, demographics, industrial capability, and national security factors, with nuclear facilities subject to particular scrutiny regarding proximity to sensitive installations and borders.
