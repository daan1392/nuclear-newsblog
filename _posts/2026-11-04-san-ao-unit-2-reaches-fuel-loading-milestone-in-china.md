Fuel loading was completed on November 2 at unit 2 of the San'ao Nuclear Power Plant in Zhejiang province, China, advancing the second of six planned HPR1000 reactors toward startup. China General Nuclear Corporation said the milestone marks "the official entry of the unit into a new stage of nuclear-powered operation and commissioning," representing a key step toward completing the first phase of the project.

The HPR1000, also known as the Hualong One, is China's domestically developed pressurized water reactor design. The San'ao facility is establishing itself as a major deployment site for the technology. The fuel loading operation employed semi-automatic equipment to manage the loading process.

Completion of fuel loading typically precedes several months of testing and regulatory checks before a reactor can achieve critical startup. The milestone demonstrates continued progress in China's nuclear construction program amid broader global interest in new reactor deployments.
