US small modular reactor developer NuScale Power and South Korea's Doosan Enerbility signed a manufacturing contract at Doosan's Changwon headquarters. Under the agreement, Doosan will produce the upper reactor coolant system—a critical component forming the upper section of the reactor pressure vessel that contains reactor coolant under high-temperature, high-pressure conditions. The contract deepens supply chain integration for NuScale's commercial deployment and leverages Doosan's established capabilities in heavy equipment manufacturing for nuclear plants. The arrangement supports NuScale's commercialization timeline while expanding international supply chain partnerships for modular reactor technology.
