Canada's 2026 budget, released on 5 November and titled "Masters of our destiny," included investments in nuclear energy as part of a strategy to double the country's grid capacity by 2050. The budget outlined funding mechanisms and identified 28 "nation-building initiatives" referred to the federal Major Projects Office for fast-tracked consideration.

Ontario Power Generation's Darlington New Nuclear project was among the first initiatives referred to the Major Projects Office, highlighting government commitment to large-scale reactor construction. The budget also addressed critical mineral supply chains, recognising the relationship between nuclear development and requirements for materials like uranium and rare earths.

The investment builds on momentum from the 2025 budget, which had already enabled major projects to advance. This sustained commitment signals confidence in nuclear power's role in Canada's energy future and decarbonisation objectives.
