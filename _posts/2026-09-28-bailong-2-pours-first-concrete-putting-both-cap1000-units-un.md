First concrete was poured for the nuclear island of unit 2 at the Bailong nuclear power plant in China's Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region at 18:27 on Sunday 27 September, so both units of the plant's first phase are now officially under construction.

The nuclear island is the part of a plant that houses the reactor and its primary systems. Chinese state television reported the milestone, and State Power Investment Corporation (SPIC) carried the report on its Weibo channel. The initial pour was expected to use 6,637 cubic metres of concrete over 58 hours.

Phase I of Bailong, which covers units 1 and 2, was among 11 new reactors that China's State Council approved in August 2024. SPIC plans to build two CAP1000 pressurised water reactors at the site. The CAP1000 is a Chinese-designed large reactor.

The pour marks the start of the main nuclear-island construction for the second unit. It adds to China's current build programme, which is among the largest in the world.
