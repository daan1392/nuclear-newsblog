Nuclear Restoration Services has completed the largest land remediation project in its history, restoring 4.2 hectares at its Harwell site in Oxfordshire following more than 60 years of liquid effluent treatment operations. The project involved removing over 97,000 tonnes of material from the former Liquid Effluent Treatment Plant, making the land available for future use. This milestone represents the culmination of extensive work to characterise contamination, safely excavate and process radioactive and non-radioactive material, and restore the site to acceptable end-state conditions. The successful completion of Harwell's largest single remediation project demonstrates progress in the UK's decommissioning programme and the technical capability to manage legacy contamination from decades of nuclear research operations.
