The US Department of Energy's Office of Energy Dominance Financing announced a conditional loan commitment of up to $5 billion to Constellation Energy for nuclear power uprates and modernization across eight reactor units in Illinois, Maryland, and Pennsylvania. The investment is intended to add approximately 1.1 gigawatts of generating capacity to the existing nuclear fleet by improving thermal efficiency and operational performance at mature plants. The loan addresses growing electricity demand from data centers, artificial intelligence infrastructure, and industrial electrification. Federal support for uprate projects reflects renewed emphasis on maximizing output from operating reactors as faster alternatives to new construction. Constellation, the nation's largest operator of commercial nuclear reactors, targets these modernization projects as a near-term source of carbon-free electricity supply. The loan remains conditional pending final agreement negotiations and project approval timelines.
