The Department of Energy has approved cost-shared funding through its Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program for an accelerated final stage of the Molten Chloride Reactor Experiment (MCRE), conducted by Southern Company and Core Power in collaboration with Idaho National Laboratory.

The project aims to reach criticality as early as 2027, which would mark a world-first for a fast-spectrum molten chloride salt reactor. The experiment is designed to generate data on fuel behavior, reactor operation, and performance to support future commercial reactor development. Molten salt technology offers potential advantages including high operating temperatures and compatibility with industrial heat applications.
