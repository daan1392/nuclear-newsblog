An inspection by the Nuclear Regulatory Commission of the Palisades nuclear plant in Covert, Michigan has raised concerns about safety-conscious work environment (SCWE) in the supply chain and quality assurance departments. The NRC's report, issued October 13, suggests that employees in these areas may be hesitant to raise safety concerns through available channels due to fear of retaliation.

Palisades, operated by Holtec International, is attempting to become the first U.S. nuclear power plant to restart after shutdown. The work environment findings are significant for a facility seeking to return to operation and will require attention to management practices and employee communication before restart approval.
