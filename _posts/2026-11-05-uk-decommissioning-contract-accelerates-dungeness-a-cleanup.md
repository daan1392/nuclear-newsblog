The UK Nuclear Decommissioning Authority has appointed engineering firm Aureos to deliver the next major phase of decommissioning at Dungeness A in Kent. The contract, worth up to £190 million, will enable removal of some of the site's largest remaining structures, accelerating the overall cleanup effort.

Dungeness A, a Magnox-type gas-cooled reactor facility, ceased generation in 1989 and entered decommissioning decades ago. The contract represents a significant expansion of removal operations beyond earlier phases focused on smaller components and interim storage facilities.

Aureos, a UK supply-chain firm, will work under the stewardship of NRS—the Nuclear Restoration Services company responsible for managing several UK decommissioning sites. The contract reflects the NDA's emphasis on using domestic expertise and supply chains to manage long-term cleanup obligations. Accelerating structural demolition reduces interim storage costs and brings final site closure closer.
