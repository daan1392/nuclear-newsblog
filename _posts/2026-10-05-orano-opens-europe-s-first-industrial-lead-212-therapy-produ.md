Orano Med has inaugurated ATLab Valenciennes, Europe's first industrial-scale facility dedicated to producing lead-212-based targeted alpha therapies in Valenciennes, France. The facility is designed to manufacture up to 10,000 treatment doses per year of this emerging class of cancer medications.

Targeted alpha therapy represents a new generation of precision oncology, leveraging alpha-emitting isotopes bound to proteins or antibodies that specifically recognize and destroy cancer cells while limiting exposure to surrounding healthy tissue. Lead-212, a short-lived alpha emitter, is particularly suited to this approach because its decay characteristics allow therapeutic efficacy while reducing long-term radiation burden on the patient.

The opening of ATLab Valenciennes marks a significant step toward making targeted alpha therapies more accessible within Europe. By establishing industrial-scale production capacity, Orano is positioning itself to support clinical expansion of these treatments as they advance through regulatory approval and clinical adoption across European healthcare systems. The facility's capacity signals confidence in the clinical and commercial viability of this radiopharmaceutical category.
