China General Nuclear announced first concrete pouring for the nuclear island of unit 4 at the San'ao plant in Zhejiang province, marking entry into the main construction phase for the second phase of the project. The milestone occurred on 10 November 2026.

The San'ao expansion demonstrates the continuing deployment of China's domestically developed Hualong One (HPR1000) reactor design. Units 1 and 2, comprising the first phase, received construction approval in September 2020, with first concrete for unit 1 poured in December 2020. Units 3 and 4 extend the two-unit foundation into a larger facility.

The Hualong One design, which incorporates active and passive safety systems alongside a 60-year design life, is becoming a centerpiece of China's nuclear expansion and an export offering for international projects. Continued deployment on domestic sites demonstrates confidence in the design and builds operational experience ahead of further international orders.
