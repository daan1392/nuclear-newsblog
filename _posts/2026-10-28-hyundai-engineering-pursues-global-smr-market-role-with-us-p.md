Hyundai Engineering announced memorandums of understanding with US SMR developers NANO Nuclear Energy and ARC Clean Technology, aiming to establish a role in the global small modular reactor market. The MoU with NANO Nuclear Energy, which is developing a high-temperature gas reactor-based micro modular reactor design, will explore potential collaboration on the KRONOS MMR Energy System project at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and broader manufacturing and deployment partnerships.

The agreements reflect growing international interest in securing positions within the emerging SMR supply chain and manufacturing ecosystem. South Korean firms bring established nuclear engineering expertise, manufacturing capabilities, and quality standards from decades of large reactor construction and operation.

These partnerships position Hyundai Engineering to support commercialization of multiple advanced reactor designs, while SMR developers gain access to established fabrication and manufacturing capacity. International collaboration on SMR deployment is becoming increasingly important as multiple vendors pursue commercial licensing and first-of-a-kind deployments.
