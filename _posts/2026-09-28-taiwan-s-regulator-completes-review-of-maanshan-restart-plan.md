Taiwan's nuclear regulator has completed its review of Taipower's plan to restart the Maanshan nuclear power plant, which the utility submitted earlier this year.

The plant's two pressurised water reactors were shut down when their 40-year operating licences expired, unit 1 in July 2024 and unit 2 in May 2025. That followed Taiwan's nuclear phase-out policy.

The legal basis for a restart changed in May 2025. Taiwan's Legislative Yuan amended the Nuclear Reactor Facilities Regulation Act so that operators can apply for a 20-year licence renewal beyond the 40-year limit. That could extend a plant's life to 60 years. Taipower submitted its restart plan under this amended framework.

The completed review is a step in the regulatory process. The supplied information does not say what the regulator concluded or what approvals remain before the units could operate again.
