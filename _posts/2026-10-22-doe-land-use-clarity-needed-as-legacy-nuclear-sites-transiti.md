The Government Accountability Office released two reports on October 16 addressing the Department of Energy's nuclear site cleanup and remediation efforts, emphasizing the need for clearer policies as DOE leases and transfers land at legacy nuclear facilities. The agency is increasingly making portions of its contaminated sites available for new nuclear fuel cycle activities and other industrial uses including solar arrays and AI infrastructure projects. The GAO reports observe the DOE's "renewed focus on the potential future uses of these sites" and note growing interest from community stakeholders and industry in commercial development. The recommendations highlight tensions between completing environmental remediation, establishing clear land-use standards, and enabling productive reuse of historically significant nuclear sites. Clarifying regulatory scope and cleanup end-states will be essential as contaminated facilities transition to new purposes.
