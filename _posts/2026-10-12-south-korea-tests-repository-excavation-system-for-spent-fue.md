Hyundai Engineering & Construction announced successful testing of equipment it developed to excavate full-scale deposition holes for used nuclear fuel disposal at a bedrock demonstration site. The test is part of a government-funded R&D programme on spent fuel management coordinated by the Korea Radioactive Waste Agency (KORAD).

Hyundai E&C is the sole construction company participating in the multi-year project, which is being conducted through the company's integrated research organization, the HMG Construction Technology Research Institute. The testing programme focuses on developing core technologies required to construct and operate a deep geological repository for long-term spent fuel isolation.

Successful excavation testing demonstrates progress toward engineering the physical infrastructure needed for Korea's spent fuel disposition strategy. As the country's nuclear fleet operates and generates spent fuel, reliable repository construction and operation capabilities are essential. The demonstration project validates key construction technologies and operational procedures that will inform future full-scale repository development.
