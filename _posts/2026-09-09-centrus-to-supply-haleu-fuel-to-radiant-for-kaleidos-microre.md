US enrichment company Centrus Energy has signed a definitive multi-year contract to supply Radiant with fuel for its Kaleidos microreactors.

Centrus will begin delivering high-assay, low-enriched uranium (HALEU) before the end of the decade. HALEU is enriched to between 5% and 20% uranium-235 and is needed by many advanced reactor designs now in development. The deal adds another domestic fuel source to support commercial scale-up of Radiant's California-based Kaleidos fleet.

The US has no commercial HALEU supply, and the Department of Energy has been actively supporting development of a domestic chain. In 2019 it awarded Centrus a contract to license and build a cascade of advanced centrifuges to demonstrate production.

The agreement shows developers of small reactors beginning to lock in fuel supply agreements, an issue that has long been seen as a bottleneck for deployment of advanced designs.
