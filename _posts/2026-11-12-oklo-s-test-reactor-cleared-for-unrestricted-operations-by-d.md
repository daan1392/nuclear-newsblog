The US Department of Energy cleared the final operations hold point for Oklo's Groves Isotope Test Reactor in Caldwell County, Texas, authorizing unrestricted operations. The facility achieved first criticality in August 2026, less than a year after groundbreaking.

Groves-1 is one of 11 advanced reactor projects selected by the DOE in August 2025 for the Nuclear Reactor Pilot Program, which expedites testing of innovative designs under a DOE authorization pathway that accelerates development timelines compared to traditional NRC licensing.

The clearance enables Oklo to proceed into the research, testing, and training phase, allowing the company to prepare teams and systems for efficient demonstration of advanced reactor operations. The rapid progression from construction to operational clearance illustrates the DOE's commitment to accelerating technology validation for next-generation reactor designs. Groves-1 will generate operational experience and performance data critical to commercialization of Oklo's metal-fueled fast reactor technology.
