The Department of Energy's Office of Environmental Management has upgraded the cask processing enclosure at Oak Ridge's Transuranic Waste Processing Center (TWPC), enabling the facility to handle additional forms of radioactive waste and accelerate site cleanup operations. The TWPC comprises four processing areas: contact-handled glovebox lines, a hot cell facility, a box breakdown area, and the newly refurbished cask processing enclosure (CPE). The CPE upgrade allows the center to process transuranic waste types not previously handled, expanding the facility's contribution to cleaning up the 37-square-mile reservation. Transuranic waste—material contaminated with elements heavier than uranium—requires specialized handling and long-term storage due to its extended radioactive half-life. The modernization represents continued investment in DOE's environmental remediation mission across the nuclear weapons complex.
