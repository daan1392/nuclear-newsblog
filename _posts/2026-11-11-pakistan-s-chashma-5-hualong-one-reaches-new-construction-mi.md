The inner safety dome has been installed on the containment building of unit 5 at Pakistan's Chashma nuclear power plant, advancing a major construction milestone for the South Asian nation's largest electricity-generating nuclear unit.

Chashma 5 will be the third Hualong One (HPR1000) reactor to be built in Pakistan, following units 2 and 3 at Karachi, which were China National Nuclear Corporation's first exports of the design. At 1,200 MWe, Chashma 5 represents Pakistan's most powerful nuclear generating unit to date. Pakistan signed a USD 4.8 billion deal with China in June 2023 for the unit's construction.

The reactor incorporates the Hualong One's active and passive safety features, including a double-shell containment and filtered venting system, with a 60-year design life. Dome installation signals progression toward critical systems assembly and testing phases. The unit strengthens Pakistan's nuclear generation capacity as the nation expands low-carbon electricity supply.
