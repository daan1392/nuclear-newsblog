Polskie Elektrownie Jądrowe (PEJ), Poland's nuclear utility, commissioned Westinghouse to begin procuring long-lead items for the first AP1000 unit at the Lubiatowo-Kopalino nuclear power plant. Long-lead procurement is a critical early-stage activity that orders components with extended manufacturing and delivery timelines—such as reactor vessels, steam generators, and heavy forgings—to maintain project schedules.

This procurement milestone signals Poland's advance toward constructing its first commercial nuclear power plant, a centerpiece of the country's strategy to reduce reliance on coal and meet European Union climate commitments. Poland has selected the AP1000 design for its planned fleet, and initiating long-lead ordering typically precedes formal construction commencement by several years.

The procurement launch reflects both Westinghouse's confidence in the project and Poland's commitment to executing its nuclear development program on schedule.
