The US Department of Energy has closed a loan of up to $1.9 billion to NextEra Energy to help finance the restart of the Duane Arnold Energy Center in Iowa.

The loan came from the department's Office of Energy Dominance Financing. Duane Arnold is a single-unit boiling water reactor in Linn County and is the state's only nuclear power plant. It was shut down in 2020.

Last October, NextEra said it planned to restart the plant by early 2029, subject to regulatory approvals. The plan is underpinned by a 25-year power purchase agreement with Google. NextEra described the project as one of the most significant nuclear restart efforts under way in the US, and said it was about delivering new power to meet new demand.

The loan closing brings the project closer to returning the plant to service, though the restart still depends on approvals from regulators. Restarts of recently closed reactors are attractive because they reuse existing sites and infrastructure, and the Duane Arnold deal shows technology companies' long-term power contracts helping to anchor financing.
