ČEZ has submitted the Environmental Impact Assessment documentation for its planned Rolls-Royce SMR project at the Temelín site to the Czech Ministry of the Environment. The EIA submission is a critical stage in the regulatory permitting process and evaluates potential environmental effects of the proposed small modular reactor, along with mitigation measures to minimize any adverse impacts.

Water availability was a central consideration in site selection and preparation for the SMR project. ČEZ conducted detailed surveys and long-term monitoring of water resources to ensure adequate supply for cooling and safe long-term operations. The environmental review also addresses broader site-specific factors relevant to reactor deployment in an established nuclear facility.

Submission of the EIA advances the project toward future licensing stages. The Temelín site, which already operates two large pressurized water reactors, offers existing infrastructure and expertise that could facilitate SMR deployment. The review process will now determine whether the project can proceed to subsequent permitting phases.
