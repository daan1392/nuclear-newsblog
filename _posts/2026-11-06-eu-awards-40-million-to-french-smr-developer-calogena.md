The European Commission's Innovation Fund has awarded €40 million (USD 44.6 million) to French small modular reactor developer Calogena to support the potential construction of its first commercial nuclear reactor. The proposed facility will be sited in Pierrelatte in southern France, a location with existing nuclear infrastructure and industrial heating demands. Calogena's design employs molten salt technology, which offers inherent safety advantages and flexibility for industrial process heat applications. The EU funding recognizes the strategic importance of advanced reactor deployment in meeting Europe's climate and energy security objectives. The award underscores accelerating momentum behind next-generation reactor commercialization in Europe, with multiple SMR developers advancing toward deployment within the current decade.
