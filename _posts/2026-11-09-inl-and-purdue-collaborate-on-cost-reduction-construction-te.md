Idaho National Laboratory's National Reactor Innovation Center (NRIC) announced a collaboration with Purdue University's Applied Research Institute (PARI) focused on demonstrating advanced construction techniques for reducing costs and risks in new reactor projects. The initiative, part of NRIC's Advanced Construction Technology Initiative (ACTI), also includes participation from the Electric Power Research Institute and the Nuclear Energy Institute.

Construction cost and schedule control remain critical challenges for nuclear new build projects. The partnership aims to identify, test, and validate innovative approaches to design and assembly that could improve project economics and delivery timelines, potentially benefiting both conventional and advanced reactor developers.
