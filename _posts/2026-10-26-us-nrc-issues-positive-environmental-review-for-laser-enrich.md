The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission issued its Final Environmental Impact Statement for Global Laser Enrichment's proposed Paducah Laser Enrichment Facility in Kentucky, recommending approval for a construction and operation license. The Final Safety Evaluation Report, the last regulatory milestone before licensing, is expected by year's end.

Global Laser Enrichment submitted its environmental report to the NRC in December 2024. Laser enrichment technology uses narrowband laser isotope separation to produce enriched uranium, potentially offering lower electricity consumption than diffusion or centrifuge methods. If licensed, PLEF would add capacity to the US uranium enrichment supply chain.

The positive environmental review advances a facility aimed at supplying fuel to US reactors and supporting allied nations' fuel security. Approval reflects the NRC's assessment that laser enrichment can meet safety, security, and environmental standards. Licensing would diversify US enrichment technology and capacity, supporting both domestic and international nuclear markets while reducing dependence on international enrichment services.
