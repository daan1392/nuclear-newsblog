Germany's Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation (SPRIND) has launched a milestone-based fusion program on behalf of the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology, and Space (BMFTR) as part of the government's High-Tech Agenda Germany (HTAD). The initiative aims to support fusion companies, particularly startups, in advancing their technology development.

The program operates through a competitive process to award funds, enabling companies to achieve technology-specific milestones that accelerate the conversion of fusion research into practical applications. This approach reflects HTAD's broader strategy of using technology roadmaps and flagship programs to strengthen Germany's high-performance innovation capacity.

The funding programme represents a policy shift toward direct government support for private fusion ventures, aligning with a growing global trend of backing advanced reactor and fusion projects as nations pursue decarbonisation and energy security objectives.
