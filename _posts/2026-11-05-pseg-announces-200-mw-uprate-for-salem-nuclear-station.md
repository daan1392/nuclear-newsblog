The Salem nuclear power plant near Hancock Bridge, New Jersey, is slated to receive a 200-megawatt uprate, bringing additional clean power generation to the grid. PSEG Nuclear will capture approximately 112 MW of the total increase, with co-owner Constellation receiving the remainder. The Salem facility comprises two units and has demonstrated strong operational performance, making it a candidate for the uprate through enhanced turbine and balance-of-plant modifications. The planned upgrade aligns with a broader industry initiative announced earlier by Constellation, which identified 11 nuclear facilities eligible for capacity increases totaling hundreds of megawatts. Such uprates represent a cost-effective alternative to new construction and extend the productive life of existing infrastructure, particularly important as electricity demand grows amid electrification and data center expansion.
