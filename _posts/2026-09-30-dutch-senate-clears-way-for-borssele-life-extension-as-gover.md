The Dutch Senate has approved an amendment to the Nuclear Energy Act that allows the Borssele nuclear power plant to operate beyond 2033, the year it was previously due to close.

The amendment opens the door to a possible life extension rather than guaranteeing one. Any extended operation would still depend on further decisions. Borssele is the only operating nuclear power plant in the Netherlands.

At the same time, the Dutch government said it has reached an agreement to acquire 70% of the shares of EPZ, the plant's operator. A state majority stake would give the government much greater influence over the future of the plant.

Together, the two steps show the Netherlands moving further away from a fixed phase-out timetable for its existing reactor. The supplied report does not give the terms of the EPZ share deal or a timeline for completing it.
