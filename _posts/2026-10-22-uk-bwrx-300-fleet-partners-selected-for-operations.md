Ontario Power Generation subsidiary Origin Nuclear and the UK project company of European SMR developer SGE have signed a letter of intent to form a joint venture for operating a planned fleet of 14 BWRX-300 small modular reactors. The partnership signals confidence in the maturity of the BWRX-300 design, which is a boiling-water reactor with passive safety features, and reflects growing momentum for SMR deployment in the United Kingdom. The collaboration brings together operational expertise from Canada's largest electricity producer with European development capabilities, positioning the venture to manage construction and operational logistics as units come online. This arrangement underscores the international nature of SMR commercialization and demonstrates how partnerships between established utilities and specialized developers can accelerate deployment timelines for advanced reactor technologies.
