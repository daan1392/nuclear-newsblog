Ontario Power Generation disclosed that unit 1 of its Darlington New Nuclear Project, featuring a BWRX-300 small modular reactor, will not meet previously announced commercial operation timelines or cost estimates. The company acknowledged the project remains subject to "inherent risks and uncertainties" affecting schedule and cost.

OPG announced the schedule adjustment while reporting third-quarter 2026 financial results, indicating a comprehensive reassessment of project cost and schedule is underway. The revision reflects challenges common to first-of-a-kind projects deploying novel technology at scale, including supply chain complexities, regulatory processes, and construction logistics.

The Darlington project comprises four BWRX-300 units planned for the site. Revised timelines will guide investor expectations and regulatory planning as Ontario pursues SMR deployment as part of its grid modernization and decarbonization strategy. The project remains strategically important for Canada's advanced reactor demonstration efforts despite scheduling pressures.
