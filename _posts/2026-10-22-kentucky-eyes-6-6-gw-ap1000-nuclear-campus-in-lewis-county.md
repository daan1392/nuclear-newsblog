The Nuclear Company outlined plans for Prosperity One, a proposed nuclear energy campus in Lewis County, Kentucky that could feature up to six Westinghouse AP1000 reactors generating approximately 6.6 GW of capacity if fully deployed. The facility would be situated along the Ohio River.

The company has partnered with local landowner Sam Howard of Trace Creek Construction and community leaders. If realized, Prosperity One would not only become Kentucky's first nuclear power plant but could also become the largest nuclear generation site in the United States. Development activities are expected to begin in 2028, encompassing site characterization, geotechnical investigations, environmental assessments, and transmission planning.

The proposal exemplifies the AP1000's growing role in new US nuclear capacity plans. Multiple AP1000 projects are now under consideration across different regions, reflecting utilities' and developers' preference for Westinghouse's large, passively cooled design. The timeline and final configuration will depend on regulatory approvals and financing.
