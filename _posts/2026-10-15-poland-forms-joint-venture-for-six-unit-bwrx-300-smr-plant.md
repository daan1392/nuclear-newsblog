Poland's SGE (formerly Sythos Green Energy) and ZE Pak have agreed to form a joint venture to develop a nuclear power plant at Pątnów featuring up to six BWRX-300 boiling water SMRs. The combined facility would generate 1,800 MWe of capacity.

SGE is pursuing multiple SMR projects across Europe, including another Polish proposal for a total of 26 BWRX-300 units and a UK proposal for 14 units across three sites under the Advanced Nuclear Framework programme. SGE is a co-investor in the GE Vernova Hitachi design. Poland's government has already issued an in-principle decision for these reactor deployments as part of its energy strategy.
