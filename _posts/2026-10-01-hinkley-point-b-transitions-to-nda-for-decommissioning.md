Hinkley Point B nuclear power station in Somerset has been transferred from EDF to the UK's Nuclear Decommissioning Authority (NDA), marking an expansion of the NDA group's decommissioning programme. The transfer brings the shuttered reactor under the government's dedicated decommissioning remit, which manages the clean-up and site restoration of retired nuclear facilities across the UK. NDA Group CEO David Peattie oversaw the formal handover. The move reflects the UK's ongoing effort to systematically address the legacy of its fleet of retired reactors, consolidating operational oversight and funding under a single strategic authority. Hinkley Point B, which closed in 2022 after more than four decades of operation, is now part of a growing NDA portfolio managing some of the country's most complex decommissioning projects.
