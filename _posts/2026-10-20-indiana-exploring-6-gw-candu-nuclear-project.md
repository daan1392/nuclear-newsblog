The State of Indiana, Hoosier Energy Rural Electric Cooperative and AtkinsRéalis Group signed a memorandum of understanding to explore a potential nuclear power project featuring up to eight CANDU reactors with a combined capacity of 6 GW.

The evaluation will assess deployment scenarios for CANDU technology in Indiana and examine potential gas-to-nuclear construction models to accelerate deployment of new nuclear capacity. The parties are considering retired or retiring power plant sites as potential locations.

CANDU reactors, Canada's flagship design, operate in several countries and use natural uranium fuel. The 6 GW project would represent a substantial addition to Indiana's generation capacity. Project timeline will depend on regulatory review, site characterization, financing and technology selection decisions by the parties.
