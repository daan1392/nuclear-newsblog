A study from the Medical University of Vienna suggests that PSMA-PET/CT imaging could help avoid unnecessary prostate biopsies.

An elevated level of prostate-specific antigen (PSA) in the blood can point to prostate cancer but cannot confirm it. When suspicion is high, doctors usually take a tissue sample from the prostate. The Vienna team examined whether a specialised scan, PSMA-PET/CT, could provide important additional information before that step. PSMA-PET is a nuclear medicine technique that uses a radioactive tracer targeting a protein found on prostate cancer cells, combined with CT for anatomical detail.

The finding points towards using the scan earlier in the diagnostic pathway, as a way of deciding who really needs a biopsy. The summary supplied did not give patient numbers or detailed results, so the size of the benefit and how well it would translate into routine practice remain to be seen.
