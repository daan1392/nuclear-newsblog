An International Atomic Energy Agency team carried out a Safety Aspects of Long Term Operation (SALTO) mission to Sweden's Oskarshamn 3 nuclear power unit from 6 to 9 October. The peer review focused on technical and operational practices supporting safe extended operation of the unit, following a similar IAEA mission conducted two years earlier.

Oskarshamn 3 is a 1,450 MWe boiling water reactor commissioned in 1985. Its operating licence and production capacity were extended in 2009, and the plant currently generates approximately 11 terawatt-hours of electricity annually. The SALTO review team consisted of two IAEA staff members and two experts from Argentina and the Netherlands, providing international peer perspective on ageing management, maintenance strategies, and regulatory oversight.

IAEA SALTO missions provide technical reviews to help operators and regulators ensure that nuclear facilities undergoing life extension programmes maintain high safety standards. As reactor fleets worldwide pursue long-term operation to maximize low-carbon electricity production, systematic assessment of ageing management and operational safety remains critical to sustained safe performance.
