French small modular reactor developer Calogena has been awarded €40 million through the European Commission's Innovation Fund to support the potential construction of its first commercial nuclear reactor in Pierrelatte, southern France. The funding underscores EU backing for diverse advanced reactor pathways as part of Europe's clean energy transition.

Calogena is developing molten salt reactor technology and aims to deploy a demonstration unit at an existing nuclear fuel cycle site, leveraging existing infrastructure and workforce. The Innovation Fund supports the commercialization of innovative zero-emission technologies at scale.

The award reflects the EU's recognition that achieving decarbonization targets will require multiple reactor technologies alongside traditional large light-water reactors. French support for SMR development, combined with European funding mechanisms, signals commitment to enabling a portfolio approach to nuclear energy across the continent.
