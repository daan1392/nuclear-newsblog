South Korea's Nuclear Safety and Security Commission approved extended operation of Kori Nuclear Power Plant's units 3 and 4, allowing the two 1,025-MWe Westinghouse pressurised water reactors to operate for an additional decade. Unit 3 received a license extension to September 2034, while unit 4 is now licensed to August 2035—each reactor operating well beyond its original 40-year design life that expired in 2024 and 2025 respectively. Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power announced plans to restart both units during the first half of 2027. The units are currently offline. Extended operation of mature reactor fleets has become increasingly significant in Asia-Pacific strategy, particularly as countries seek to maximize existing nuclear assets while new build projects advance. South Korea's approval reflects confidence in aging reactor management, though specific safety upgrades or modernization requirements were not detailed in the announcement.
