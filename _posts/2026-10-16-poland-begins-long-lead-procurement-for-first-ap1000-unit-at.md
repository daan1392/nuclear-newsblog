Polskie Elektrownie Jądrowe (PEJ), Poland's state nuclear company, commissioned Westinghouse Electric to begin procuring long-lead items (LLIs) for the first AP1000 unit at the planned Lubiatowo-Kopalino nuclear power plant. Long-lead procurement is a critical scheduling activity, ensuring that components with extended manufacturing timelines are ordered well ahead of construction needs.

Poland's first commercial nuclear power plant project represents a major strategic commitment to decarbonisation and energy independence following the country's transition away from coal. The AP1000's standardised design and proven construction experience at Vogtle make it an attractive choice for demonstrating nuclear deployment in Central Europe.

Initiation of long-lead procurement signals that the project is transitioning from planning and licensing phases toward construction preparation. Successful execution of Poland's first nuclear project is expected to encourage subsequent deployments and to establish supply chain and workforce capabilities for the region.
