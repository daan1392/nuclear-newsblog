Unit 1 at Turkey's Akkuyu nuclear power plant successfully completed hydraulic tests of the primary circuit systems and equipment, a major milestone in the path to initial criticality and commercial operation. During testing, primary circuit pressure was raised to 24.5 MPa—approximately 1.5 times the reactor's normal operating pressure—while water temperature reached 135°C at the initial stage.

Specialists verified that all equipment met design requirements and functional specifications throughout the pressure and temperature transients. The completed hydraulic tests clear the pathway for hot functional tests, in which the reactor's systems are operated at elevated temperature and pressure, further validating readiness for fuel loading and criticality.

Akkuyu unit 1 is the first of four planned Rosatom VVER-1200 reactors at the Turkish site. Progress toward criticality underscores the project's advancement despite geopolitical complexities and represents a major addition to capacity in a country working to diversify its generation mix.
