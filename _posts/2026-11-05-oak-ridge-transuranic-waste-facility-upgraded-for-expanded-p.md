The Department of Energy's Office of Environmental Management announced upgrades to the Transuranic Waste Processing Center (TWPC) at Oak Ridge Reservation, specifically modernizing the cask processing enclosure (CPE) with new equipment. These upgrades expand the facility's ability to process different forms of transuranic waste to support efficient site cleanup operations.

The TWPC comprises four functional areas: contact-handled glovebox lines, a hot cell facility for remote handling, a box breakdown area, and the newly upgraded CPE for receiving and processing waste containers. Transuranic waste—material contaminated with alpha-emitting elements with atomic number greater than uranium—requires specialized handling and processing before shipment to the Waste Isolation Pilot Plant in New Mexico.

The expanded processing capability at Oak Ridge accelerates waste characterization and packaging, directly supporting the Department's environmental remediation mission and allowing the site to manage its legacy waste inventory more efficiently.
