The Nuclear Regulatory Commission finalized a rule on October 22 permitting commercial nuclear reactor applicants to utilize analyses, test data, and other technical evidence from reactor demonstrations previously authorized by the Department of Energy or Department of War to support their license applications.

The rule establishes a streamlined pathway for applicants whose reactor designs have already undergone government demonstration programs. Rather than requiring duplication of testing and analysis, the new framework allows direct incorporation of existing technical evidence into NRC commercial licensing reviews.

This regulatory change is intended to accelerate the licensing timeline for advanced reactor designs and reduce duplicative work between government demonstration initiatives and the NRC's commercial review process. The measure reflects ongoing efforts to create efficient pathways for new reactor technologies to reach commercialization while maintaining safety and security standards.
