The Space Nuclear Power Corporation (SpaceNukes) received an Other Transaction Agreement from the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency to design, build, and conduct a ground test of a kilopower-class reactor in collaboration with Los Alamos National Laboratory. The compact reactor concept targets applications requiring autonomous, long-duration power in remote environments.

Kilopower reactors, typically in the single-to-tens-of-kilowatt electrical output range, were initially developed for deep space exploration and lunar surface operations but have applications in terrestrial remote locations, disaster response, and military installations. The technology employs advanced heat pipes and compact cores to minimize size and complexity while maintaining safety and controllability.

DARPA's support signals continued investment in nuclear technologies for defense and space missions. The ground test will validate reactor performance, safety systems, and operational procedures. Success with the kilopower platform could expand options for autonomous power in applications where terrestrial infrastructure is absent or compromised, from Arctic research stations to remote mining sites.
