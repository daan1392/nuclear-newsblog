Framatome inaugurated a EUR 30 million (USD 33.5 million) industrial pilot facility dedicated to scaling production of its PROtect Enhanced Accident Tolerant Fuel technology at its manufacturing plant in Paimboeuf, western France. The facility applies an innovative chromium coating via physical vapour deposition to zirconium alloy cladding tubes used in nuclear fuel assemblies. Accident-tolerant fuels enhance reactor safety margins by improving cladding oxidation resistance and mechanical properties during beyond-design-basis scenarios. The pilot facility represents a major step toward commercial deployment, with planned capacity to exceed 100,000 chromium-coated tubes annually, supporting both existing reactor fleet modernization and future new build projects.
