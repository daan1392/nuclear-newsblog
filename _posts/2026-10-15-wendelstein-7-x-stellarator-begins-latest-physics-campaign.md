Wendelstein 7-X (W7-X), the stellarator operated by the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics in Greifswald, Germany, has begun its OP2.4 experimental campaign, which will run until mid-December. The campaign involves more than 100 scientists from approximately 20 European laboratories.

The stellarator concept confines plasma in a twisted, three-dimensional magnetic field, providing inherent stability without requiring a strong electric current through the plasma—a key advantage over tokamak designs. Each experimental campaign aims to improve understanding of stellarator physics and push performance boundaries. This campaign will attempt to advance beyond results achieved in earlier operational phases.
