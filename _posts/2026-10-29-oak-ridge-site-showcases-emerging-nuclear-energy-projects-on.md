Department of Energy Assistant Secretary Tim Walsh gathered with industry representatives on 27 October at the former Oak Ridge Gaseous Diffusion Plant site to showcase several nuclear energy initiatives under development in the area. The meeting highlighted the potential for repurposing one of the nation's historic uranium enrichment facilities as a center for new nuclear energy projects. Converting decommissioned nuclear fuel cycle sites into hubs for advanced reactor deployment and related industrial applications leverages existing infrastructure, skilled workforces, and site characterization data while accelerating decarbonization goals. The convening underscores federal strategy to enable commercial nuclear development on remediated government-owned nuclear sites.
