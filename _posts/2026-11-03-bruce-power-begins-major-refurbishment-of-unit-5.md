Bruce Power's fifth reactor at its Ontario facility entered a Major Component Replacement (MCR) outage on November 1, marking the fourth unit to undergo renewal in a privately funded modernization programme targeting six of the station's eight units.

The MCR project aims to extend the operational life of units 3 through 8 and maintain reliable baseload power generation for Ontario's electricity system and medical isotope production. Units 6 and 3 have already completed refurbishment and are operating with strong performance, while unit 4 recently set performance records during its MCR outage.

These refurbishments represent a significant capital investment by Bruce Power to sustain Canada's largest electricity generating station and ensure continued supply of cobalt-60 and other medical isotopes produced as reactor byproducts. The programme demonstrates the economic viability of extending the operational life of existing CANDU reactors through systematic component replacement.
