The operator of the Chornobyl Nuclear Power Plant reported steady progress on preparing its three undamaged reactor units for the next phase of decommissioning, with all preparatory work due by the end of 2028.

The 1986 accident destroyed unit 4. Units 1 to 3 were not damaged and kept running, and the last of them shut down 14 years later. According to the operator's update, preparatory work is 94% complete at unit 2 and 44% complete at unit 1. Planning is under way for unit 3, with physical work scheduled to begin in 2027.

The preparatory work covers decommissioning systems and components inside the conservation zone premises. It also includes removing thermal insulation and combustible materials, and removing power, signalling and control cables.

The figures show slow but measurable progress at a site that has been shut for decades and now operates in a difficult wartime setting.
