Hot testing has begun at the ACP100 small modular reactor demonstration project under construction at Changjiang in Hainan province, China. The tests simulate the temperatures and pressures the reactor systems will experience during normal operation, marking progress toward commissioning of the unit.

The ACP100 is a Chinese-designed compact reactor intended for district heating, desalination, and power generation in remote locations. The Changjiang site hosts the demonstration unit as part of China's push to develop domestically manufactured SMRs for both domestic deployment and export markets.
