Quantum Leap Energy, a subsidiary of ASP Isotopes Inc, and South Africa's state-owned nuclear energy corporation Necsa announced the formation of a joint venture to develop, finance, build and operate facilities for producing high-assay low-enriched uranium (HALEU) at Necsa's Pelindaba site.

HALEU—uranium enriched to between 5 and 20 percent U-235—is required as fuel for many advanced reactor designs, including high-temperature gas reactors and some small modular reactors. Domestic HALEU production has been identified as critical infrastructure for expanding the global nuclear fleet.

The partnership will initially develop a test bench facility followed by commercial-scale production capacity. The collaboration brings together Necsa's nuclear infrastructure and expertise with Quantum Leap Energy's enrichment technology, positioning South Africa as a potential regional supplier of HALEU for African and international markets.
