The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission issued a construction permit for Dow's Long Mott Generating Station in Seadrift, Texas, marking a historic milestone: the first construction permit for a high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGCR) issued in the United States in nearly six decades. The 320 megawatt-electric project will feature four X-energy Xe-100 reactors at Dow's UCC Seadrift manufacturing facility.

X-energy CEO praised the achievement, attributing it to thorough NRC work, Congressional reforms implemented over the preceding six years, and the efforts of the X-energy and Dow teams. The permit approval demonstrates regulatory pathways are now accessible for advanced SMR designs that differ significantly from light-water reactor technology.

The Xe-100's helium cooling and pebble-bed design offer inherent safety characteristics and potential applications in industrial heat demand. This permit signals the NRC's capacity to evaluate and license innovative reactor technologies, potentially accelerating deployment of the growing SMR fleet planned across US industrial and grid applications.
