The Canadian Space Agency released a request for information seeking input from industry and academia on the nation's technologies and capabilities for lunar microreactors. The RFI aims to identify areas where Canada has differentiated or strategically valuable expertise as fission power becomes complementary to solar and batteries for lunar missions.

The agency specified that proposed technologies must be at technology readiness level 2 or higher, which could include complete reactor systems or subsystems. Fission power is seen as essential for long-duration lunar exploration and eventual crewed operations where solar generation becomes intermittent or insufficient.

Canada's space sector has existing expertise in robotics, materials science, and reactor design that could position the country as a supplier of advanced power systems for international lunar exploration programs.
