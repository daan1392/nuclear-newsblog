Scientists at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory developed a machine learning tool capable of detecting and estimating past radiation exposure using data from a complete blood count (CBC), one of the most routinely performed clinical blood tests.

The method leverages standard hematological parameters—red blood cells, white blood cells, platelets and related measurements—to identify signatures of prior radiation exposure. A CBC is inexpensive and widely available in clinical settings worldwide, making this approach potentially valuable for medical surveillance and emergency response scenarios.

The tool could aid in rapid triage following radiological incidents, help occupational health monitoring in radiation-exposed workforces, and support epidemiological studies. The technique complements existing biological dosimetry methods such as chromosome aberration analysis, offering a complementary tool for radiation dose reconstruction.
