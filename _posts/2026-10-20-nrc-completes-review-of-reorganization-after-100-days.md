The Nuclear Regulatory Commission met with its five commissioners at the NRC Region IV office in Arlington, Texas, to assess the outcomes of a major structural reorganization implemented approximately 100 days earlier.

The reorganization centered on restructuring around core business lines and established two new offices: the Office of the Chief Nuclear Reactor Inspector (CNRI) and the Office of Advanced Reactors. All four regional offices now report to the CNRI, consolidating inspection oversight and coordination.

Commissioners welcomed the opportunity to meet at a regional office to observe firsthand how the changes affected field staff operations. The reorganization addresses the NRC's need to modernize its structure as the nuclear sector expands beyond traditional large light-water reactors to include small modular reactors, advanced designs, and increased back-end activities.
