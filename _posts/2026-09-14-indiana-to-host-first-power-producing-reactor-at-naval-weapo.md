A commercially owned and operated micromodular reactor (MMR) is to be installed at Naval Weapons Station Crane, making it the first power-producing reactor in Indiana and the US Navy's first shore-based advanced micromodular reactor.

The US Department of War described the installation as providing round-the-clock power independent of the commercial grid, able to sustain critical operations in demanding conditions. It called the deployment the first step in a wider department-wide effort to use nuclear power for national security.

The project is being carried out through the Army's Janus Program and implements two executive orders. The department describes Crane as one of the country's premier hubs for warfighting innovation and capability development.

Microreactors are small units intended for remote or security-sensitive sites where a reliable, self-contained supply matters more than grid connection. Military installations have become an early target market for the technology.
