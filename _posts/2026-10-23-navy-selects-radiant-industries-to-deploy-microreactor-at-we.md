The U.S. Department of the Navy selected Radiant Industries to design, build, and deliver a prototype microreactor power plant featuring the company's Kaleidos design for deployment at Naval Weapons Station Crane in Indiana. Microreactors are compact, factory-built nuclear power systems designed for remote or distributed applications where conventional grid connection is impractical.

The Kaleidos design represents advances in small modular reactor technology tailored for military and industrial end-use applications. Such systems offer benefits including reduced capital requirements, smaller physical footprints, and potential for enhanced security and self-sufficiency at military installations.

This deployment marks a significant step in validating microreactor technology for real-world applications beyond research and development phases. The Navy's selection reflects growing confidence in advanced reactor designs and their potential to meet diverse energy requirements in both military and civilian contexts.
