China General Nuclear announced the pouring of first concrete for unit 4 at the San'ao nuclear power plant in Zhejiang Province on 10 November, marking the official start of main construction and the full-scale construction phase of the project's second phase.

Unit 4 will be a Hualong One pressurised water reactor, China's advanced reactor design. The San'ao site's first phase comprises units 1 and 2, which received construction approval from China's National Nuclear Safety Administration on 30 December 2020, with unit 1's first concrete poured the following day. Unit 3 is also under construction, making San'ao a multi-unit site supporting the expansion of China's nuclear generation capacity.

The Hualong One design continues to gain operational experience from units already in operation and under construction across China. San'ao's development reflects sustained investment in nuclear power as a cornerstone of China's energy strategy.
