Saltfoss (formerly Seaborg), a Danish developer of molten salt power barges, has notified the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission of its intent to begin pre-application engagement toward a commercial operating license. The company plans to submit its Regulatory Engagement Plan to the NRC in the first quarter of 2027.

Saltfoss aims to become the first advanced reactor developer licensed under Part 53, the NRC's new risk-informed, performance-based regulatory framework that entered force in March 2026. If successful, it would also be the first advanced maritime small modular reactor licensed anywhere globally. The molten salt design represents a distinct technical approach offering inherent safety features and potential advantages for remote or maritime deployment.

Part 53 was designed to streamline licensing for non-light-water reactor technologies, reducing regulatory timelines compared to legacy licensing pathways. Saltfoss's pursuit of this route represents an important test case for the framework's practical application.
