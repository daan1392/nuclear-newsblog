American Electric Power (AEP), X-energy and Amazon announced a joint development agreement to evaluate a 960 MW small modular reactor project at a former coal-fired power plant site in Sullivan County, Indiana. The project would deploy 12 of X-energy's Xe-100 high-temperature gas-cooled reactors arranged in three groups of four, each producing 80 MW.

Amazon will support the project through development funding and a long-term power purchase agreement intended to cover facility costs and operations, providing financial certainty for the utility to advance the initiative. The companies said the project could create more than 1,000 construction jobs and over 100 permanent positions if it reaches a final investment decision.

The proposal marks a significant example of technology companies securing long-term, carbon-free electricity to meet surging data center and artificial intelligence demands, while offering existing nuclear operators new commercial pathways. Sullivan County has become part of Indiana's Advanced Nuclear Region, signaling state-level support for the nuclear sector. The project remains in early stages with no final investment decision yet made.
