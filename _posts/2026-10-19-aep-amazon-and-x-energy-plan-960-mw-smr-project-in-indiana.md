American Electric Power and X-energy signed a joint development agreement with financial support from Amazon to evaluate a potential 960 MW small modular reactor project at the former Breed coal-fired power plant in Sullivan County, Indiana.

The project would deploy twelve of X-energy's Xe-100 high-temperature, gas-cooled reactors, each rated at 80 MW. Amazon is supporting development through a funding agreement and a long-term power purchase agreement designed to cover facility costs and operations for the duration of the contract.

The partnership exemplifies a growing model where technology companies secure long-term, decarbonized electricity supplies to power data centers and AI operations, while providing utilities and developers with the financial certainty needed to justify investment in nuclear infrastructure. The companies estimate the project could create over 1,000 construction jobs and more than 100 permanent positions. The project remains in early feasibility stages with no final investment decision yet reached.
