Kairos Power has commissioned an automated quality control and tracking system at its Manufacturing Development Campus in Albuquerque, New Mexico, to inspect and verify specifications for each fuel pebble while engraving unique identification codes for traceability throughout testing and operation. The facility supports Kairos Power's iterative fuel development approach and enhances manufacturing accountability.

The system streamlines production throughput while generating detailed data on fuel pebble characteristics that can inform future manufacturing and handling protocols. Unique traceability enables precise tracking of fuel performance and helps optimize designs for commercial deployment.

Kairos Power is developing the KP-FHR (Kairos Power Fluoride salt-cooled High-temperature Reactor), which uses molten fluoride salt coolant circulated through pebble beds containing TRISO (tristructural isotropic) fuel particles. TRISO fuel's layered ceramic design provides inherent safety margins, and automated quality systems are critical to ensuring consistent fuel performance at scale. The manufacturing investment reflects progress toward commercial readiness for advanced reactor technologies.
