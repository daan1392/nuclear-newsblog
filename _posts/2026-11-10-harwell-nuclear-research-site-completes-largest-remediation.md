Nuclear Restoration Services completed its largest remediation project to date at the Harwell site in Oxfordshire, restoring 4.2 hectares of land that had been used for liquid effluent treatment operations for more than 60 years. The work involved removing over 97,000 tonnes of material from the former Liquid Effluent Treatment Plant.

Harwell, established in 1946 as Britain's first Atomic Energy Research Establishment, hosted five research reactors and numerous nuclear research facilities, including plutonium handling operations and radioactive waste management systems. The site is now progressing through a comprehensive decommissioning programme to safely restore its historic facilities.

The completion of this remediation phase demonstrates the practical application of modern decommissioning techniques and represents significant progress in returning the site to productive use. Restored land can now be considered for alternative industrial or environmental purposes as the broader Harwell decommissioning continues.
