Studsvik, a Swedish provider of nuclear technical services, has agreed to supply technical information and design guidance to Japanese nuclear engineering startup ReNu Industries for a Japanese adaptation of Studsvik's inDRUM technology. The system treats problematic radioactive wastes through controlled incineration and specialized processing, reducing waste volumes for storage and disposal.

The deployment supports ongoing decommissioning and cleanup efforts at Fukushima Daiichi following the 2011 accident. Specialized waste treatment technologies are critical for managing the diverse contaminated materials generated during site remediation, including contaminated soil, structural debris, and other materials requiring volume reduction and conditioning for safe interim storage or final disposal.

The collaboration combines Studsvik's proven waste processing expertise with Japanese regulatory and operational knowledge, allowing adaptation of proven international technology to Japan's specific waste management requirements and infrastructure.
