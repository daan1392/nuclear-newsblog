Westinghouse announced a $1 billion investment to construct a new fuel fabrication plant at its Columbia Fuel Fabrication Facility in Hopkins, South Carolina, specializing in low-enriched uranium fuel enriched to 8 percent U-235 (LEU+). The expanded facility is designed to produce 600 metric tons of uranium annually beginning in 2030. Pre-construction work is complete and the company has been coordinating with the Nuclear Regulatory Commission since 2024 to amend the site's operating license. LEU+ fuel, enriched higher than conventional fuel but below weapons-usable levels, is gaining traction for advanced reactor designs including some SMRs and research reactor conversions. The investment demonstrates Westinghouse's commitment to establishing domestic capacity for next-generation fuel products as reactor demand grows.
