Bangladesh's Rooppur Nuclear Power Plant Unit 1 achieved criticality on 11 October, marking a significant milestone in the country's nuclear programme. The self-sustaining nuclear reaction was confirmed at 15:34 local time, three days after the national atomic regulator granted approval to proceed.

The achievement represents a major step toward diversifying Bangladesh's electricity generation and enhancing energy security. Rooppur is designed to strengthen low-carbon energy supplies while reducing reliance on fossil fuels. Following criticality, the plant must complete a series of safety verification tests and meet other operational requirements before full commissioning.

The project had faced delays over several years, but the successful reach of criticality signals progress toward commercial operation. Officials have emphasized that the plant will play a crucial role in meeting the country's growing electricity demand and supporting broader development goals.
