Cameco agreed to purchase all of the future production of the planned Paducah Laser Enrichment Facility in Kentucky under an exclusive offtake agreement with Global Laser Enrichment (GLE).

Silex Systems announced the deal. GLE was formed in 2007 to commercialise the SILEX laser-based uranium enrichment process in the United States, and it holds the exclusive licence for the technology. Silex, the Australian inventor of the process, owns 51% of GLE, and Cameco owns the other 49%.

An offtake agreement commits a buyer to take a facility's output, which can help a developer secure financing and demonstrate commercial demand. The deal comes as Western utilities look for enrichment capacity beyond existing suppliers. Laser enrichment is a newer route than the centrifuges used in most commercial plants, and the Paducah facility has yet to be built.
