Idaho National Laboratory's National Reactor Innovation Center (NRIC) and Purdue University's Applied Research Institute (PARI) have announced a collaboration to demonstrate advanced construction techniques aimed at reducing the cost and technical risk of new reactor deployment. The project, which also includes the Electric Power Research Institute and the Nuclear Energy Institute, is part of NRIC's Advanced Construction Technology Initiative (ACTI). By piloting innovative building methods and processes, the partnership seeks to identify and validate approaches that can be applied across the reactor industry. This effort addresses a critical barrier to cost-competitive nuclear power: construction efficiency. Successful demonstration of cost-saving techniques could accelerate economic viability of both large new reactors and small modular reactors.
