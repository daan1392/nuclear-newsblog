An opening ceremony was held for ATLab Valenciennes, developed by Orano subsidiary Orano Med in Valenciennes, France—Europe's first industrial-scale facility for producing lead-212-based targeted alpha therapies.

The facility is designed to produce up to 10,000 doses per year of next-generation cancer treatments. Targeted alpha therapy combines an alpha-emitting isotope like lead-212 with a protein or antibody that specifically targets cancer cells, delivering destructive radiation while minimizing damage to surrounding healthy tissue.

Lead-212 is an emerging isotope in nuclear medicine, offering advantages over traditional treatment approaches through its precise cellular targeting capability. The new facility positions Europe to scale production of these therapies and reduce dependence on imports, supporting broader clinical adoption across the continent.

This development represents progress in the emerging field of therapeutic nuclear medicine, where radioisotopes enable increasingly sophisticated treatment strategies for difficult-to-treat malignancies.
