The European Commission awarded a €40 million grant to French SMR developer Calogena through its Innovation Fund, marking the first time the fund has financed construction of a commercial small modular reactor. The support follows earlier backing from the French state in 2026.

Calogena, a subsidiary of the Gorgé Group, is developing SMRs for district heating and other applications. The EU grant remains conditional on Calogena obtaining exploitation rights for the Pierrelatte district heating system. The award signals growing European institutional support for domestic SMR development, following earlier backing from the European Investment Bank for Finnish developer Steady Energy, and reflects a shift toward demonstrating commercial viability of advanced nuclear technologies.
