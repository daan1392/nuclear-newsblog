Bruce Power initiated the Major Component Replacement (MCR) outage for unit 5 at its eight-unit Ontario generating station on November 1, marking the fourth reactor to undergo systematic refurbishment under the company's plan to renew six units. The MCR process involves replacement of major internal components, including steam generators and other long-lived equipment, extending operational life.

Units 6 and 3 have already completed refurbishment and returned to service with strong performance records, while unit 4 recently set records during its MCR outage. The phased approach distributes project execution and allows the company to apply lessons learned from successive units.

Bruce's MCR program is privately funded and designed to maintain clean, reliable generation capacity for Ontario's electricity system while supporting medical isotope production—Bruce is a major supplier of medical cobalt-60. Life extension through major refurbishment represents a cost-effective pathway to decarbonization compared to new construction.
