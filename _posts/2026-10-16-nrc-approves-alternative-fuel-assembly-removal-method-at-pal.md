The tilted assembly resulted from a fuel loading incident that required immediate corrective action.

The approval demonstrates the NRC's rapid licensing process for safety-critical issues and Holtec's engineering response to resolve the situation. Standard fuel assembly removal procedures may not accommodate tilted configurations, necessitating specialized equipment design. The emergency approval allowed resolution without prolonged operational constraints.

Palisades, a Holtec-operated pressurized water reactor, returned to commercial operation in 2023 after a period of derating and modifications. The fuel handling incident and its resolution underscore the importance of procedural rigor and contingency planning in reactor operations.
