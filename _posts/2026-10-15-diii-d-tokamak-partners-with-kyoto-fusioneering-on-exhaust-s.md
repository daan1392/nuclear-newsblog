The DIII-D National Fusion Facility has announced a partnership with Kyoto Fusioneering to design, manufacture, install, and operate a new power exhaust system in the DIII-D tokamak. The collaboration aims to advance negative triangularity plasma shape approaches toward commercial viability.

Power exhaust—removing heat from the plasma without damaging reactor walls—is a critical challenge for future fusion reactors. The partnership builds on an earlier agreement between Kyoto Fusioneering and Oak Ridge National Laboratory, indicating growing private-sector involvement in solving core fusion engineering problems.
