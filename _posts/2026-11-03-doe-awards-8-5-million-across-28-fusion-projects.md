The Department of Energy's Office of Science announced $8.5 million in awards across 28 projects through the Innovation Network for Fusion Energy (INFUSE) programme, which provides fusion companies access to capabilities at US national laboratories and universities. INFUSE aims to help fusion developers overcome critical scientific and technological hurdles by connecting private sector innovation with government research infrastructure and expertise. The awards support a range of fusion approaches and development stages, reflecting the DOE's strategy to foster a diverse commercial fusion sector while leveraging public research capabilities.
