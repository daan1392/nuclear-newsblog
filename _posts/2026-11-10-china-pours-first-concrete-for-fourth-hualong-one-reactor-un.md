China General Nuclear announced the first concrete pour for the nuclear island of unit 4 at the San'ao nuclear power plant in Zhejiang province, marking the official start of main construction for the second phase of the project. The pour occurred on November 10 and signals the transition to full-scale construction activity.

San'ao is building four Hualong One units across two phases. The State Council approved units 1 and 2 as the first phase in September 2020, with the National Nuclear Safety Administration issuing construction permits in December 2020. Unit 1's first concrete was poured in late December 2020. This latest milestone for unit 4 reflects China's continued expansion of indigenous reactor capacity and the Hualong One design's deployment across multiple sites.
