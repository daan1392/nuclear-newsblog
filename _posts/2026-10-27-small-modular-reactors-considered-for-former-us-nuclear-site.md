Dairyland Power Cooperative and Applied Atomics announced a letter of intent to explore deploying small modular reactors at Dairyland's La Crosse nuclear power plant site in Genoa, Wisconsin. Separately, Vermont officials have publicly discussed SMR deployment prospects at a former reactor site in the state.

These initiatives reflect growing interest in reusing existing nuclear plant locations for advanced reactor deployment. Former nuclear sites often have established electrical interconnections, cooling water access, trained workforces familiar with nuclear operations, and regulatory relationships with local authorities—factors that can reduce development timelines and costs compared to greenfield sites.

SMRs, typically designed for outputs of 300 MWe or less, can be deployed in modular fashion and offer flexibility for remote locations, industrial heat applications, or smaller electricity grids. Repurposing decommissioned sites preserves nuclear infrastructure expertise while contributing to regional energy security.
