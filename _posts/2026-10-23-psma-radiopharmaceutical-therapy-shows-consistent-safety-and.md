Analysis of outcomes from a French early-access program confirms the safety and efficacy of prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA)-targeted radiopharmaceutical therapy for patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC). The study, representing the largest real-world cohort examined to date, shows results consistent with findings from the VISION clinical trial.

PSMA-targeted radiopharmaceuticals deliver therapeutic radiation directly to cancer cells expressing the PSMA biomarker, minimizing exposure to healthy tissue. The real-world data provide important validation of the therapy's benefit-risk profile and offer insights into patient selection criteria and usage trends in actual clinical settings.

These findings support broader adoption of PSMA theranostics in oncology practice. As nuclear medicine increasingly integrates targeted radionuclide therapy with imaging diagnostics, therapies like this demonstrate the clinical value of precision medicine approaches in treating advanced cancers.
