The Nuclear Regulatory Commission approved Westinghouse's renewal and update of the AP1000 standard design certification, establishing Vogtle unit 4 as the reference plant for future U.S. AP1000 deployments. The renewal allows Westinghouse to incorporate operational experience and lessons learned from the construction of Vogtle units 3 and 4 in Georgia into the certified design baseline.

Design certification renewal is a critical milestone for scaling advanced reactors. Each successive deployment benefits from construction experience, supply chain development, and workforce familiarity with the design. By establishing a post-Vogtle reference design, the NRC aims to enable faster, more efficient licensing and construction of future AP1000 units—a key goal as the U.S. seeks to quadruple nuclear capacity over the coming decades.

The AP1000 has emerged as one of the leading large advanced reactor designs for U.S. new build, with strong international deployment in China and ongoing interest from utilities. The design certification renewal supports Westinghouse's commercial strategy and signals regulatory confidence in the design's maturity and safety basis.
