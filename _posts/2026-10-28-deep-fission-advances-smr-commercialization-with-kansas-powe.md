Deep Fission announced a master energy purchase agreement with the Great Plains Development Authority in Kansas, marking a step forward in the company's transition from research and testing toward commercial electricity production. The DOE-backed startup is developing advanced small modular reactors.

The agreement timing comes as Deep Fission manages fallout from a short-seller report that questioned the company's commercial viability and raised concerns about the accuracy of some of its prior statements. The company's management responded to the report and has continued advancing its development program.

Power purchase agreements are critical milestones for SMR developers, providing evidence of customer demand and revenue certainty needed to secure financing and demonstrate commercial viability. Such agreements typically specify power output, delivery schedules, and pricing terms, and help support regulatory and financial approval processes required for reactor deployment.
