Rosatom has completed manufacturing of the reactor internals and reactor head for the first unit at Egypt's El Dabaa Nuclear Power Plant, representing a significant milestone in the multi-unit project's construction. The components, produced at Rosatom's Izhora facility in St. Petersburg, total approximately 340 tonnes and will be shipped to Egypt for installation. Combined with the previously assembled reactor vessel, the complete set of major components exceeds 670 tonnes. Installation of these internals marks a transition to subsequent construction phases, with workers able to proceed with remaining systems integration once components are positioned. The El Dabaa project represents a major commitment by Egypt to expand its clean energy generation capacity through nuclear power, with plans for multiple VVER-1200 units at the coastal site.
