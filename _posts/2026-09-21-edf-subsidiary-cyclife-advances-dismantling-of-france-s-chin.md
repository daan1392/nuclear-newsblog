EDF subsidiary Cyclife said the Chinon A decommissioning project has reached a milestone with the validation of the contractual strategy for dismantling the heat exchangers at Chinon A2.

Chinon A2 is one of six gas-cooled reactors of the UNGG type (Uranium Naturel Graphite Gaz, natural uranium with graphite moderator and gas coolant) that EDF is decommissioning at Bugey, Chinon and Saint-Laurent-des-Eaux. The six entered service between 1963 and 1972. Apart from Chinon A1, which closed in 1973, all ran for more than 20 years.

Chinon A2 is the lead site for EDF's strategy of immediate dismantling for its UNGG fleet. The heat exchanger work is an early step toward physically taking the first of these reactors apart.

The UNGG fleet is a decommissioning challenge because of its distinctive graphite-moderated design. Progress at the lead site is likely to shape the approach taken at the others.
