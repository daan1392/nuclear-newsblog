The budget builds on momentum from 2025 initiatives and designates 28 major projects for accelerated review through the federal Major Projects Office, including Ontario Power Generation's Darlington New Nuclear project—one of the first nuclear projects referred for fast-track consideration. This commitment reflects Canada's recognition of nuclear energy's essential role in meeting growing electricity demand, supporting industrial development, and achieving net-zero emissions targets.
