Saltstone Disposal Unit (SDU) 6 at the Savannah River Site near Aiken, South Carolina, has been completely filled with grouted legacy tank waste, according to the Department of Energy. This marks the first of seven mega-volume disposal units approved in 2012 to reach full capacity.

SDU 6 began accepting waste material in 2018 and has processed grouted waste from some of the 51 carbon-steel tanks that have stored radioactive material at the site for decades. The disposal unit represents a significant step in the multi-decade cleanup effort to remediate legacy nuclear weapons production wastes.

The completion of the first mega-volume unit demonstrates progress in addressing one of the nation's largest stockpiles of legacy radioactive waste. The successful operation validates the grouting and disposal approach for the remaining six units. Full remediation of the Savannah River Site remains a long-term effort, but the continued processing and disposition of tank waste is essential to site cleanup and eventual restoration.
