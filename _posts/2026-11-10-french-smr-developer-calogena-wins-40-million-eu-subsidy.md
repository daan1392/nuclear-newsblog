Calogena, a small modular reactor developer and subsidiary of industrial group Gorgé, received €40 million in support from the European Commission's Innovation Fund—a first for the EU backing commercial SMR construction. The subsidy follows earlier French government support in 2026.

The funding comes with conditions: Calogena must secure exploitation rights for district heating at Pierrelatte, a French site where the company plans its first commercial unit. The award reflects growing EU investment in advanced reactor technologies as part of climate and energy security strategies.

The subsidy signals shifting EU policy toward tangible support for domestic SMR commercialization, following earlier backing from the European Investment Bank for Finland's Steady Energy. European regulators and policymakers increasingly view SMRs as strategic infrastructure for industrial heat applications and grid decarbonization, justifying direct financial support alongside market development mechanisms.
