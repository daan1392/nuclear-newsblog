Centrus Energy completed and shipped the first enrichment centrifuge built at its Technology and Manufacturing Center in Oak Ridge, Tennessee to its uranium enrichment facility in Piketon, Ohio, demonstrating progress in rebuilding a domestic fuel cycle supply chain. The shipment on 29 October represents the company's capacity to manufacture the specialised equipment at scale in the United States, connecting manufacturing in Tennessee with enrichment operations in Ohio. Centrus has been expanding its Piketon facility and received NRC authorisation in September 2025 to enrich uranium to 10% U-235 (LEU+), which enables new fuel options for both conventional and advanced reactors. The company stated this achievement reflects assembly of the technical expertise and domestic suppliers needed to sustain enrichment production.
