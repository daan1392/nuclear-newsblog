Urenco USA has successfully produced its first commercial cylinder of LEU+—uranium enriched to levels between 5% and 10% uranium-235—at its National Enrichment Facility in New Mexico. The company states this represents the first commercial LEU+ production in the Western world, opening a new era of fuel options for reactor operators.

The NRC authorized Urenco USA to enrich uranium up to 10% U-235 in September 2025, and all the facility's gas centrifuge cascades are now licensed for LEU+ production. LEU+ occupies an intermediate enrichment level above standard low-enriched uranium (≤5% U-235) but below highly enriched fuel, enabling certain reactor designs to operate more efficiently or achieve higher burnup with extended cycle lengths.

While Russia and China have previously explored LEU+ as a fuel option, Urenco's commercial production represents a significant expansion of Western supply diversity and supports interest from utilities and advanced reactor developers seeking alternative fuel specifications.
