An international donor conference organized in Paris by France, Ukraine, and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development raised €303 million in pledges from 14 countries and international organizations to fund repairs of Chernobyl's New Safe Confinement (NSC) structure. The New Safe Confinement was damaged by a Russian drone attack on 14 February 2025.

Repair works are estimated to require at least €500 million total, with the pledged funds contributing to the initial phase of stabilization and restoration. The confinement structure, completed in 2016, was designed to isolate reactor unit 4's damaged core and surrounding contaminated materials from the environment for approximately 100 years.

The international response demonstrates coordinated commitment to maintaining Chernobyl's long-term safety and environmental protection despite ongoing geopolitical challenges. Successful repair and restoration of the confinement system remains critical to containing residual radioactivity and protecting regional water resources and ecosystems.
