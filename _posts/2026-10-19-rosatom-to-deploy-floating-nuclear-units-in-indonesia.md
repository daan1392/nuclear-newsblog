Russia's state nuclear corporation Rosatom signed an agreement during Russian Energy Week in Moscow to deploy floating nuclear power units in Indonesia. Two FPU-100 floating units with a combined capacity of 200 MW will supply electricity to the northern part of Halmahera Island in North Maluku.

Rosatom expects the units to begin supplying electricity to consumers within five years of the agreement. Floating nuclear power plants are designed for remote locations with limited grid infrastructure, offering a modular approach to nuclear power deployment.

The deal reflects growing interest in nuclear energy among Southeast Asian nations seeking to meet rising electricity demand while reducing carbon emissions. Indonesia joins other countries exploring nuclear as part of their energy transition strategy.
