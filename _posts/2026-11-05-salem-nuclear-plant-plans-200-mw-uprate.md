The Salem nuclear power plant near Hancocks Bridge, New Jersey, is planning a 200-megawatt uprate across its two units, according to executives from co-owners PSEG and Constellation during the utility's third-quarter earnings call. Of the total 200-MW increase, PSEG will receive roughly 112 MW through its 57 percent ownership stake; Constellation owns the remaining 43 percent.

The planned uprate was previously announced by Constellation in October as part of a broader package affecting 11 nuclear facilities across its portfolio. Salem's uprate capacity aligns with earlier 2026 estimates from PSEG executives, who identified the plant as having an uprate potential of nearly 200 MW.

Power uprates represent a cost-effective means of increasing output from existing reactors without new construction. The Salem upgrade is part of a broader industry trend toward maximizing productivity from the current operating fleet to meet rising electricity demand.
