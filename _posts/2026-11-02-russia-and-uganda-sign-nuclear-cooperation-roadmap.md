Russia and Uganda signed a roadmap for cooperation in peaceful nuclear energy uses at the Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow, establishing a framework to advance planning for Uganda's first nuclear power plant at the Nakasongola site. The agreement calls for creation of a dedicated working group to evaluate Uganda's national nuclear infrastructure and identify joint steps toward negotiating a contract for engineering surveys to select the plant's location. Rosatom Director General Alexey Likhachev stated the intention to work with Ugandan partners to assess national capabilities and prepare for site selection. The roadmap reflects growing interest among African nations in nuclear power as part of their energy portfolios, supported by vendor interest from established nuclear suppliers.
