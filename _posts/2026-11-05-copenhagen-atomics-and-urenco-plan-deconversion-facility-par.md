Copenhagen Atomics, a developer of molten salt reactors, has signed a memorandum of understanding with Urenco to design and construct a uranium deconversion facility in Copenhagen. Under the agreement, Urenco will assist in designing a dedicated plant to convert uranium hexafluoride (UF6) into uranium tetrafluoride (UF4).

Copenhagen Atomics plans to source enriched UF6, convert it into UF4, and synthesize high-purity fuel salts for use in its own reactors. The facility will also supply molten salt fuel to other commercial MSR developers and research institutions, positioning Copenhagen as a potential hub for fuel salt production.

Deconversion—the conversion of depleted uranium hexafluoride into more stable chemical forms—has typically been handled at large centralized facilities. The partnership suggests growing interest in distributed fuel production to support the emerging molten salt reactor sector and reflects efforts to streamline the fuel supply chain for advanced reactor designs.
