The State of Indiana, Hoosier Energy Rural Electric Cooperative, and Montreal-based engineering company AtkinsRéalis Group have signed a memorandum of understanding to explore a potential nuclear power project featuring up to eight CANDU reactor units with a combined 6 GW capacity.

The initiative will evaluate various deployment scenarios for CANDU technology in Indiana, including the feasibility of a gas-to-nuclear construction model designed to accelerate development. This approach could involve utilizing retired or retiring fossil fuel generation sites, which may streamline permitting and infrastructure development.

The project timeline depends on site selection and regulatory processes. If realized, the facility would represent a significant addition to nuclear capacity in the Midwest and underscore growing interest among regional utilities in large-scale new nuclear deployment. CANDU's proven track record and design are attractive to utilities seeking reliable, large-capacity options for meeting rising electricity demand.
