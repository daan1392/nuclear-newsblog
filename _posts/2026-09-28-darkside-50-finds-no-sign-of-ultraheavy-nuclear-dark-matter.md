The DarkSide collaboration has reported results from a seven-year experiment that found no direct evidence for "ultraheavy nuclear" dark matter.

The search tested whether dark matter particles might be composites of smaller, elementary particles rather than single fundamental ones. The data came from the DarkSide-50 detector.

A null result still carries weight. The researchers said it could lay the groundwork for future experiments that help establish whether dark matter has an internal structure. The supplied material gives no further detail on the mass ranges or sensitivity covered.
