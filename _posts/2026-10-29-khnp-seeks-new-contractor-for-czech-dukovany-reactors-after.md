Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power (KHNP), the preferred bidder for constructing two APR1000 reactors at Dukovany in the Czech Republic, terminated contract negotiations with the Daewoo-Doosan consortium and announced plans to select a new construction partner. The Czech government selected KHNP in July 2024 and awarded the firm a contract worth approximately USD 19.4 billion in June 2025. Daewoo Engineering & Construction and Doosan Enerbility, designated as construction partners in 2018, participated in the bidding process but negotiations with the consortium subsequently failed. The setback delays the project timeline for Dukovany's expansion but the search for alternative contractors indicates KHNP's continued commitment to the Czech build program.
