Framatome has signed what it calls the first commercial contract to deliver a fuel reload with uranium-235 enrichment above the industry-standard 5% to an operating reactor in the United States.

Under the agreement, part of the company's Advanced Fuel Management (AFM) programme, Framatome will supply a series of higher-enriched reloads using AFM technology, with the first delivery in early 2028. The contract supports long-term use of AFM fuel at the reactor and includes options for further reloads. The company did not name the reactor.

Uranium-235 is the fissile isotope that sustains the chain reaction in most power reactors. Higher enrichment allows more energy to be extracted from each fuel assembly, which can support longer operating cycles and more efficient fuel use.

The contract is a commercial milestone for fuel above the 5% level that current commercial fleets use. Delivery is still about a year and a half away.
