The Nuclear Regulatory Commission released a final environmental impact statement for Global Laser Enrichment's (GLE) proposed Paducah Laser Enrichment Facility (PLEF) near Paducah, Kentucky, a milestone that clears the way for construction licensing. GLE and Australian technology developer Silex Systems—which owns 51 percent of GLE—indicated that the final EIS supports issuance of a construction license. GLE's application was completed in July 2025. The PLEF would employ laser-based enrichment technology to produce low-enriched uranium fuel for civilian reactors. The final EIS represents the culmination of the regulatory environmental review process and brings the project closer to physical construction. The facility represents US efforts to develop domestic enrichment capacity independent of Russian and European providers, supporting long-term fuel security for the American nuclear fleet.
