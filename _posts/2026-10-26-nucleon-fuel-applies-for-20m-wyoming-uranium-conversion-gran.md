Nucleon Fuel, a subsidiary of Canadian small modular reactor developer Nucleon Energy, has applied for a $20 million grant and an additional $10 million loan from Wyoming's Energy Dominance Fund to build a uranium conversion facility near Gillette. The fund, newly created by Wyoming, targets energy projects including nuclear ventures in the state.

The conversion facility would process uranium into forms suitable for fuel fabrication. For SMR deployments, which typically require frequent refueling schedules, a domestic conversion capacity helps reduce supply chain dependencies and supports fuel cycle competitiveness.

The application signals growing investment in fuel cycle infrastructure to support the anticipated deployment wave of small reactors. Wyoming's backing reflects state interest in maintaining economic activity in uranium-related sectors as the energy landscape shifts.
