Estonia is progressing toward its first nuclear power plant by focusing site selection efforts on three locations in Viru-Nigula municipality, approximately 120 kilometres east of Tallinn. The search eliminated three candidate sites in Lüganuse municipality due to security considerations.

The move represents a significant step forward for the Baltic nation's nuclear ambitions, as it concentrates resources on locations deemed more suitable from both technical and strategic perspectives. The narrowed search should accelerate the evaluation process for Estonia's planned entry into nuclear power generation.

Europe's smaller economies have increasingly looked to nuclear as part of their energy transition strategies, and Estonia's project reflects broader regional interest in deploying capacity outside traditional nuclear markets.
